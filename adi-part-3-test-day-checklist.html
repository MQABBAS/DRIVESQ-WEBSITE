Questions people ask

What do I need to bring to the ADI Part 3?

Your pupil, a suitable car and your trainee instructor licence. If you do not have a trainee licence, bring your UK driving licence or passport.

How long is the ADI Part 3 test?

About 45 minutes, with the pupil driving for about 40.

What happens if I bring the wrong things?

The test can be cancelled with no refund.

Is my car insurance relevant?

The DVSA report form includes a declaration that the test vehicle is fully covered by a valid insurance policy for the test, so check yours before the day.

When do I get my result?

You get your grade and the completed report by email at the end of the test.
