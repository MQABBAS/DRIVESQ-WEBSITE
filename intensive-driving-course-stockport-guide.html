Why Intensive Works

Stop forgetting between lessons — build momentum instead

The problem with a lesson a week is retention. Most pupils spend the first 15 minutes of each lesson relearning what they forgot since last time. A 30-hour course at one lesson a week takes seven months — with seven months of forgetting built in.

With an intensive course, lessons run in longer sessions spread across a few weeks. What you practise Monday you still remember Wednesday. Skills stack rather than reset. Progress is faster because retention is better.

DriveSQ's Stockport intensive courses are structured specifically around the roads on Stockport DVSA test routes — Buxton Road, Wellington Road South, Bramhall Lane, Dialstone Lane — so every session has test-specific purpose.

Questions

FAQ — Intensive driving course Stockport

How long does an intensive driving course take in Stockport?

DriveSQ's 20-hour intensive typically completes in 2–4 weeks with sessions of 2–3 hours every couple of days. The 40-hour complete usually takes 4–6 weeks. It depends on your availability — the more regularly you can commit to sessions, the faster the progress.

Do I need my theory test before starting an intensive in Stockport?

You need a valid theory pass certificate before you can book your DVSA practical test. You can start intensive lessons before having it — but you won't be able to sit the practical test at the end without it. We recommend having theory done (or very close to done) before your first intensive session.

Can I do my Stockport intensive course in automatic?

Yes — and many intensive pupils in Stockport choose automatic. Without clutch management taking up mental bandwidth, you can focus on observations and road awareness from the very first session. The price is exactly the same as manual for DriveSQ intensive courses.

Which test centre will I use after my Stockport intensive?

Almost all Stockport intensive pupils test at Stockport DVSA on Dialstone Lane, Offerton SK2. Your mock test and test-day warm-up are run on the actual routes from that centre — the A6 Buxton Road, Wellington Road South, Bramhall Lane and the Turncroft Lane roundabout return.

Is 20 hours enough to pass from scratch in Stockport?

It depends on your starting point. The national DVSA average is 47 hours from scratch. 20 hours can be enough if you've driven abroad, had prior lessons years ago, or driven off-road vehicles. For a complete beginner with no driving history at all, 40 hours is more realistic. Your instructor will give you an honest assessment after your first two sessions and won't pressure you into either package.
