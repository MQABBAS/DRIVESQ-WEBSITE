How soon can I rebook my test after a fail?

You can rebook immediately after your test, but DVSA waiting times in Greater Manchester are typically 6–10 weeks. Use that time for focused refresher work — book 4–8 sessions with DriveSQ before your next test date arrives.

Should I switch instructors after failing?

Not necessarily — but if you feel your previous instructor wasn't identifying the right issues or pushing you enough, a fresh perspective can make a significant difference. DriveSQ offers a free consultation to review your test report before you commit to lessons.

How many refresher hours do I need before retesting?

Depends entirely on your fault pattern. Minor fault clusters usually need 3–4 hours. Serious or dangerous faults, or recurring major faults, may need 8–12 hours of targeted work. Your DriveSQ instructor will give you an honest estimate after the assessment drive.

Will DriveSQ do a mock test before I rebook the real one?

Yes — and we recommend it strongly. A mock test on the actual Bredbury routes, delivered by your DriveSQ instructor in examiner style, is the most accurate predictor of real test readiness. Only book when the mock produces a genuine pass.
