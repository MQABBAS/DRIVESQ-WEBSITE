Sale Manual Lesson Routes

Washway Road A56 — Gear Selection on the Dual Carriageway

Washway Road A56 is a 40 mph dual carriageway between Stretford and Altrincham and the main spine road through Sale. In a manual car, confident gear selection through third and fourth gears is essential on this road — dropping to second at bus stops or pelican crossings and returning to fourth promptly to match traffic flow. The two Metrolink crossings at Brooklands tram stop and Sale tram stop both require a complete stop at the red signal, first-gear hold, then a smooth pull-away over the tram rails. We run this full A56 corridor in lessons multiple times before the test.

Brooklands & Sale Metrolink Tram Crossings

The two Metrolink crossings on Washway Road are a distinctive feature of Sale's driving test routes. Both use a full signal-controlled system — red, amber, white proceed light — and the track surface is slippery in wet weather. In a manual car, the key skill is selecting first gear before the crossing rather than trying to engage it while stationary on the tracks, and using gentle throttle to avoid wheelspin as the car crosses the raised rails. We dedicate at least two lesson sessions to these crossings in different traffic conditions before any test is booked.

School Road, Marsland Road & Manoeuvre Streets

School Road through Sale town centre is a one-way system with right-turn prohibition at several points — a detail that catches manual pupils who are focused on their gear selection and miss the road signs. Marsland Road and the streets around Ashton-on-Mersey provide the manoeuvre training zone: parallel parking beside the school boundary wall on Reddish Road, three-point turns on Cavendish Road off Hale Road, and bay parking in the Sale Water Park car park on Rifle Road where the surface is level and the bays are wide. All manoeuvres are practised in manual gear with the clutch as the speed controller.

Stretford DVSA Mock Test from Sale — Manual Circuit

The Stretford DVSA circuit for Sale manual pupils departs Lacy Street M32, travels south on Chester Road A56 through Stretford, joins Washway Road A56 at the Sale boundary, crosses the Brooklands Metrolink line and extends to the Altrincham Road roundabout before returning via Manchester Road through Urmston and Stretford Road. An alternative shorter circuit turns at Sale town centre via School Road. We run both versions as full mock tests — including the emergency stop on Chester Road — with gear change commentary so pupils understand what standard is expected at each point on the route.

FAQs — Manual Lessons in Sale

Which test centre do Sale manual pupils use?
Sale manual pupils test at Stretford DVSA on Lacy Street M32, approximately 10 minutes away via Washway Road A56 and Chester Road.
Are there Metrolink crossings on the Sale test route?
Yes, the Altrincham Metrolink line crosses Washway Road at both Brooklands and Sale tram stops. Manual pupils must make a smooth first-gear pull-away over the tram rails after the proceed signal.
How many manual lessons do I need in Sale?
Most Sale pupils take 35–50 hours of manual lessons. The Washway Road dual carriageway, Metrolink crossings and Ashton-on-Mersey manoeuvre streets are all covered across the course.
Is Washway Road A56 difficult in a manual car?
Washway Road carries 40 mph traffic and two Metrolink crossings. In a manual car, correct gear selection for the dual-carriageway speed and smooth clutch control at tram crossings are the main skills we focus on.
Do you cover Brooklands and Ashton-on-Mersey?
Yes, Brooklands, Ashton-on-Mersey and the Stretford boundary are all within our Sale manual lesson coverage.
Can I start manual lessons with no experience in Sale?
Yes, we teach complete beginners from the very first gear change right through to test standard. No previous driving experience is needed.
