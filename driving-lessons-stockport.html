Stockport Test Centre — What to Expect

Stockport Driving Test Centre is on Millgate, SK1 1JN — right in the town centre, a two-minute walk from the bus station. Tests depart along Wellington Road North and South, through Heaton Chapel, out towards Reddish Vale, and back through the residential roads of Edgeley and Offerton. Some test routes also use the A6 towards Hazel Grove before looping back through Great Moor.

The most common faults at Stockport test centre involve the one-way system near the bus station, the pedestrian crossing on Wellington Road South, and the filter lane at the junction of the A6 and Bramhall Lane South. DriveSQ covers all of these in mock test sessions before your real test date.

Local Road Knowledge

Stockport Roads Your Instructor Knows Inside Out

DriveSQ instructors are trained specifically on Stockport's unique challenges

Wellington Road South (A6) Hill

The steep descent from Heaviley into Heaton Chapel is one of the steepest A-road hills in Stockport. It appears on many test routes. DriveSQ lessons cover hill starts, controlled descents, and the give-way at the bottom where the A6 crosses Didsbury Road.

Mersey Square & One-Way System

The town centre one-way system through Mersey Square, along Prince's Street, and under the viaduct catches many learners out. We practise lane discipline here specifically in the lessons before your mock test.

A6 Bramhall Lane South Junction

The filter at the A6 / Bramhall Lane South junction in Hazel Grove direction involves a right-turn filter that is easy to miss. It appears on test routes that head south from the test centre. Our mock tests cover it in full.

Reddish Vale & Gorton Road

Test routes through Reddish involve narrow residential streets off Gorton Road and the crossing at Reddish Vale Road. Speed limits change frequently in this area — from 30 to 20 outside schools. DriveSQ covers every change point.

Dialstone Lane, Offerton

Offerton is a common test route area. Dialstone Lane has a tricky junction with Lisburne Lane and several pelican crossings. The roads through Offerton estate are often where parallel parking and bay parking manoeuvres are set.

Edgeley: Castle Street & Belmont Street

Edgeley's narrow residential grid near Stockport County's Edgeley Park is used for both manoeuvre practice and test routes. Parked cars, tight corners, and frequent pedestrian crossings make it excellent training ground for new learners.
