Mersey Square Gyratory: The Lane-Choice Problem No Competitor Teaches

Mersey Square is a multi-exit gyratory in Stockport town centre where the approach from Wellington Road South converges with the A6 Stockport viaduct slip road. The result is a lane merge that disorients learners: the correct lane for each exit is not signposted in a way that is intuitive to someone who has never driven it, and the approach speed means decisions must be made early.

This junction is a known fault generator on the Stockport DVSA practical test. Examiners expect learners to position correctly before the merge point and to signal and adjust without hesitation. DriveSQ instructors dedicate specific lesson time to walking through every exit option at Mersey Square, so you arrive at your test date having made the correct lane choice dozens of times — not for the first time in front of an examiner.
