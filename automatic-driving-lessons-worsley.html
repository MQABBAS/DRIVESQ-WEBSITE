Worsley Roads We Train On

Worsley Road & the Bridgewater Canal — Village Approach

Worsley village sits beside the Bridgewater Canal and its road network reflects its historic layout — Worsley Road narrows at the canal bridge approach and priority is not always obvious to newcomers. Lessons here cover the Barton Road hump-back bridge where a single-track section at the peak forces give-way decisions, the right turn into Worsley Green from Worsley Road where the tight sight line requires slow creeping to see oncoming traffic from the Eccles direction, and the speed limit change from 40 mph on Worsley Road to 30 mph at the village boundary near the Packet House pub. These village transitions appear on some Salford DVSA independent driving circuits routed through Worsley.

East Lancashire Road A580 — Worsley Brow Interchange

The Worsley Brow area is where the A572 Leigh Road, the A575 Boothstown Road and the A580 East Lancashire Road all converge through a series of large signal-controlled roundabouts and slip roads. For learner drivers this is one of the most complex junctions in Greater Manchester. DriveSQ introduces the interchange progressively: first approaching the A572 / A575 roundabout from Boothstown where speeds are lower, then tackling the A580 on-slip from Worsley Road, and finally practising the full junction in both directions. Lane discipline on the A580 between the Worsley Brow and Bridgewater Way exits is covered in detail as the lane markings change rapidly over a short distance.

Boothstown Road & Ellenbrook — Manoeuvre Practice

The residential streets of Boothstown and Ellenbrook provide ideal manoeuvre training conditions away from the busy A-road network. Leigh Road through Boothstown has wide kerbside areas opposite the Boothstown Methodist Church suitable for parallel parking, and the cul-de-sacs off Greenleach Lane in Ellenbrook are used for bay parking practice in low-traffic conditions. Three-point turns are trained on Newearth Road where road width is 7.5 metres — the standard test reference — and on Leigh Road approaching the Boothstown / Worsley boundary. These quiet streets build pupil confidence in manoeuvres before they are introduced in busier locations.

Salford DVSA Mock Test Circuits via Worsley

Worsley pupils typically test at Salford DVSA on Trafford Road M5, approximately 20 minutes south via the A57. The Worsley circuit departs Trafford Road southbound, joins Eccles New Road, continues through Patricroft and Barton Road to the Bridgewater Canal bridge, turns through Worsley village and Worsley Green, then heads south via Roe Green to the East Lancashire Road, crosses the A580 and returns via Boothstown Road and the A572 back towards Eccles. We run the full circuit as a timed mock test — including a sat-nav independent driving section through Worsley village — so every junction is familiar before test day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which DVSA test centre do Worsley pupils use?
Worsley pupils typically test at Salford DVSA on Trafford Road M5, approximately 20 minutes south via the A57 or the A580 Eccles exit.
Is Worsley Brow difficult for learner drivers?
Worsley Brow involves converging roundabouts where the A572, A575 and A580 meet. We build up to it across multiple sessions — quieter approach roads first, then the full interchange.
How many hours will I need for automatic lessons in Worsley?
Most Worsley pupils take 25–40 hours in an automatic car. Village roads, the A580 sliproads and the Barton Road canal bridge are all built into the course progressively.
Do you cover Boothstown and Ellenbrook?
Yes, Boothstown, Ellenbrook, Walkden and Little Hulton are all covered from our Worsley base.
Is the Barton Road canal bridge on the test route?
The hump-back bridge on Barton Road over the Bridgewater Canal features on Salford DVSA circuits. The single-track peak requires precise positioning and give-way judgement.
Can I book one automatic lesson in Worsley first?
Yes, a single 1-hour lesson is £33 with no obligation. Most pupils try one lesson before committing to a 10-hour block or intensive course.
