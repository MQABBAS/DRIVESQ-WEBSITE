FAQs — Theory Test Preparation in M11

What is on the DVSA theory test?

The DVSA theory test has two parts: a multiple-choice section (50 questions, pass mark 43/50) and a hazard perception section (14 video clips, pass mark 44/75). You must pass both parts in the same sitting to get your theory test certificate.

How long is the theory test valid?

Your theory test certificate is valid for 2 years from the date you pass. If you don't pass your practical test within those 2 years, you must retake the theory test before booking another practical test attempt.

What is the best way to revise for the theory test?

Use the official DVSA learning materials: the Highway Code, Know Your Traffic Signs, and the official DVSA theory test app. Study in short sessions of 15–20 minutes daily, focus on questions you keep getting wrong, and practise hazard perception clips until you're consistently scoring above 60/75.

Does DriveSQ help with theory test preparation in M11?

Yes. DriveSQ instructors in M11 link theory to practical driving throughout your lessons — explaining why rules exist as you encounter them on the road. This real-world context makes theory revision much easier and faster than studying in isolation.
