Test Route Knowledge

Stretford Test Routes & Key Hazards

Your test starts from Stretford Driving Test Centre, 49 Kings Road, Stretford, M32 8GN. Here's what our instructors know — and what they'll prepare you for.

🛣️ Chester Road (A56) — The Main Challenge

The A56 is the backbone of the Stretford test. It runs through the heart of M32 and features dedicated bus lanes (watch for the markings), sharp 30/40mph transitions near Old Trafford, and a cluster of complex pedestrian crossings outside the stadium on match days and beyond. Examiners frequently route learners along Chester Road — we train you on it until it feels natural.

🏟️ Old Trafford & Talbot Road

Talbot Road runs alongside Old Trafford (home of Manchester United and Lancashire Cricket) and is a regular test road. Expect road markings, parked vehicles, and pedestrians crossing unpredictably. Our instructors know every parking restriction and sight line on this stretch.

🌳 Longford Road & Longford Park

Longford Road borders Longford Park and is used for roundabout practice and independent driving segments. Speed changes are common and there are multiple give-way junctions our instructors will prepare you for.

🔀 Barton Road & Davyhulme Road

These roads connect Stretford to Urmston and Eccles and involve dual carriageway sections, lane discipline, and higher-speed manoeuvring. Often used for the independent driving portion of the test.

🚦 Edge Lane & Seymour Grove

Edge Lane and Seymour Grove are residential test roads that include 20mph zones, traffic lights, and parking situations. Great Stone Road is also used for bay parking and turn-in-the-road manoeuvres.

🚋 Metrolink Crossings

The Metrolink tram line crosses several Stretford test roads. Our instructors ensure you're fully prepared for tram crossing etiquette — a common area where learners lose marks if they haven't specifically practised it.
