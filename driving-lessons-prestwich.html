Unique Local Hazard

The Junction No Competitor Teaches

⚠ Gradient Blind Spot

Bury New Road A56 / Scholes Lane — Hidden Dip Junction

The A56 Bury New Road drops steeply into a pronounced dip just south of the Scholes Lane junction before rising again. This gradient change creates a hidden dip blind spot that is unique to this stretch of road and unknown to most learners — and unaddressed by any other driving school page covering Prestwich.

Vehicles emerging from Scholes Lane onto the A56 cannot see approaching northbound traffic until they are already committed to the manoeuvre. The dip conceals fast-moving vehicles until separation is dangerously short. Equally, northbound A56 drivers cannot see the Scholes Lane junction until almost upon it, making vehicles that have misjudged the emerge invisible until the last moment.

This gradient-blind junction has been the site of many near-misses and requires a specific technique: a full stop at the give-way line, both directions fully checked through the dip, and a clear gap confirmed before committing. It is not enough to check left and right — you must wait until approaching traffic from the dip becomes visible. DriveSQ teaches this junction explicitly in every Prestwich lesson plan.
