Hazel Grove’s Most Challenging Junction

The A6 / London Road Staggered Crossroads at Hazel Grove

Where the A6 from Stockport meets the A523 Macclesfield Road in a staggered arrangement, learners face a right-turn that crosses a live lane of oncoming traffic. The bend on London Road compresses sight lines precisely where you need them most, and the volume of HGVs routing south via the A6 means large vehicles close the gap faster than most new drivers anticipate.

What we do about it: We approach this junction progressively — first observing it as a passenger, then turning left, then right under low-traffic conditions, then under full traffic. By your test date you will have turned right at this junction dozens of times. No competitor driving school page addresses this junction specifically. We do, because it matters.
