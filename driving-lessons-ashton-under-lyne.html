Know the Roads

Roads You'll Drive on Your Ashton Test

We train you on every significant road in the Ashton-under-Lyne OL6 test area — so nothing surprises you on test day.

Test Route

Old Street A635

Main arterial through the town centre. Test candidates deal with phased signals, bus stops, and pedestrian crossings in quick succession. Signal timing and lane positioning are key assessment points here.

Give-Way

Mossley Road

Approaches the critical three-way junction with Katherine Street. Instructors ensure learners read the faded give-way markings confidently and manage the tight bend with limited forward visibility.

Priority

Katherine Street

Vehicles from Katherine Street must yield at the Mossley Road junction — but the line is badly worn and the sight line around the bend is restricted. Heavy bus traffic makes this a consistent source of minor faults on Ashton tests.

Town Centre

Stamford Street Central

Busy pedestrianised-edge zone with market-day footfall, delivery vehicles, and cyclists sharing the space. Observation, speed management, and hazard perception are tested extensively on this road.

Residential

Cavendish Street

Tight residential street with parked cars on both sides reducing it to single-lane width in places. Learners practice passing places, appropriate speed, and anticipating door-opening hazards.

Dual Approach

Manchester Road A635

Dual carriageway approach from the west. Lane discipline, road markings, and signage are the focus. Candidates also practise safe junction entry onto this road from side streets during lessons.

Motorway Prep

M60/M67 Junction 23 Approach

Motorway preparation starts on the approach to J23. We cover slip-road joining, lane discipline at 70mph, and safe exit — essential preparation for independent post-test driving in Greater Manchester.

School Zone

Henrietta Street

School-zone road with crossing patrols, speed humps, and significant pedestrian activity at drop-off and pick-up times. Timing awareness and hazard anticipation are drilled on this street.

⚠️ Local Hazard: Ashton Old Road / Mossley Road / Katherine Street Junction

This three-way give-way near Ashton town centre is one of the most fault-generating junctions on the test. Vehicles approaching from Katherine Street must give way — but the road markings have faded to the point where the line is barely visible, and the sight line around the bend is limited. Combined with frequent bus traffic stopping on Mossley Road, many Ashton test candidates accumulate minor faults here simply because they hesitate or misread the priority order. DriveSQ instructors practise this junction in every lesson plan for OL6 learners — so you arrive knowing exactly what to look for and how to manage it confidently.

Ashton-under-Lyne Examiner Routes

The Ashton-under-Lyne DVSA test centre sits on Wellington Road. Tests leave the centre and typically follow one of three core circuits. We practise all three.

Route A Wellington Road → Town Centre Circuit

Departs Wellington Road, turns onto Old Street A635 through the signal sequence, continues onto Stamford Street Central through the pedestrian zone fringe, then completes a residential circuit before returning. This route tests town-centre observation, signal compliance, and pedestrian hazard management.

Route B Manchester Road A635 → Mossley → B5336 Return

Heads west on Manchester Road A635 — assessing dual-carriageway lane discipline and junction entry — then turns toward Mossley and joins the rural B5336 before returning via Stamford Street. This route tests speed management, road positioning, and rural-to-urban transition.

Route C Cavendish Street → Hyde Road A57 → Godley Return

Takes the narrower residential Cavendish Street, joins the busier Hyde Road A57, and extends into Godley before the return leg. Tests parked-car passing, priority management on narrow roads, and A-road positioning. School zones and crossing patrols are frequently encountered on this route.
