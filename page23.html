Dukinfield Local Area Guide

Lessons around Dukinfield use real local roads including Concord Way, King Street and Astley Street, so by the time you're ready for your test you've already driven the streets you'll use every day after passing. Dukinfield Old Chapel, dating to around 1706, is among the oldest surviving Nonconformist places of worship in the north-west of England — a quiet survivor of the religious ferment that followed the Civil War, still standing in the centre of a town more famous for its cotton mills.

We also plan around school-run traffic near St John's CE Primary School and Greenfield Primary School, using quieter spots like Dukinfield Old Chapel (Grade II listed, one of the oldest surviving Nonconformist chapels in the north-west) for early manoeuvre practice before stepping up to busier sections of Concord Way.

Test centre: most learners around Dukinfield test at Bredbury (Manchester) Driving Test Centre, Lingard Lane, Bredbury, Stockport, SK6 2QT; mock tests are planned around the routes examiners actually use from there.
