Local Road Knowledge

Roads We Teach in Reddish SK5

Every lesson uses real Reddish roads. You'll learn the exact junctions and hazards that appear on your test route from Stockport DVSA — not generic city driving exercises.

Gorton Road – Speed Awareness and Crossroads Control

Gorton Road runs south-west from Reddish town centre toward Levenshulme and carries a mix of local traffic, buses, and delivery vehicles throughout the day. The posted limit is 30 mph, but speed awareness matters especially near the junctions with Longford Road West and Reddish Road where sightlines are reduced by parked vehicles. Learners practise reading the road ahead, judging safe gaps at side junctions, and responding to pedestrians stepping between parked cars — a core skill tested by examiners from Stockport DVSA. The road also contains a zebra crossing near the community centre that requires early observation and smooth, unhurried braking.

Manchester Road A6102 – Dual Carriageway Discipline and Lane Changing

The A6102 Manchester Road bisects Reddish as a dual carriageway with a 40 mph limit and multiple filter lanes at its major junctions. Learners spend time here building motorway-adjacent skills: matching speed to traffic flow before joining, selecting the correct lane well in advance of exits, and maintaining safe following distance at higher speeds. The junction at Reddish Road / Travis Brow is particularly demanding — a multi-arm signal-controlled crossroads with a right-turn phase that requires precise position in the lane and calm observation of oncoming vehicles. We also cover the approach to the Houldsworth roundabout at the north end of the road, where lane discipline feeds directly into the test route from Stockport DVSA.

Longford Road West – Residential Precision and Bay Parking

Longford Road West runs through a quiet residential grid between Reddish and Heaton Chapel, offering ideal conditions for practising manoeuvres at low speed with real-world hazards. The road is narrow in sections and parking on both sides creates natural pinch points where priority is unclear — exactly the kind of situation examiners set up. Bay parking exercises use the Reddish Vale car park approach, where an open, flat surface allows the skill to be repeated until it becomes second nature. We also include parallel parking and three-point turns on the tighter stretches near Reddish Vale Road, building the accuracy and observation needed for the independent driving section of the test.

Reddish Road and Houldsworth Square – Town Centre and Roundabout

Reddish Road through Houldsworth Square forms the commercial heart of the area and is one of the busiest stretches in SK5 during school drop-off, market days and early evening. Traffic builds quickly around the crossing near the Co-op, and pedestrians treat the road informally in both directions. Houldsworth Square itself is a compact one-lane roundabout that requires giving way to the right while simultaneously watching for cyclists filtering on the inside. The combination of pedestrian activity, informal crossing behaviour and the roundabout junction makes this a high-value section for learners preparing for test conditions. We include approaches from both Gorton Road and Sandy Lane to ensure all entry angles are covered.

Test Centre Info

Stockport DVSA Test Centre

Reddish learners sit their practical test at Stockport DVSA Test Centre, Dialstone Lane SK2 6AP, approximately 10 minutes from Reddish town centre via the A6102. The test routes from Stockport typically include sections on Manchester Road, Gorton Road, and the Houldsworth roundabout — all roads we cover in your lessons.

We offer a pre-test lesson starting from your home in Reddish, driving to the test centre and warming up on the routes examiners use most. This last-minute preparation is included as part of your lesson time, not charged separately.

Stockport Test Centre Guide

What to expect on test day

  • Independent driving section — typically 20 minutes following sat-nav or road signs
  • One or two reversing manoeuvres chosen by the examiner
  • Show-me / tell-me vehicle safety questions
  • Eyesight check at the start of the test
  • Approximately 40 minutes of driving in and around Stockport
