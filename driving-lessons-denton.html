Local Hazard Insight

The Denton A57 Problem — Why It Catches Everyone Out

⚠ Denton Roundabout: The A57/A57 Dual-Destination Trap
Manchester Road and Hyde Road both carry the A57 number — and they diverge right here

Most roundabouts in Greater Manchester are straightforward to navigate once you know which road number to follow. Denton roundabout is different — and uniquely confusing — because both main exits carry the same road number: A57.

Manchester Road heading west toward Gorton is the A57. Hyde Road heading east toward Hyde is also the A57. The approach signs reference both A57 destinations at the same time, which means following "A57" on a satnav or road sign gives you no useful information about which exit to take. Even experienced drivers who have never driven in Denton before — and this includes some examiners testing learners from outside the area — have been observed taking the wrong exit.

The roundabout itself is large and multi-lane. On approach from the north and south, the lane markings do not clearly resolve which lane serves which A57 direction until you are already committed to a lane. At peak times, following traffic creates pressure to move quickly before the signage can be properly read.

No other driving school we are aware of addresses this specific A57 dual-destination issue in published tuition materials. DriveSQ covers it explicitly: we walk you through the correct lane selection for both A57 directions, explain how to read the secondary destination names on approach boards (Gorton, Audenshaw, Hyde, Romiley) rather than relying on the road number alone, and practise the roundabout in multiple directions until your exit choice is instinctive.

DriveSQ Tip: Read secondary destination names (Gorton / Audenshaw / Hyde / Romiley) — not the A57 number — to identify the correct exit at Denton roundabout.
