Road-by-Road Breakdown

The Roads You'll Drive in Farnworth

Market Street & King Street — Town-Centre Junction Flow

Market Street runs through the heart of Farnworth with a series of signal-controlled junctions, pelican crossings near the market hall, and the Farnworth bus interchange on King Street where buses make frequent unannounced stops. The signal sequence at the Market Street / Brackley Street junction runs on a short cycle during peak hours, catching learners who approach too fast. The bus interchange itself introduces learners to commercial-vehicle hazards — buses pulling out from bays, passengers crossing between stopped buses, and delivery vehicles blocking the nearside lane. We build this as a progressive exercise: first at quiet times to understand the junction layout, then at peak times to manage the real traffic flow.

Moses Gate Roundabout — Complex Multi-Arm Approach

Moses Gate is a five-arm roundabout on the A6053 carrying traffic between Bolton, Farnworth, Kearsley, and the Irwell Valley. The approach from Farnworth via Bolton Road requires learners to select the correct lane at least 100m before the give-way line — the left lane exits only to Kearsley, while the right lane covers the three remaining arms. Traffic from Bolton approaches at speed and does not always signal early. We practise approach lane selection, roundabout observation, and exit signalling at Moses Gate in multiple directions until the learner can navigate any arm without prompting. The Bolton DVSA test frequently uses Moses Gate on its longer test routes, making early familiarity here a significant test advantage.

Manchester Road A575 — Arterial Speed Transitions

Manchester Road connects Farnworth with Walkden and Worsley to the south. The speed limit transitions from 30mph through Farnworth to 40mph past the Farnworth fire station and then 50mph as it approaches the Roe Green junction. We use this corridor to practise progressive acceleration matching the new limit, early speed-limit sign recognition, and the discipline to reduce speed before the 30mph zone resumes approaching Walkden town centre. The junction with Newbrook Road — a right turn across a 40mph road — requires clear gap selection and a confident acceleration away. The road's gradient also rises slightly south of Farnworth, giving learners exposure to maintaining speed on an uphill carriageway in higher gears.

Harper Green & Prestolee — Beginner Foundation Estates

Harper Green Road and the surrounding residential streets — Prestolee Road, Radcliffe Road B6196 (the quieter section south of Prestolee), and Stoneclough Road — give all DriveSQ Farnworth beginners a gentle, low-traffic start. The roads are wide, straight, and lightly used, allowing full focus on clutch biting point, smooth first-gear movement, and basic left-hand T-junctions. The Farnworth Park car park adjacent to Farnworth & Kearsley Golf Club provides a flat, open space for bay parking and reverse manoeuvres. Prestolee Road crosses the River Irwell via a narrow brick bridge — a useful single-width passing exercise for more advanced learners within the estate section.
