Local Road Knowledge

Roads We Teach in Gatley SK8

Gatley's road network provides everything a learner needs — from quiet residential streets to busy junctions — all within a few minutes of your front door.

Gatley Road – Shopping Parade, Pedestrians and Crossings

Gatley Road is the village's commercial spine, running from the junction with Church Road through the shopping parade and on to Northenden Road to the north. The road is 30 mph with high pedestrian activity — shoppers crossing informally between parked cars, delivery vehicles double-parked alongside the row of independent shops, and buses pulling in and out of stops. Learners practise reading hazards ahead: a delivery van with hazards on means a full obstruction check before passing; a bus indicating means anticipating passengers crossing the road from the front of the vehicle. The mini-roundabout at the northern end of Gatley Road where it meets Northenden Road requires disciplined give-way to the right and smooth lane positioning — it appears on Stockport DVSA test routes from the Northenden direction.

Church Road – Priority, Narrowing and Approach to Gatley Station

Church Road connects Gatley village to the railway station and the Cheadle border, threading through a residential section that narrows noticeably near the railway bridge at the southern end. The bridge creates a genuine single-file pinch point where the rule of priority — the driver with the obstruction on their side gives way — must be understood and applied consistently. Station Road adjacent to Gatley station generates a regular surge of parked and moving vehicles at commuter times; learners observe how queuing affects priority decisions and practise holding position patiently rather than forcing through. The approach to the B5166 Cheadle Road junction at the southern end of Church Road requires a clear left-right-left check at a T-junction with no road markings — a scenario that appears frequently in independent driving sections.

Longley Lane – Speed Limit Changes and Boundary Roads

Longley Lane forms the western boundary of Gatley and connects to Northenden and the M60 junction area. The lane itself is 30 mph and narrows beside allotments and hedgerows, creating sections where two vehicles cannot pass simultaneously. Speed awareness here is about more than reading the signs — learners must judge the road width at each oncoming vehicle and decide early whether to hold position or give way. The junction where Longley Lane meets the Styal Road B5166 introduces a dual-carriageway emerge with a 40 mph flow — a significant step up in traffic speed. We build up to this junction over several lessons, starting with observation and ending with smooth, committed emergence into a gap that is genuinely safe rather than merely possible.

Cheadle Road B5166 – Roundabouts, Retail and 40mph Discipline

The B5166 Cheadle Road connects Gatley to Cheadle village and carries a mix of local, retail and through traffic at 30–40 mph. The Gatley Crossroads signal junction on Cheadle Road is a multi-arm controlled junction with a prominent keep-clear box — learners practise stopping short of the box when traffic is queuing ahead and moving only when there is a clear space to exit. The approach to Cheadle village from Gatley Road passes the Asda turning and the pedestrian crossing near the Royal Oak public house, where pedestrians use the crossing at irregular times throughout the day. We include this section as a hazard perception exercise and as preparation for the independent driving segment, which frequently routes through Cheadle village from the Stockport DVSA test centre.

Test Centre Info

Stockport DVSA Test Centre

Gatley learners sit their practical test at Stockport DVSA Test Centre, Dialstone Lane SK2 6AP, approximately 15 minutes from Gatley village. Test routes from Stockport frequently route through Cheadle, Gatley and the Cheadle Road B5166 — areas covered in depth during your lessons.

We cover all the principal test route roads during your lessons and offer a pre-test warm-up lesson from your door to the test centre, included within your lesson hour.

Stockport Test Centre Guide

What to expect on test day

  • Eyesight check at the test centre
  • Show-me / tell-me vehicle safety questions
  • Around 40 minutes of driving including an independent section
  • Independent section — usually 20 minutes following sat-nav
  • One or two manoeuvres chosen by the examiner
