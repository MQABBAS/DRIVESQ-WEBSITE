⚠️ Oldham's Most Misunderstood Hazard — The Town Centre Loop Exit

The Oldham town centre one-way loop — Union Street → Yorkshire Street → King Street — contains a yield situation that no competitor page covers, and that most learners encounter unprepared on test day. The exit back onto Manchester Street is approached through a tight left-hander at the end of Yorkshire Street. Immediately on that exit sits a bus stop. Buses departing the stop pull out directly into the path of a turning vehicle, creating a real-world yield that is not signposted and not marked with give-way lines. Examiners know this junction. DriveSQ practises it repeatedly with every Oldham learner until the observation and yield decision are automatic.
