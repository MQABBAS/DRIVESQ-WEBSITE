Know the Routes

Chadderton Test Centre — Oldham Test Routes

Chadderton Driving Test Centre, 9 Broadgate, Broadgate Business Park, Chadderton, OL9 9XA. Our instructors prepare every Oldham learner for the real routes used by DVSA examiners.

🔵 Oldham Way Inner Ring Road — Lane Discipline & Slip Roads

The Oldham Way ring road is one of the most commonly used sections on Chadderton test routes and one of the most commonly failed. Examiners direct candidates onto this fast dual carriageway and immediately test lane discipline and correct use of exit slip roads. Many learners who haven't had locally-expert tuition hesitate on the exits or position incorrectly. DriveSQ trains every Oldham learner on Oldham Way until the lane choices are automatic.

🟠 Mumps Bridge & Metrolink Oldham Tram Line

Mumps Bridge is a key junction on test routes, and the Metrolink tram extension to Oldham Town Centre runs alongside several test-route roads in this area. Learners must be aware of tram tracks (which affect steering), tram stops with pedestrians crossing, and the priority rules at tram signals. Our instructors train candidates specifically on the Mumps area to ensure confident, safe navigation on test day.

🟢 Manchester Road A62 — Dual Carriageway Driving

The Manchester Road (A62) dual carriageway forms part of several test routes from Chadderton. Examiners test safe joining from slip roads, appropriate speed, lane changing, and hazard awareness on this busy arterial road. DriveSQ ensures all Oldham learners have covered the A62 in multiple lessons before test day — including during peak hours — so they're prepared for real conditions.

🔴 Boundary Park Area — Sheepfoot Lane

Sheepfoot Lane, leading to Oldham Athletic FC's Boundary Park ground, is a well-known landmark on certain test routes. The road involves junctions, parked vehicles on matchdays (or surrounding roads), and residential hazards. Our instructors flag this route and give learners specific practice around the Boundary Park area — turning a potentially stressful landmark into a familiar checkpoint.

🟣 Chadderton Business Park — Unusual Road Priority

The test begins and ends at Chadderton Driving Test Centre on Broadgate Business Park, OL9 9XA. Business park roads have unusual lane markings and priority rules that differ from typical residential streets — learners unfamiliar with the area often show hesitation right at the start or end of their test. DriveSQ instructors practice the test centre approach and departure as a standard part of every Oldham mock test session.
