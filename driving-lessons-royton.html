⚠️ Royton-specific hazard: Grains Road gradient approaching Shaw

The uphill gradient on Grains Road catches learners who don't anticipate the hill start demand at the give-way junction near the top. Vehicles stopping at the top of the hill must hold on the handbrake while assessing the gap in fast-moving traffic — a situation that causes clutch roll-back under test conditions. DriveSQ instructors practise this junction repeatedly until the hill-start sequence is reliable.
