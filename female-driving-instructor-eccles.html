Roads You'll Learn on in Eccles

Liverpool Road (A57)

The A57 is the main arterial route through Eccles, running from Salford through the town centre and on towards Patricroft and Irlam. Traffic volumes are high during peak hours and the road passes several school entrances, requiring learners to anticipate pedestrian activity even outside of formal crossing points. The box junction at the A57 / Barton Lane crossroads catches many learners — your instructor will explain the rules for entering a yellow box before you encounter one on test.

High traffic volumeBox junctionSchool entrances

Church Street & Eccles Town Centre

Church Street is the pedestrianised heart of Eccles, and the roads immediately around it — Regent Street, Wellington Road and Station Road — require careful navigation of the town centre one-way system. The junction at Regent Street and the A57 is a crossroads where the priority is not always obvious from signage alone: your instructor will walk you through the correct approach from all four directions before your test.

One-way systemPriority junctionsTown centre navigation

Patricroft Road

Patricroft Road runs south from Eccles through to the Bridgewater Canal crossing, where a low-clearance bridge and narrow road surface require precise positioning and early signalling. The road transitions from a 30 mph residential section to a quieter 40 mph stretch past the canal — learners practise speed limit recognition and appropriate acceleration on this section before tackling the A57 again.

Low clearance bridgeSpeed limit changeCanal crossing

Monton Road

Monton Road provides a pleasant contrast to the busy A57 — a residential road through one of Eccles' more affluent areas, with speed humps, parked vehicles on both sides and a village-centre section around Monton Green. It's an excellent route for practising controlled observation at junctions, dealing with parked vehicles in both directions and managing the pressure of slower-moving traffic behind you.

Speed humpsVillage centreObservation junctions

Your Test Centre: Salford DVSA

Eccles learners sit their practical test at Salford DVSA on Silk Street, Salford M3 — approximately 10 minutes from Eccles town centre via the A57. Test routes include the Salford Crescent area, Manchester Road A57, and residential routes around Weaste and Pendleton. Your instructor will cover all of these thoroughly in the weeks before your test.
