Roads You'll Learn on in Ashton

Stamford Street

Stamford Street is one of Ashton's busiest through-roads, running from the Snipe retail park area through the town centre and on towards Stalybridge. The road passes Ashton bus station and the Metrolink terminus at the southern end, where trams cross the carriageway at grade — learners must identify the tram-crossing give-way lines and treat them correctly, which differs from a standard give-way in how priority is granted. Your instructor will cover this from your very first lesson using the road.

Tram-crossing give-wayBus stationTown centre

Katherine Street

Katherine Street runs parallel to Stamford Street and forms part of the inner ring road around Ashton town centre. The junction with Old Street is a multi-arm crossroads with an unusual phasing — pedestrian crossing phases run simultaneously with some vehicle phases, which surprises learners who aren't watching for the pedestrian signals as well as the vehicle ones. Getting this junction right is important because it regularly features in test routes from Ashton DVSA.

Multi-arm crossroadsPedestrian phasingTest route

Old Street

Old Street runs east from the town centre towards Mossley Road and the DVSA test centre. The road narrows past the former market site and the parked vehicle density is high during market days. Independent driving sections of the test often use Old Street — your instructor will teach you how to navigate it using the road signs alone, building the skill before your test date so you don't freeze under examiner observation.

Independent drivingNarrow sectionTest approach

Mossley Road & Dukinfield

Mossley Road heads north from Ashton towards the test centre and then on to Mossley. The section between Old Street and the test centre car park involves a steep gradient — controlled descent and appropriate gear selection are tested here. Dukinfield's residential streets, accessed via King Street West, provide excellent practice for narrow-road positioning and the give-way discipline that regularly appears in independent driving sections.

Steep gradientGear disciplineResidential navigation

Your Test Centre: Ashton DVSA

Ashton learners test at Ashton-under-Lyne DVSA on Mossley Road, OL6 — approximately 5 minutes from the town centre. Test routes regularly use Stamford Street, Katherine Street, Manchester Road and the residential streets of Dukinfield. Your instructor will cover all of these before your test date.
