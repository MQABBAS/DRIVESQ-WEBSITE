A local guide for Wigan & Leigh College learners

Wigan & Leigh College is rebuilding. The Department for Education has appointed Willmott Dixon to deliver a new £48.8m net carbon zero campus at the Parsons Walk site, while the college stays open, and the college says more than 3,400 students study there every day. Construction on a busy site can change drop-off and parking, so agree a pick-up point with your instructor that does not depend on campus access.

Atherton is the Manchester-group test centre in the Wigan borough, with 13,035 tests and a 44.3% pass rate in 2025/26, the lowest of the eight centres. That says something about who takes tests there, not how hard the routes are, but it is a good reason to take a mock test and practise the local test roads beforehand. Your instructor can arrange both.

Atherton is the nearest DVSA centre in the Manchester group for Wigan borough learners, but check the DVSA booking service for the centre that suits you.
