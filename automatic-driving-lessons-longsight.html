Longsight Roads We Train On

Stockport Road A6 — Bus Corridor & Signal Junctions

Stockport Road A6 cuts straight through Longsight from Ardwick to Levenshulme and is one of the busiest bus corridors in Greater Manchester. Lessons here focus on the bus stops between Kirkmanshulme Lane and Slade Lane where buses pull in and out frequently, the pelican crossings at Longsight Market on Stockport Road near Plymouth Grove junction, and the signal-controlled T-junction at Dickenson Road where right-turning vehicles must hold position in a marked box. The 30 mph speed limit is vigorously enforced and the presence of cyclists filtering between lanes adds an important observation element to every run along this stretch.

Plymouth Grove & Manchester Royal Infirmary Approach

Plymouth Grove runs from the Oxford Road A34 junction near Manchester Royal Infirmary through to the Longsight boundary at Slade Lane. The MRI hospital entrance on Oxford Road is a major hazard zone — ambulances have priority and pedestrian crossing activity is constant around the emergency department. Pupils practise approaching the Upper Lloyd Street junction on Plymouth Grove (an unmarked priority junction that catches many test candidates), navigating the tight left turn onto Hathersage Road from Plymouth Grove, and dealing with the parking pressure near the student housing developments along Thurloe Street. The route then opens into quieter residential streets suitable for bay parking and turn-in-the-road exercises.

Dickenson Road & Slade Lane — Manoeuvre Practice

Dickenson Road branches off Stockport Road at the Longsight / Levenshulme boundary and curves through dense terraced housing towards Fallowfield. It provides excellent manoeuvre training grounds: the car park behind Dickenson Road Library is used for forward and reverse bay parking, the straight residential section near Whitby Road is used for parallel parking against parked cars, and the quiet close at Clyde Road off Dickenson Road allows unhurried three-point turns. Slade Lane adds another dimension — the Levenshulme Baths car park sits here, the pavement widths change abruptly near Errwood Road, and the Slade Lane / Stockport Road junction requires precise right-turn positioning across oncoming traffic.

Openshaw DVSA Mock Test Route from Longsight

The Openshaw DVSA test centre on Ashton Old Road M11 is the nearest test centre for Longsight pupils, approximately 15 minutes away. The standard Longsight circuit leaves Ashton Old Road, joins Stockport Road A6 southbound through Longsight Market, turns right at Dickenson Road, continues through the Slade Lane / Levenshulme junction, and returns via Kirkmanshulme Lane to the Belle Vue area before heading back to Ashton Old Road via Hyde Road. An alternative circuit extends through Plymouth Grove and the MRI junction before looping back through Rusholme and the Wilmslow Road corridor. We run both circuits in full mock-test format — independent driving included — so every junction is familiar on test day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which DVSA test centre serves Longsight?
Longsight pupils typically use Openshaw DVSA on Ashton Old Road M11, approximately 15 minutes from Longsight Market. Stretford DVSA on Lacy Street M32 is also accessible via the A57(M) for pupils in the west of the area.
Is Stockport Road A6 part of the driving test?
Yes, Stockport Road A6 features on several Openshaw test routes. Bus stops, bus lanes, pelican crossings and the Dickenson Road signal junction are all tested regularly.

Do you teach near Plymouth Grove and the MRI?
Yes, Plymouth Grove and the Manchester Royal Infirmary entrance on Oxford Road are key lesson routes. The hospital bus stops, the Upper Lloyd Street unmarked junction and the Hathersage Road turn all feature in our lessons.
Can I pass faster in an automatic car?
Many pupils pass in fewer hours in an automatic because they concentrate fully on hazard perception, positioning and observations rather than clutch and gear control. In busy urban areas like Longsight this advantage is particularly noticeable.
What areas near Longsight do you also cover?
We cover all neighbouring areas including Rusholme, Fallowfield, Victoria Park, Levenshulme, Gorton, Ardwick and the Hulme border. A single instructor covers the whole south Manchester corridor.
