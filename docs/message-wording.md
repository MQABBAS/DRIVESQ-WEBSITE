# DriveSQ message wording (one place, so the WhatsApp assistant and the automatic messages say the same thing)

**Key points every student message should keep (also for the assistant):**
- Friendly, short, UK English. Welcome them to DriveSQ and say they are registered.
- Their instructor's name and phone, plus a tap-to-message WhatsApp link.
- The instructor may be in a lesson, so there may be a short wait. Meanwhile: **message the instructor with the days and times you are available** to get started.
- Free Student Portal: https://www.drivesq.co.uk/student (log in with the mobile number, no password): lessons, theory practice, message the instructor, track progress.
- Have the provisional licence ready for the first lesson.
- "Just reply here if you have any questions."
- Prices only as published: £35 an hour, manual or automatic; new learners first 10 hours £330 paid in advance.

**Booking received (website booking, before an instructor is chosen)**
Hi {name}, thanks for booking with DriveSQ! We have passed your details to a driving instructor near you, and they will contact you shortly. They may be in a lesson when they see it, so if you don't hear straight away, they will get back to you as soon as they can. If anything changes, or you have a question, just reply here.

**After the instructor accepts (`dsq_student_welcome_2`)**
Hi {name}, welcome to DriveSQ! You're now registered with us. Your driving instructor is {instructor}, on {phone}. Your instructor may be in a lesson right now, so there might be a short wait. In the meantime, please message them with the days and times you're available so you can get started: {WhatsApp link}. Your free Student Portal: https://www.drivesq.co.uk/student ...

## Terms & conditions wording (use everywhere, keep identical)
