A local guide for Salford City College learners

Salford City College describes its provision as 16 to 23 courses, adult courses, higher education, apprenticeships and online learning, so its learners are a mix of ages and situations. The college's adult open events are held at FutureSkills at MediaCityUK on Thursday 3 December 2026 (5pm to 7pm) and Thursday 7 January 2027 (9am to 7pm), which are natural moments to start planning big changes such as retraining, and a driving licence is often part of that plan.

The college runs its own student transport network, with 2026-27 tickets on sale. If you rely on it, a licence is about independence outside college hours rather than the journey in. If you are retraining as an adult, a car can also open up jobs and placements that public transport does not reach.

Salford has no centre of its own in the DVSA Manchester group.
