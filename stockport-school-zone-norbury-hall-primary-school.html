How do instructors decide when to use Norbury Hall Road?

Do instructors take over the controls near school zones?

Only in emergencies. If a hazard develops faster than the learner can respond, the instructor will use the dual controls — but this is rare. The goal is always for the learner to handle the situation themselves, with verbal guidance from the instructor.

What if I make a mistake near Norbury Hall Primary School?
