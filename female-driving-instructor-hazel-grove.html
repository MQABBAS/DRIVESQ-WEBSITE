Roads You'll Learn On

Hazel Grove roads your instructor knows best

Hazel Grove has a distinctive road character — the long A6 corridor with its speed changes, the village-centre junctions and the residential streets feeding the A6 from either side. DriveSQ builds your lessons around these roads from the beginning.

  • A6 London Road — the spine of Hazel Grove The A6 runs through the centre of Hazel Grove with multiple speed limit changes between 30mph and 40mph as it passes through the village and approaches Stepping Hill Hospital. Bus stops, pedestrian crossings and right-turn holding bays make this essential test-route territory.
  • Chester Road A5102 — the southern approach Running southwest from Hazel Grove toward Cheadle, Chester Road requires controlled speed approaching the Norbury roundabout at the A6 junction. A common manoeuvre practice location for left-turn give-ways into side roads.
  • Commercial Road — through the village The B5358 Commercial Road through Hazel Grove village centre combines parked cars, a zebra crossing near the parade and slow-moving traffic — ideal for early-lesson observation practice.
  • Torkington Road — residential discipline This residential road east of Hazel Grove has junctions, give-way situations and parked cars creating meeting points. DriveSQ uses it for building confidence before the busier A-roads.
  • Dialstone Lane — the Stockport test centre road The road to Stockport DVSA in Offerton. The uphill exit from the test centre requiring a right turn across traffic is one of the most anxiety-inducing moments for test candidates. DriveSQ practises this junction specifically before your test day.
