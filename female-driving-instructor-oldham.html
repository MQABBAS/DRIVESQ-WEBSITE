Roads You'll Learn on in Oldham

Yorkshire Street

Yorkshire Street is the main shopping street running through Oldham town centre. Deliveries, pedestrians crossing mid-block and vehicles reversing out of parking bays create a constant stream of hazards. The two-way section south of the bus station requires precise lane positioning, particularly when left-turning buses pull out from the bus stop. Your instructor will explain each hazard type systematically before you encounter it at full traffic speed.

Town centreBus stop hazardsLane discipline

Mumps Roundabout

The Mumps roundabout at the eastern edge of Oldham town centre is one of the busiest and most complex junctions in the area — three major roads converging with the Metrolink tramway crossing nearby. Many learners struggle here on test because the signage is overhead rather than at the standard side-of-road height, requiring them to look up rather than forward. Your instructor will cover the approach from every entry point so you know which lane to use before you need it.

Complex roundaboutOverhead signageTest route feature

Manchester Road (A62)

The A62 Manchester Road links Oldham to Failsworth and Manchester city centre, with a mix of 30 and 40 mph sections and several traffic-light-controlled crossroads. The section through Waterhead — where Manchester Road narrows beside the reservoir — needs particular care when meeting large vehicles. The dual carriageway sections further west are good for practising overtaking judgment and lane discipline at higher speeds.

Speed limit changesDual carriagewayNarrow section

Union Street & Werneth

Union Street provides the southerly approach to Oldham DVSA on Lord Street. The one-way section approaching the town hall needs an early decision about positioning — staying in the left lane for Lord Street requires forward planning 200 metres before the junction. Werneth provides residential driving practice: streets that are completely passable but only just, teaching learners to read width restrictions and position correctly well before they arrive at the tight section.

One-way approachTest centre accessResidential precision

Your Test Centre: Oldham DVSA

Oldham learners test at Oldham DVSA on Lord Street, OL1 — in the heart of Oldham town centre. Routes typically include the A62 Manchester Road, Yorkshire Street, Mumps roundabout and residential streets in Werneth and Coppice. Your instructor will cover all of these well before your test date so nothing is a surprise.
