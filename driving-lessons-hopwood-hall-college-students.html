A local guide for Hopwood Hall College learners

Hopwood Hall College has two campuses, Middleton (Rochdale Road, Middleton, M24 6XH) and Rochdale, and runs events and employer forums at both. Its adult learners section covers in-person courses, training for trades and all modes of study. Trades and care careers often start early in the morning or finish late, which is exactly when public transport thins out, so a licence can make a course far easier to follow through into work.

Rochdale has its own DVSA test centre in the Manchester group. In 2025/26 it had a 48.3% pass rate, up 7.1 points on the year before, the biggest rise of any Manchester centre. Pass rates reflect who takes tests rather than how hard the routes are, but a local centre means you can practise on roads you may be tested near.
