FAQs — Automatic Mock Tests in SK8

What does a DriveSQ automatic mock test include?

A full mock test includes a Show Me / Tell Me question, 40 minutes of driving on real SK8 roads, one parking manoeuvre, and a debrief using DVSA marking criteria. The route mirrors the type of roads your nearest test centre uses — roundabouts, main roads, residential streets, and independent driving.

When should I book a mock test?

Aim for at least two mock tests: one about 10 hours before your real test to identify faults, and one 2–3 hours before to confirm readiness. Earlier mocks are useful too — finding faults early means more time to correct them. DriveSQ mock tests in SK8 give you honest, actionable results.

How is a mock test debrief different from a normal lesson?

A mock test debrief uses the same categories the DVSA examiner uses — driving fault, serious, dangerous. Your DriveSQ instructor maps each fault to the specific junction or situation where it happened, giving you a targeted list of exactly what to focus on in your remaining lessons.

Do mock test routes match the real test in SK8?

DriveSQ instructors know the routes used by examiners at your local test centre and plan mock tests on the same roads. By test day you've driven the route style multiple times. This familiarity significantly reduces test nerves and gives you a real read on your readiness.
