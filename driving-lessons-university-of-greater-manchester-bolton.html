What the University of Greater Manchester says about getting around

  • The University of Bolton now trades as the University of Greater Manchester, after a regulatory approval process that ITV News reported in December 2024.
  • The campus is on Deane Road, Bolton, BL3 5AB, a short walk from Bolton's rail and coach stations.
  • The university says visitor parking on campus is limited and recommends the Wellington Street Pay and Display car park.
  • Orlando Village, the student accommodation featured on the university's accommodation page, is a 10-minute walk from the main campus and lists car parking, a free campus bus and 24/7 security on site.
  • The university's student union is called the Greater Manchester Students' Union.

A local guide for the University of Greater Manchester learners

If you knew this place as the University of Bolton, the name has changed but the campus has not: it is still on Deane Road, Bolton (BL3 5AB), a short walk from the town's rail and coach stations. That central position helps lessons. Your instructor can collect you from the campus or from Orlando Village, which the university's accommodation page describes as a 10-minute walk from the main campus, with car parking available and a free campus bus.

Visitor parking on campus is limited, and the university recommends the Wellington Street Pay and Display car park. If you will bring a car as a learner or a new driver, plan your parking before your first week. Many students also live in Bolton's surrounding areas or travel in from places such as Bury and Wigan, so tell us your home postcode as well as your campus.

We are independent of the university. Name and campus details are from its own website.
