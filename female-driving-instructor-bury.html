Roads You'll Learn on in Bury

Bolton Road (A58)

Bolton Road is the main road heading south from Bury towards Radcliffe and the M66, passing Bury Grammar School and several residential junctions. The road carries significant HGV traffic linking to the motorway network — learners practise overtaking judgment and giving HGVs appropriate space before passing. The section around Elton Reservoir has an open feel that gives learners an early introduction to 40 mph rural driving within the BL9 postcode.

Rochdale Road (A58)

Rochdale Road heads north-east from Bury, passing the DVSA test centre on Rochdale Old Road before reaching Heywood and Rochdale. For Bury learners, this is one of the most important pre-test routes — your instructor will cover every feature of the approach to and departure from the test centre car park, which involves a right turn across fast traffic that catches many candidates on their first test.

Test centre approachRight turn across trafficSpeed variation

Bury New Road (A56)

Bury New Road runs south from Bury town centre through Prestwich and into north Manchester. The Metrolink tram line runs parallel to the road through this section — learners need to identify the tram stops and the pedestrians crossing the road to reach them, particularly during rush hours when the stops are busy. The road also features one of the more complex contraflow bus lanes in Greater Manchester, which your instructor will explain fully before you drive through it.

Metrolink corridorContraflow bus lanePedestrian crossings

Kay Gardens & Town Centre

Kay Gardens roundabout sits at the heart of Bury's one-way system and is a test-route staple. The roundabout has multiple exits including the bus station access road, which is often blocked by buses pulling in and out. Your instructor will cover the correct approach to each exit and explain how bus priorities operate here, so you don't hesitate on test day. The surrounding streets — The Rock, Haymarket Street — also feature in independent driving routes.

Your Test Centre: Bury DVSA

Bury learners test at Bury DVSA on Rochdale Old Road, BL9 — approximately 5 minutes from the town centre. Your instructor will systematically cover all of these before your test date.
