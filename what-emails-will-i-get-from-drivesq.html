What emails will DriveSQ send me?

Updated 16 November 2026

Short answer: a handful, and they are all about your booking and your lessons. When you book with DriveSQ you get a confirmation, your instructor's details, and, only if you haven't got started yet, a friendly nudge. Every automatic email links to an unsubscribe option, and we send no more than one automatic email a day.

The emails you may receive, and when

1. We've got your details

Straight after you book online. It confirms we've received your request, shows the details you gave us (with a booking reference), explains what happens next and points you to your free Student Portal.

2. You're booked in

Once your lessons are confirmed with your instructor. It gives your instructor's name and contact details, asks you to message them to say hello, explains what to bring and what happens in your first lesson, and shows how to use the free Student Portal.

3. Your new instructor

Only if we move you to a different instructor. It tells you who they are and how to reach them, so you're never left wondering.

4. Welcome back

If you return after a break, to pick up lessons again.

5. Ready to get started?

Only if you asked about lessons but haven't started. One friendly nudge, and a single reminder about a week later. Then we stop.

6. Your instructor is trying to reach you

If your instructor hasn't been able to get hold of you, so you can get back in touch.

7. Can we help?

If it didn't work out with a booking, a short note asking whether we can help put it right.

What you can rely on

  • Limited and relevant. Automatic emails are tied to your booking. Some also mention our current new-learner offer (the first 10 hours for £330), and we say so clearly.
  • No more than one automatic email a day.
  • You can stop them. Use the unsubscribe link in the email, or message us and we'll do it. If you unsubscribe, our system skips you.
  • Your details have a purpose. We share them with your instructor so they can arrange your lessons. Your request goes to the DriveSQ team and isn't sold or shared for other purposes. See our privacy policy.
  • Urgent things aren't left to email. For lesson-day changes, your instructor will contact you directly, usually by WhatsApp or phone.

Why we keep it this simple

Most of what makes a good learning experience happens in the car, not in your inbox. Our emails exist to make sure the basics happen: you know who your instructor is, you know what to expect, and nobody falls through the gaps. Behind the scenes, we also email our instructors to keep standards consistent. You can read about that in how we keep driving lesson quality high.

Questions people ask

What emails will I get when I book driving lessons with DriveSQ?

A confirmation that we've received your booking, a "you're booked in" email with your instructor's name and contact details, and, only if you haven't started yet, a friendly nudge and one reminder. We send no more than one automatic email a day.

Will DriveSQ spam me?

No. Our automatic emails are tied to your booking, capped at one a day, and each includes an unsubscribe option. Some mention our current new-learner offer.

How do I stop DriveSQ emails?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of the email, or message us on WhatsApp on 07352 932003 and we'll stop them.

Will I be told who my instructor is?

Yes. Your "you're booked in" email gives your instructor's name and contact details, and we ask you to message them to say hello and agree days and times.

Who sees my details?

Your details go to the DriveSQ team and your instructor, so they can arrange your lessons. They aren't sold. See our privacy policy for details.
