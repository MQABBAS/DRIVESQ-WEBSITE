Roads You'll Learn on in Swinton

Bolton Road (A575)

Bolton Road is the main arterial route through Swinton, connecting the town centre to Leigh Road in the north and the A57 Eccles Road in the south. The road carries significant commuter traffic during peak hours — learning to merge smoothly with fast-moving traffic near the Swinton Lions Rugby Club stretch and to navigate the pelican crossing sequence near Swinton Comprehensive School are both features your instructor will cover in detail.

Commuter routePelican crossingSchool zone

Chorley Road (A575)

Chorley Road connects Swinton to Worsley and the M60 motorway junction. The road passes through Pendlebury — where it narrows to one lane in each direction with parking bays eliminating the hard shoulder — and then opens up again near Worsley Woods. The transition between these two environments in quick succession is a good test of whether learners have internalised road position or are still consciously correcting it each time.

Narrow through sectionM60 approachSpeed transitions

Station Road

Station Road runs through the heart of Swinton town centre between Swinton railway station and Chorley Road, passing the bus terminus and several controlled junctions. The junction with Worsley Road at the town centre end is a four-arm traffic light crossroads with a separate pedestrian phase — timing your approach so you reach the junction when the lights are already on green (rather than stopping unnecessarily) is a skill that your instructor will develop over your early lessons.

Town centreBus terminusTraffic light timing

Agecroft Road & Pendlebury

Agecroft Road heads south from Swinton towards Agecroft Power Station heritage site and the Irwell Valley, connecting eventually to Salford and the DVSA test route area. The road includes a shared-use cycle and foot path section that requires lane positioning adjustments — cyclists emerge unexpectedly at the end of the shared path section, so your instructor will train you to anticipate this before the junction rather than reacting to it.

Cycle path junctionIrwell ValleyTest route connection

Your Test Centre

Swinton learners typically test at Salford DVSA on Silk Street, Salford M3 — approximately 15 minutes from Swinton town centre via the A57. Test routes include the A57, residential streets around Weaste and Pendleton, and the approach to and from the Salford Crescent area. Your instructor will cover all of these in your lessons.
