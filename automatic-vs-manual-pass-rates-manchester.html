Automatic vs manual pass rates in Manchester

Updated 15 November 2026 · Source: DVSA test-centre statistics, April 2025 to March 2026 (published June 2026)

Nearly one in three practical tests at Manchester centres is in an automatic car (32.4% in 2025/26, against 29.5% across Great Britain). But the pass rate for automatic tests in Manchester was 43.0%, against 51.5% for manual. This page shows the gap centre by centre, what it does and does not mean, and how to choose.

What the numbers show

  • Automatic pass rates were lower than manual at all 8 centres, by between 6.0 and 11.2 points. Nationally the gap was 7.7 points.
  • The highest automatic pass rate was at Bredbury (47.6%) and the lowest at Atherton (37.9%).
  • Cheetham Hill had the highest share of automatic tests: 46.3%. Bredbury had the lowest (24.9%).
Does automatic mean harder? No. The figures can't tell us why the rates differ. Learners who choose automatic may differ from those who choose manual: for example, some move to automatic after finding manual difficult, or have fewer lessons before testing. An automatic car is simpler to drive in one way (no clutch or gears), but the test, the faults and the standard are exactly the same.

How to choose between manual and automatic

  • If you will only ever drive automatics, an automatic licence is a sensible choice. It lets you drive automatics only.
  • If you want to drive any car, a manual licence covers both manual and automatic cars.
  • If you are very nervous, removing gears and the clutch can let you concentrate on road awareness. Some learners find that helps.
  • Whichever you pick, test readiness matters most. Take enough lessons, practise between lessons if you can, and do a mock test before booking.

Questions people ask

Is the automatic driving test easier than the manual test?

The test itself is the same and is marked to the same standard. The DVSA figures show lower pass rates for automatic tests than for manual tests in Manchester and nationally, but they can't tell us why, and the learners involved aren't the same groups.

What percentage of driving tests in Manchester are automatic?

In 2025/26, about 32.4% of tests at the eight Manchester centres were automatic. The Great Britain figure was 29.5%.

What is the automatic driving test pass rate in Manchester?

43.0% across the eight Manchester centres in 2025/26, against 44.6% for Great Britain. The manual rate in Manchester, worked out from DVSA's tables, was 51.5%.

Does an automatic licence limit what I can drive?

Yes. If you pass your test in an automatic car you can only drive automatic cars. A manual licence lets you drive both.
