FAQs — Intensive Automatic Lessons in SK8

What is an intensive automatic driving course?

An intensive automatic course compresses your learning into blocks — typically 2–5 days of driving rather than weekly lessons spread over months. DriveSQ's intensive courses in SK8 combine back-to-back lesson time with theory support and mock tests so you reach test standard faster.

How many hours of intensive lessons do I need in SK8?

Most learners in Cheadle & Gatley need 25–35 hours to reach test standard through intensive tuition (less than the 45-hour DVSA average because intensive learning builds habits faster). Your DriveSQ instructor will assess your starting level and give you an honest estimate in the first session.

Is intensive automatic faster than weekly lessons?

Yes — intensive learning works because skills practised repeatedly in consecutive days cement faster than skills practised once a week. The momentum builds quickly. However, some learners benefit from a mix: an intensive block to build core skills, then weekly lessons to consolidate before the test.

What is included in a DriveSQ intensive automatic course in SK8?
