Roads You'll Learn on in Middleton

Long Street

Long Street is the main shopping street through Middleton town centre, running north from the town square to the library and on towards Parkfield. The road is two-way through the town section but narrows significantly near the market — stopping correctly on the left-hand side without blocking the bus stop requires experience of this specific road. Your instructor will walk through every bus stop and pedestrian crossing on this route so that on test day the sequence feels automatic.

Town centreBus stopsPedestrian crossings

Manchester New Road (A664)

The A664 Manchester New Road links Middleton to Moston and Manchester city centre. The road passes through several distinct speed zones — starting at 30 mph through the residential sections, briefly reaching 40 mph near Alkrington Golf Club, and returning to 30 mph through Moston. Learners who don't actively look for speed limit signs frequently catch a minor fault here on test. Your instructor will train you to spot speed limit repeater signs well before you need to act on them.

Speed limit changes40 mph sectionSuburban road

Boarshaw Road

Boarshaw Road runs north from Middleton junction through the Boarshaw estate and up towards Bowlee and the M62 corridor. The road includes a multi-exit roundabout at the A627 junction where the lane markings are worn and misleading — knowing which lane feeds which exit is something that must be learnt before your test, not discovered during it. Your instructor will cover this roundabout from all four approaches.

Multi-exit roundaboutWorn lane markingsM62 approach

Middleton Road & Alkrington

Middleton Road connects Middleton to Oldham, passing through Alkrington Garden Village — a quiet, tree-lined residential area with excellent practice for driving in areas with reduced sightlines due to overhanging branches and parked vehicles narrowing the road. The Alkrington roundabout at Manchester New Road is compact and busy, making it a good preparation exercise before tackling larger roundabouts on test routes.

Reduced sightlinesCompact roundaboutResidential precision

Your Test Centre

Middleton learners typically sit their test at Bury DVSA (Rochdale Old Road, BL9) or Chadderton DVSA (Broadway, OL9) — both 15–20 minutes from Middleton town centre. Your instructor will determine which test centre your postcode routes to and cover all relevant pre-test roads in the weeks before your appointment.
