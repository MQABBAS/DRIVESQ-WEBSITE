# Who to send each outreach email to

Templates are in **Admin → Email → Templates → "Universities & colleges"** (13 of them). Add the contact first (Email → Contacts → Add contact), tick it, write/choose the template, then **Choose who gets it → "Only the contacts I selected"**. Send yourself a test first.

Send to a **team or role address** (partnerships, media sales, student services, employer engagement), not to a named person's personal address. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

## Before you send
- Offer in the emails: **10 hours for £320**, paid in advance, manual or automatic (standard £35 an hour), plus the free Student Portal.
- Send **one at a time**, personalise the greeting if you have a contact name, and keep a note of when each was sent.
- If an institution asks for a fee or has a process, follow it. Do not use their name or logo without written permission.
