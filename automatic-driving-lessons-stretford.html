Stretford Roads We Train On

Chester Road A56 — The Stretford Spine

Chester Road A56 runs the full length of Stretford from the Old Trafford boundary at Warwick Road North to the Bridgewater Canal bridge at Barton-upon-Irwell. Lessons on this road cover the signal-controlled Stretford Mall junction where right-turning pupils must wait in a central box while oncoming traffic passes, the bus lane that operates northbound in the peak morning hours between Edge Lane and St Ann's Road, and the 40 mph section south of Dane Road which transitions sharply back to 30 mph near the Longford Park entrance. The road surface features three significant dips near Brooks Bar that create temporary blind spots on approaches — a detail we point out early so pupils are never caught out on test day.

Edge Lane & Chorlton Road — Metrolink Crossings

The Altrincham Metrolink line crosses Edge Lane at the Stretford tram stop and Chorlton Road at the Chorlton tram stop. Both crossings appear frequently on Stretford DVSA test routes and have caused test failures for pupils who approach without observing the signal sequence correctly. Our lessons dedicate a full session to Metrolink crossing behaviour — arriving at the red amber phase, waiting for the all-clear white light, checking both tracks before crossing, and accelerating smoothly over the rails. Edge Lane itself is also used for parallel parking practice near the junctions with Campbell Road and Northumberland Road where consistent spacing exists along the kerb.

Barlow Moor Road & Longford Park — Manoeuvre Zone

Barlow Moor Road connects Stretford to Didsbury and Chorlton, crossing Longford Park at the Stretford end. The Longford Park car park accessed from Stretford Road is used for forward and reverse bay parking in relatively low-pressure conditions before we move to busier sites. Three-point turns are practised on the residential sections of Northenden Road and St Ann's Road near the Gorse Hill library, where road widths match those on the DVSA test circuits. Parallel parking is trained on Barlow Moor Road near the Barlow Road junction, where parked cars are consistently present and the 30 mph limit remains in force throughout.

Stretford DVSA Lacy Street — Mock Test Circuits

Stretford DVSA test centre on Lacy Street M32 sits just off Chester Road A56. The northbound test circuit leaves Lacy Street, joins Chester Road northbound, passes under the Metrolink bridge near the stadium approach at Old Trafford, continues to Seymour Grove and loops through the residential streets of Firswood and Gorse Hill before returning along Edge Lane through the Metrolink crossing. The southbound circuit takes Chester Road to Barton-upon-Irwell, crosses the Bridgewater Canal, turns through the Urmston roads and returns via Flixton Road. We run both circuits in full mock-test format including independent driving sections, so every junction is rehearsed before the real test day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Stretford DVSA test centre?
Stretford DVSA is on Lacy Street, Stretford M32 0JX, just off Chester Road A56 in the centre of Stretford. It serves south and west Manchester including Sale, Urmston, Chorlton and Eccles pupils.
Are Metrolink crossings tested at Stretford DVSA?
Yes, the Altrincham Metrolink line crosses several roads on Stretford DVSA test routes including Edge Lane (at Stretford tram stop) and Chorlton Road (at Chorlton tram stop). We cover all Metrolink crossing procedures in dedicated lesson sessions.
How many hours do I need for automatic lessons in Stretford?
Most Stretford pupils take 25–40 hours in an automatic car. Automatic transmission reduces the number of simultaneous skills and many pupils reach test standard noticeably faster than in a manual.
Do you cover Old Trafford and Gorse Hill?
Yes, Old Trafford, Gorse Hill, Firswood, Longford Park and Lostock are all within our Stretford coverage area. Pupils from these locations follow the same Chester Road / Edge Lane / Barlow Moor Road circuits.
Is the Stretford Mall junction on the test route?
Yes, the signal-controlled Chester Road junction near Stretford Mall is a regular feature of Stretford DVSA test routes. We practise both northbound and southbound approaches, and the right-turn box hold, across multiple lesson sessions.
Can I start with one lesson before buying a block?
Yes, a single hour costs £33 with no obligation to commit to a block. Many Stretford pupils take one or two single lessons first to assess their starting level before choosing the 10-hour or 20-hour package.
