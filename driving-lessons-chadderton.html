Local Road Knowledge

Chadderton roads your instructor drives every day

From Broadway to Foxdenton Lane, your DriveSQ instructor knows every junction and test route road in OL9.

🏆
Broadway A669

The main road through Chadderton — dual carriageway in sections, transitioning to single carriageway through the town. Examiners regularly use the Broadway corridor for speed selection, lane discipline and junction decision tests. DriveSQ instructors cover every stretch of this road with Chadderton students.

Primary test route road
🛣️
Middleton Road A669

Runs south from Chadderton toward Failsworth and connects to the Middleton Road test centre approach. The transition from 40mph dual carriageway to 30mph residential sections catches learners who don't check speed limit signs at the boundary. Active speed management is trained here from early lessons.

Test centre approach
🌳
Foxdenton Lane

Links the residential Foxdenton area to Broadway — a road with a deceptive bend that reduces sight distance on approach to a priority junction. Learners misjudge the junction visibility until they've been shown the correct approach position. DriveSQ instructors specifically address this in Chadderton lessons.

Limited visibility junction
🚉
Mills Hill Road

Shared with Middleton — the level crossing at Mills Hill is relevant for Chadderton learners approaching from the south. The amber phase timing and barrier sequence are covered specifically by DriveSQ before any student's test day, as the crossing features on examiner routes from Chadderton test centre.

Level crossing route
⚠️
Higginshaw Lane

Winding road climbing from Chadderton toward Royton — changes gradient and road width repeatedly. The combination of uphill starts, narrow passing points and a sharp bend mid-route makes this one of the most technically demanding local roads for learners to master.

Challenging gradient
🏡
Block Lane

Residential road west of Broadway — used for bay parking, turn-in-the-road and parallel park exercises. The junctions feeding onto Broadway require confident observation given the speed of Broadway traffic. DriveSQ uses Block Lane specifically for manoeuvre practise before moving students to busier roads.

Manoeuvre area
🏙️
York Street

Cuts through the older residential part of Chadderton — narrow with frequent priority-give-way situations where parked vehicles narrow the road to single file. Reading parked-car sequences and assertively taking priority where correct are key skills practised here.

Priority junctions
🎯
Burnside Way (Test Centre)

The road that leads directly to Chadderton Driving Test Centre — and the first road on every examiner route. Learners who have never driven the test centre approach cul-de-sac, understood the exit sequence, or know which direction examiners turn first are at a disadvantage. DriveSQ ensures every Chadderton student drives this road multiple times before test day.

Test centre exit road

⚠️ Chadderton-specific hazard: Foxdenton Lane blind junction approach

The junction where Foxdenton Lane meets Broadway has a misleading sight line — a gentle bend in the approach road means learners think they can see clearly but actually cannot see oncoming Broadway traffic until they're too far forward. The correct approach is to creep forward past the obstruction and take a second observation point before committing. DriveSQ instructors walk through this specific junction procedure as part of every Chadderton lesson block covering the Foxdenton area.
