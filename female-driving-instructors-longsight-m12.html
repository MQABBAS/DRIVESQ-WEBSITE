Learning to drive in Longsight

Longsight sits just south-east of Manchester city centre, between Ardwick, Rusholme, Levenshulme and Gorton. Most addresses are M12 or M13, and the A6 Stockport Road runs through the middle of the area.

Roads and junctions your lessons use

  • Stockport Road (A6)
    A busy bus route with frequent stops, side-street junctions and people crossing. It is where learners practise observation, lane position and judging gaps.
  • Plymouth Grove
    A long residential road lined with Victorian houses. Parked cars narrow the carriageway, so it is useful for passing parked vehicles and giving way safely.

Longsight is real urban driving: bus stops, pedestrians, parked cars and side roads close together. We use that to build the habits that matter on a test and afterwards, such as checking mirrors before every manoeuvre, keeping a safe gap behind buses and reading parked-car gaps early. Quieter terraced streets nearby are ideal for reverse parking and turning in the road.

Getting around: Frequent buses run along Stockport Road, and Longsight is a short drive from both Piccadilly and the Oxford Road corridor.

Women and the driving test in Longsight

The DVSA publishes pass rates by test centre and by gender. Longsight learners commonly take their test at one of these Manchester-group centres: West Didsbury, Cheetham Hill. Figures are for April 2025 to March 2026.

At West Didsbury, the DVSA recorded 8,292 tests by women, with a pass rate of 44.7%, and 9,514 tests by men, with a pass rate of 50.3%. That is a gap of 5.6 percentage points in men's favour.
