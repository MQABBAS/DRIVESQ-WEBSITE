Questions people ask

How long do I have to retake the ADI Part 3?

GOV.UK says you must book your next attempt within 2 years of passing your ADI Part 1.

How long does a trainee licence last?

6 months. You choose either supervision for 20% of your lessons or at least 20 hours of extra training.

When must I apply for my first ADI badge?

Within 12 months of passing the Part 3, or you must pass all three qualifying tests again.

What is the extra training deadline?

If you chose option 2 on your trainee licence, the extra training must be done within 3 months of getting the licence and before you book the Part 3, whichever is sooner.

Does this calculator store my dates?

No. It runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
