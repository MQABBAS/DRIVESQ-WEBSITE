Swinton Roads We Train On

Worsley Road & Swinton Town Centre

Worsley Road runs through the heart of Swinton from the Pendlebury boundary at Agecroft Road to the Eccles boundary near the A57 junction. In Swinton town centre lessons cover the pedestrianised section's transition onto the gyratory at the Swinton Shopping Centre entrance, the traffic signals at the Chorley Road / Station Road crossroads where lane selection for the Pendleton direction often catches pupils out, and the bus stops on Worsley Road near the Metrolink Swinton park-and-ride where buses indicate without warning. The 20 mph school zone on Moorside Road off Worsley Road is also included — speed awareness in school zones is tested by examiners using speed-monitoring equipment on the Salford DVSA circuits.

East Lancashire Road A580 — Fast Dual Carriageway

The A580 East Lancashire Road is the primary motorway-standard road between Manchester and Liverpool and one of the fastest A-roads in Greater Manchester. It passes directly through Swinton at the Worsley Brow interchange. Lessons on this road teach pupils to enter from the Worsley Road slip, build speed to 60 mph in the national speed limit section, read overhead signs for the Liverpool-bound exits, and exit cleanly at the Boothstown or Little Hulton slips. We also cover the contraflow section near the Leigh Road traffic lights where the speed limit drops temporarily during roadworks — a detail that changes seasonally and which we flag in every lesson briefing.

Lancaster Road & Pendlebury — Manoeuvre Zone

Lancaster Road and the surrounding residential streets of Pendlebury provide DriveSQ's Swinton manoeuvre training area. The Lancastrian Centre car park on Lancaster Road is used for forward and reverse bay parking — the double yellow lines at the car park entrance require pupils to stop just clear of them before entering a bay. Three-point turns are practised on Church Street near Pendlebury church where the road is typical of the 8-metre-wide residential streets that appear on Salford DVSA test routes. Parallel parking is trained on Broadoak Road M27 where the kerbside is consistent and parked cars provide realistic reference points for judgement.

Salford DVSA Mock Test Circuits from Swinton

The Salford DVSA test centre on Trafford Road M5 is the main test venue for Swinton pupils, approximately 15 minutes south. The Swinton circuit leaves Trafford Road and heads north via Eccles New Road A57 to the Patricroft junction, continues north through Barton-upon-Irwell and Barton Road, crosses the A57 at Peel Green and then runs through the residential streets of Boothstown and Worsley before returning via the East Lancashire Road A580 slip. We run this circuit in full mock-test format — including the sat-nav independent driving section near the Leigh Road / A580 intersection — so pupils build familiarity with every junction before their test day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which DVSA test centre do Swinton pupils use?
Swinton pupils most commonly test at Salford DVSA on Trafford Road M5, approximately 15 minutes south. Pupils in the Eccles area of Swinton may use the Eccles New Road circuit which starts at the Church Street Metrolink terminus.
Is East Lancashire Road A580 on the Swinton driving test?
Yes, the A580 appears on some Swinton test circuits as an independent driving section. It is a 60 mph dual carriageway and we introduce pupils to it in managed stages — short slips first, then full length — before any test booking.
How many hours do I need for automatic lessons in Swinton?
Most Swinton pupils take 25–40 hours to pass in an automatic car. The A580 and the Worsley roundabout system add complexity that we build up to gradually across the course.
Do you cover Worsley and Pendlebury from Swinton?
Yes, Worsley, Pendlebury, Little Hulton and Boothstown are all within our Swinton coverage area. Lessons from Worsley include the A572 / A575 / A580 Worsley Brow roundabout interchange.
What is the Worsley roundabout system?
The Worsley interchange involves a series of large roundabouts where the A572 Leigh Road, A575 Boothstown Road and A580 East Lancashire Road converge near Worsley Brow. These carry fast-moving traffic and require confident lane selection. We build up to them over multiple sessions.
Can I book a trial lesson in Swinton?
Yes, a single 1-hour trial lesson costs £33. Many Swinton pupils start with one lesson before committing to a 10-hour block (£330) or 20-hour intensive (£660).
