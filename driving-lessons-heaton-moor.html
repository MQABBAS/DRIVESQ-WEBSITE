Driving Lessons in Heaton Moor — Village Roads and the A6 Wellington Road

Heaton Moor occupies a pleasant plateau between the A6 Wellington Road South to the west and the Mersey Valley to the north. Its main street — Heaton Moor Road — has independent shops, on-street parking, and the kind of controlled driving environment where early-stage learners encounter real hazards (pedestrians stepping out, doors opening, parked delivery vehicles) without the volume of traffic found on the A6 itself. By mid-stage training, learners move to Wellington Road South — the A6 — which runs past Heaton Moor's western edge towards Stockport town centre in one direction and Didsbury in the other.
