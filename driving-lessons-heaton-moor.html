8 Key Roads Covered in Every SK4 Lesson

Road 02

Emerging from Shaw Road requires precise observation and timing. The narrowed junction mouth — a consequence of parked vehicles on both approaches — demands accurate road positioning and confident gap judgment in both directions.

Local Hazard — Heaton Moor Instructors Know This. Most Competitors Don't.

The junction of Heaton Moor Road at Shaw Road presents a priority right-turn situation that is unique to this area. Street parking on both sides of Heaton Moor Road reduces the carriageway to a single effective lane near the junction. A learner turning right onto Shaw Road must wait in the centre of this narrowed road while oncoming traffic clears — a positioning decision that requires precise placement of the vehicle to avoid blocking through traffic while leaving sufficient clearance from parked cars. Incorrect positioning here results in oncoming vehicles having nowhere to pass, or the learner being stranded at an angle across the carriageway. We dedicate specific practice time to this junction so that on test day, your examiner sees a composed, correctly-positioned right-turn — not a hesitant one.

3 Examiner Routes We Train You On

All lessons with DriveSQ incorporate the actual routes used by examiners at Stockport DVSA Test Centre. No surprises on test day.

A
Route A — Village Circuit
Heaton Lane → Heaton Moor Road → Shaw Road Residential Circuit

Begins at the test centre on Heaton Lane, joins Heaton Moor Road via the priority right-turn at Shaw Road, then completes a residential circuit returning to the test centre. Tests include the Shaw Road junction, school-zone awareness on Green Lane, and parallel parking on residential side streets.

B
Route B — Arterial Loop
Stockport Road A6 → Didsbury Road → Mauldeth Road Return

Travels north on the A6 Stockport Road dual carriageway section, turns onto Didsbury Road B5167 through a sequence of signalled junctions, picks up Mauldeth Road for the arterial return leg. Tests dual carriageway lane management, traffic light approach, and bus route awareness.

C
Route C — Estate Loop
Green Lane → Errwood Road → Estate Loop

Leaves the test centre via Green Lane through the school zone, enters the Errwood Road estate network for low-speed hazard work, then completes the loop back via residential streets. Tests crossroads priority, reverse parking, and meeting situations on narrower roads.
