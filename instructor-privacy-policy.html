Privacy Policy

DriveSQ Instructor Portal
Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DriveSQ ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the DriveSQ Instructor Portal app and website.

1. Who We Are

DriveSQ is a DVSA-approved driving school based in Greater Manchester, UK. The Instructor Portal is a private tool for use by DriveSQ driving instructors only.

2. Information We Collect

When you use the Instructor Portal, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address (used to create and manage your instructor account)
  • Login credentials (password stored securely, never in plain text)
  • Lesson booking and pupil data you enter into the portal
  • Device information for push notifications (if you opt in)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide access to the Instructor Portal
  • Manage lesson bookings and pupil records
  • Send push notifications about bookings and updates (only if you opt in)
  • Communicate with you about your role at DriveSQ

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device token is stored to deliver notifications to you. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you have an active instructor account with DriveSQ. If your account is closed, your data will be deleted within 30 days upon request.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@drivesq.co.uk.

Driving in Manchester — Local Knowledge

Manchester's Northern Quarter has narrow cobbled streets that test low-speed vehicle control and observation skills. DriveSQ instructors in Manchester cover Oxford Road and Deansgate in every lesson plan, ensuring students develop genuine local road confidence before test day. Your instructor's knowledge of M1 roads, pedestrian patterns, and local junction behaviour gives you a measurable advantage over learners who have simply followed a generic syllabus. The Trafford Centre, opened in 1998, is the UK's second largest indoor shopping centre with over 35 million annual visitors. Its road network — Trafford Way and the A56 — is one of Greater Manchester's busiest non-motorway driving environments.

The DriveSQ Student App — Your 24/7 Learning Companion

Every DriveSQ student in Manchester gets full access to the DriveSQ student app from the moment they book — at no extra cost. Here are the features our M1 students use most:

Hazard Perception Tips

The DriveSQ student app includes a dedicated hazard perception coaching section with tips drawn from real DVSA test data. Learn exactly when to click, how to identify developing hazards versus static ones, and how to avoid the most common scoring mistakes. Hazard perception is one of the most underestimated parts of the theory test — the app gives it the attention it deserves.

Daily Streak Tracker

The DriveSQ app rewards consistent study with a daily login streak counter. Every day you engage with the app — whether completing a theory mock, reviewing topics, or checking your progress — extends your streak. Research shows spaced repetition and daily engagement significantly improves theory test scores.

Progress Tracker

Your DriveSQ student dashboard tracks every lesson you complete, showing total hours done, topics covered, and an automatically calculated test readiness score. Your instructor updates your progress after each lesson — you see exactly what you have mastered and what still needs work, so every lesson has a clear purpose.

Theory Test Guide & Mock Tests

The average UK learner takes 47 hours of professional tuition before passing their practical test. DriveSQ students average fewer hours due to structured lesson planning linked directly to progress tracking in the student app. The DriveSQ student app includes a complete theory mock test engine with DVSA-style questions, instant scoring, and detailed answer explanations. Students can run unlimited mock tests before their theory test date — the app tracks scores over time so improvement is clearly visible.

The theory guide section covers all 14 subject areas assessed by the DVSA: alertness, attitude, safety and your vehicle, safety margins, hazard awareness, vulnerable road users, other types of vehicle, vehicle handling, motorway rules, rules of the road, road and traffic signs, documents, accidents, vehicle loading, and environmental responsibility.

Practical Driving Test Guide

A candidate can request their test in Welsh at any DVSA test centre in Wales. In England, tests are conducted in English only, though written guidance is available in multiple languages. DriveSQ serves learners from diverse backgrounds across Greater Manchester. DriveSQ's practical guide is built into the student app and covers every element of the Manchester City test routes. From the moment you sit in the car with the examiner to the independent driving section and final manoeuvre — the guide prepares you for exactly what to expect, eliminating surprises on test day.

The guide includes step-by-step instructions for all three possible manoeuvres (reverse bay parking, parallel parking, pulling up on the right), the Show Me Tell Me vehicle safety questions, emergency stop procedure, and independent driving using both sat-nav and road signs — so whichever combination your examiner chooses, you are ready.
