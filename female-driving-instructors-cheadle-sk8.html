What the DVSA figures show for women in Cheadle

The DVSA publishes pass rates by test centre and by gender. Cheadle learners commonly take their test at one of these Manchester-group centres: West Didsbury, Bredbury, Sale. Figures are for April 2025 to March 2026.

At West Didsbury, the DVSA recorded 8,292 tests by women, with a pass rate of 44.7%, and 9,514 tests by men, with a pass rate of 50.3%. That is a gap of 5.6 percentage points in men's favour.
