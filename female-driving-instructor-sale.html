Roads You'll Learn on in Sale

Sale's road network mixes busy A-roads, suburban streets, a Metrolink corridor and quiet residential zones — all relevant to your test.

Washway Road (A56)

The A56 is Sale's main artery, running from the M63 motorway spur through the town centre and out towards Altrincham. Traffic moves quickly through Sale and Brooklands — this road teaches lane discipline, matching speed to traffic flow, and reading signals at the Sale Road / Washway Road junction near Sale Metrolink stop. Bus lanes and cyclists using the outside edge of the tram corridor also need constant monitoring.

Dual carriagewayBus lanesTram corridor

School Road

School Road runs through Sale's residential core, passing Sale Grammar School and several smaller roads that feed into Brooklands. The school run creates complex hazard scenarios: double-parked vehicles, children running from between cars, and parents making sharp U-turns. Your instructor will time lessons to include both peak and off-peak conditions so you understand how the same road feels completely different depending on time of day.

School zone hazardsParked vehiclesResidential junctions

Northenden Road

Northenden Road links Sale to Wythenshawe via Northenden village. The priority reversal at the junction with Baguley Crescent catches learners who haven't studied give-way markings carefully. Further north, the approach to the river crossing at Jackson's Bridge needs early speed reduction and awareness of cyclists and pedestrians using the bridge footway. A useful road for rural-feel driving within the M33 postcode area.

Priority reversalsRiver crossingPedestrian activity

Broad Road & Sale Town Centre

Broad Road feeds into Sale's one-way town centre system around Stanley Square. Managing a one-way system under time pressure — especially when you're unsure which exit to take — is a source of minor faults on test. Your instructor will cover the correct approach from multiple directions so that on test day it feels automatic.

One-way systemTown centrePedestrian crossings

Your Test Centre

Stretford DVSA

Sale learners sit their practical test at Stretford DVSA on Lacy Street, Stretford M32 — approximately 10–12 minutes from Sale town centre via Chester Road A56. Common test routes include the A56 dual carriageway, Lostock Road and the residential streets around Gorse Hill. Your instructor will include all of these in your pre-test sessions.
