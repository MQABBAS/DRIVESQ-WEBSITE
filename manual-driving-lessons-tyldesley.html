Road-by-Road Breakdown

The Roads You'll Drive in Tyldesley

Elliott Street & Shuttle Street — Town Centre Complexity

Elliott Street is Tyldesley's busiest shopping street — a 30mph two-way road narrowed by intermittent parking, pedestrian crossings outside the library and Co-op, and the junction with Shuttle Street where a priority change catches many learners off-guard. We teach careful approach speed, early mirror checks, and confident signalling through this corridor. Shuttle Street itself passes the bus terminus where buses halt and pull out without indicating — reading commercial vehicle behaviour is a key skill we reinforce here. The Tyldesley roundabout at the junction of Shuttle Street, Elliot Street, and Mather Lane is a three-arm mini-roundabout that appears on Leigh test routes.

Manchester Road A572 — Speed Transition Mastery

The A572 runs south-east from Tyldesley toward Worsley, transitioning from a busy 30mph town-fringe road through a 40mph stretch before hitting the Boothstown junction. Northbound it connects to the A580 East Lancashire Road interchange near Roe Green. We use the A572 to teach progressive acceleration from a standing traffic-light start to 40mph, following distance management behind lorries and buses, and the Mosley Common Road junction — a right-turn off a fast road where gap selection timing is critical. The road surface deteriorates in places north of Shakerley, introducing learners to wet-weather braking distances and lane positioning around drainage channel repairs.

A577 Leigh Road — Arterial Driving & Priority Discipline

Leigh Road A577 connects Tyldesley town centre to Leigh — a continuous 30mph arterial with a series of staggered pedestrian crossings, school zones near St George's Central Church of England, and a pelican crossing outside Tyldesley Leisure Centre. Traffic volume is high at school run times (8:15–8:50am and 3:00–3:30pm) making it an ideal environment to practise urban driving under real pressure. The junction with Higher Green Lane is unsigned and sits on a slight bend — drivers approaching from Higher Green have a restricted view, and learners must hold their position confidently while waiting for a safe gap, rather than creeping into the flow.

Garrett Hall Estate — Beginner Foundation Work

The Garrett Hall residential estate — Garrett Hall Road, Firs Lane, Cleworth Hall Lane, and the surrounding cul-de-sacs — is where DriveSQ beginners in Tyldesley start. These wide, low-traffic streets are perfect for establishing the clutch-biting-point feel, smooth gear transitions, and mirror-signal-manoeuvre discipline without the noise of a town centre. Firs Lane has a gentle uphill section useful for hill-start practice and the T-junction at Cleworth Hall Lane gives beginners their first experience of emerging into a live road with moving traffic. The Tyldesley Leisure Centre car park off Sale Street offers a large, flat surface for bay parking and turn-in-road practice.

Test Centre

Leigh DVSA Test Centre

Tyldesley learners sit their practical test at Leigh DVSA, Marsh Lane, WN7 1ND — approximately 2 miles from Tyldesley town centre via the A577 Leigh Road.

Test routes from Leigh use sections of the A577, the Atherleigh Way bypass, the Leigh town-centre one-way system, and the Leigh Road B5217. We cover every route variant in dedicated pre-test sessions so there are no surprises on test day.
