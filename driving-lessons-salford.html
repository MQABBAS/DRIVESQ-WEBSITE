Local Hazard Alert

The Salford Hazard No Competitor Warns You About

Knowing the road is not enough — you need to know what catches learners out before they get to the test.

Average Speed Cameras Active

Salford Crescent / A57(M) Regent Road — Camera & Signal Trap

Regent Road feeds directly into the A57(M) inner ring road, which operates a strict 30mph limit enforced by average speed cameras across the entire stretch. Learners who have spent the first part of a lesson on slower residential streets in Ordsall or Broad Street often accelerate instinctively when they reach the wider, clearer Regent Road — without realising the camera zone has already begun.

The junction also has a complex signal phase where the filter onto the A57(M) operates independently from the main Regent Road signals. The filter green appears earlier and clears before the main phase — learners who miss this timing can either jump a red or be caught static in a filter lane holding traffic. We train on this junction specifically so it feels routine before your test day.

Why this matters: No competitor driving school in Salford currently addresses this specific combined camera and signal hazard on their public pages. DriveSQ instructors include dedicated Regent Road camera awareness in every Salford lesson plan.

Salford M5’s Roads & Driving Environment

Regent Road & the A57(M) Approach

Regent Road is the most technically demanding road in the Salford M5 driving area. As it feeds into the A57(M) inner ring road, learners encounter a combination of average speed camera enforcement and a complex independent filter signal phase that operates separately from the main junction signals. We cover this junction in detail — including exactly where the camera zone begins, how to manage consistent 30mph adherence in both directions, and how to read the independent filter phase so you never stall in a filter lane or mis-read a red.

Chapel Street — Historic Route with Modern Complexity

Chapel Street follows one of the oldest road alignments in Salford, but its modern traffic management includes contraflow bus lanes, one-way sections, and the kind of multi-arm junctions that appear frequently on test routes. The route into Manchester city centre along Chapel Street requires continuous observation, early lane selection, and bus-lane awareness — skills we build progressively across your lessons.

Eccles New Road to The Quays

The Eccles New Road A57 corridor forms the backbone of Salford driving experience, connecting the residential areas of M5 and M6 to MediaCityUK and the Trafford waterfront. Bus lanes, frequent signals, and the transition onto the modern peninsula road network at The Quays provide a lesson structure that takes you from residential confidence-building through to arterial road and roundabout competence within a single postcode area.

Lesson Design: Salford lessons are structured to progress logically — quiet residential streets in Ordsall first, then Broad Street complexity, then Chapel Street one-ways, then Regent Road and the camera zone. No learner is taken to the A57(M) before they’re ready.
