What The Manchester College says about getting around

  • The Manchester College lists campuses in three areas: Central Manchester (Centre of Excellence for Medical Sciences, Nicholls Campus and City Campus Manchester), South Manchester (Wythenshawe Campus) and North and East Manchester (Harpurhey and Openshaw campuses).
  • The college has a dedicated Adult Learners section and a published list of adult courses.
  • Its campuses are spread across Greater Manchester, so where you learn to drive often depends on which campus you attend and where you live, not on one central point.

A local guide for The Manchester College learners

The Manchester College is not one site but six, and where you learn to drive naturally follows which one you attend and where you live. In central Manchester there are City Campus, Nicholls Campus and the Centre of Excellence for Medical Sciences. In the north and east there are Harpurhey and Openshaw. In the south there is Wythenshawe. We already teach around Harpurhey, Openshaw, Wythenshawe and the city centre, so most learners can be matched with an instructor who knows their area.

As an adult learner you may be balancing study with shifts, caring or a family. Say so on the form. Lessons that finish where your day continues, rather than back at a campus you do not need to visit, are usually easier to keep up with week after week.

Fit lessons around your studies

Tell us which campus you study at and which days you work, so lessons do not clash with either. A valid provisional licence is required before you drive with us. You can apply for one on GOV.UK.
