Local Hazard — Oldham Road A62 Bus Lane

The A62 Oldham Road bus lane through Failsworth operates inbound only, during morning peak hours (7am–10am). Outside those times it is a normal traffic lane — yet many learners treat it as a permanent bus lane and refuse to use it at any hour. This causes hesitation, unnecessary late lane changes, and “undue hesitation” faults on test.

The problem is compounded near the Failsworth Pole landmark junction, where bus lane markings are faded in sections. Examiners know this road well. Your DriveSQ instructor will have you confident on the A62 in both peak and off-peak conditions from your very first lesson. No competitor page covers this specific A62 bus lane confusion.
