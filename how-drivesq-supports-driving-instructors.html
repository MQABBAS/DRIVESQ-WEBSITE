How DriveSQ supports driving instructors

Updated 16 November 2026

If you are an ADI or a PDI thinking about joining DriveSQ in Greater Manchester, it helps to know how we work with our instructors day to day. Here is what we send you, when, and why. Everything also appears in your Instructor Portal, so nothing depends on one inbox.

What you receive

A new student, with the details you need

When we assign you a student you get their name, phone, email, pick-up address and postcode, lesson type, availability, any test date and notes, organised so you can read it at a glance. In your portal, View opens the student's full profile.

A weekly Monday reminder

Every Monday you get a short email with one focus for the week (for example first lessons, test preparation or weather and visibility) plus the basics for every lesson. It also appears in your portal and as a notification.

A nudge for students you haven't been able to reach

A summary of students waiting to hear from you, so none are forgotten. We keep it to every couple of days at most.

Clear emails about money

Franchise instructors get a weekly Monday fee reminder. Referral instructors get a weekly statement of the students we've confirmed as booked with you, with each student's amount and a note to ignore it if you've already paid. If we've got something wrong, you tell us and we correct it. No booking means no fee.

Prompts that help your reputation

In your portal, a reminder at the end of lessons to ask learners for an honest Google review, with the QR code and link ready in your portal.

You stay in control

  • Email options: each instructor can opt out of everything, of fee and statement emails, or of weekly reminders. You'd still see the messages in your portal.
  • WhatsApp for anything urgent. Emails are a record and a reminder. Real-time contact is by WhatsApp or phone.
  • Everything in one place. The Instructor Portal shows your students, messages, progress records and notifications.

Why we do it this way

A driving school is only as good as the lessons in its cars. Regular, short, practical reminders keep standards consistent across everyone who teaches with us, and clear communication about students and money avoids confusion. See how we keep lesson quality high for the standards behind the weekly focus.

Thinking of joining?

We work with qualified ADIs and trainee PDIs across Greater Manchester. There are two ways to work with us, a fee per student who books or a flat weekly franchise fee, and we're happy to explain both. Read about the franchise option or independent versus franchise, or message us to ask for the current terms.

Questions people ask

What emails does DriveSQ send to driving instructors?

A weekly Monday reminder with one focus for the week, details of each new student we assign, a nudge for students you haven't reached, and clear fee reminders or referral statements depending on how you work with us.

Can I opt out of DriveSQ emails as an instructor?

Yes. You can opt out of everything, of fee and statement emails, or of the weekly reminders. You'd still see the messages in your Instructor Portal.

Do I get paid or charged without being told?

No. Franchise instructors get a weekly fee reminder, and referral instructors get a statement of the students confirmed as booked, with each amount shown and a way to tell us if something is wrong. No booking means no fee.

Is the information about each student organised?

Yes. You get their name, contact details, pick-up address and postcode, lesson type, availability and notes in one clear message, and the portal opens their full profile.

Do you take on trainee instructors (PDIs)?

We work with both ADIs and PDIs. Ask us about the current terms by WhatsApp on 07352 932003.
