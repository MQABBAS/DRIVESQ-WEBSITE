The same fault categories come up repeatedly at Sale Driving Test Centre. DriveSQ's lesson plans are built around eliminating these before they cost you a test.

Top Fault Categories

1. Junctions — Observation

Not looking long enough before emerging from a junction. The fix: look left, right, left again at every junction — even when it looks clear. Your DriveSQ instructor builds this into every lesson until it's automatic.

2. Mirrors — Change of Direction

Not checking mirrors before turning or changing lane. More than 3 instances in the same category triggers a serious fault. We make mirror checks a ritual from lesson one.

3. Speed — Inappropriate

Driving too fast for conditions — not necessarily over the limit, but too fast for the junction density or pedestrian activity on Sale Driving Test Centre routes.

4. Independent Driving — Navigation

Taking a wrong turn and panicking. A wrong turn is NOT a fault — panicking and making a dangerous correction IS. We practise wrong-turn recovery in every independent driving session.

How DriveSQ Eliminates These Faults

We track fault patterns using a DVSA-format sheet after every lesson. By your mock test, your fault profile has been systematically addressed and you know exactly what you're being assessed on.
