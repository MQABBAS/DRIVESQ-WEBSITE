FAQs — Parking Techniques in SK8

What parking manoeuvres are on the DVSA test?

The DVSA test includes one manoeuvre chosen by the examiner. The options are: parallel park (behind a parked vehicle), bay park (forward or reverse into a marked bay), or pull up on the right and reverse. You must complete it safely, accurately, and with good all-round observation.

What is the easiest parking manoeuvre on the test?

Bay parking is considered the most manageable by most learners because the bays give clear reference points. However, all three manoeuvres are straightforward once you know the reference points your DriveSQ instructor teaches for your specific car.

How do I reverse bay park correctly?

For reverse bay parking: choose your bay, drive forward past it (checking mirrors and blind spots), select reverse, and steer into the bay using your chosen reference point. DriveSQ teaches a consistent reference point method so you hit the bay accurately every time, not by guesswork.

Does DriveSQ teach parking in SK8?

Yes — all three DVSA parking manoeuvres are taught in SK8 using real car parks and on-road bays. Your instructor teaches a repeatable reference-point method so you're not relying on feel or luck. Mock tests then include a random manoeuvre exactly as the examiner would ask.
