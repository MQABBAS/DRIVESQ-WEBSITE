Test Centre & Routes

Sale Test Routes & Stretford Test Centre

Everything you need to know about where Sale learners take their practical test and the roads you'll be tested on.

📍 Stretford Driving Test Centre

The primary test centre for Sale (M33) learners is Stretford Driving Test Centre, located at 49 Kings Road, Stretford, M32 8GN — approximately 3 miles from Sale town centre.

Some Trafford-area learners also use Altrincham Driving Test Centre. DriveSQ instructors are fully familiar with routes from both centres.

Key Route Roads from Stretford DTC
  • → Washway Road (A56) — primary test artery with mixed speed limits
  • → Chester Road (A56) — busy dual carriageway sections
  • → Marsland Road — residential and junction challenges
  • → School Road, Sale — pedestrian crossings near Sale Grammar
  • → Hope Road & Cross Street Sale — urban navigation
🚊
Tram Crossings — Washway Road
Washway Road carries the Metrolink tram line. Tram crossings are a frequent examiner focus — DriveSQ lessons cover correct approach, signal reading and safe clearance of tram rails, which differ from standard level crossings.
🚶
School Road Pedestrian Crossings
School Road passes near Sale Grammar School and is a key test route. DriveSQ prepares you for busy pedestrian crossings, school-zone awareness, and the 20 mph zones that operate during school hours in this area.
🔄
Sale Water Park Roundabout
The roundabout approach near Sale Water Park on Rifle Road is a common test manoeuvre. Our instructors will walk you through correct lane selection, signalling, and hazard observation well before test day.
🚗
A56 Speed Limit Changes
The A56 through Sale transitions between 30 mph, 40 mph and national speed limit zones. Missing a change is a common test fault — DriveSQ lessons specifically drill speed limit awareness along this corridor.
🔵
Altrincham Road Mini-Roundabouts
Altrincham Road through Sale Moor has a series of mini-roundabouts and residential 20 mph zones. We practise correct priority, road position and speed management through these tighter junctions.
🛣️
Marsland Road / Washway Road Junction
This busy junction combines a Give Way, multiple lanes, and Metrolink proximity. Understanding lane discipline and observation here can make the difference on test day — our instructors will have you confident long before your test.

⚡ DriveSQ Mock Tests — Sale-Specific: Our mock tests follow the actual Stretford DTC examiner routes, replicating real test conditions. You'll know exactly what to expect before you step into the examiner's car.

Sale Local Knowledge

We Know Sale Inside Out

Sale is one of the most learner-friendly areas in Greater Manchester — and DriveSQ makes the most of that. We use the area's natural variety of road types to build well-rounded drivers who are ready for anything their test — and life after the test — throws at them.

Quiet Practice Streets — Brooklands & Sale Moor

When you're just starting out, Sale's residential neighbourhoods in Brooklands and Sale Moor offer calm, low-traffic cul-de-sacs ideal for building core skills. Here you'll master clutch control, bay parking, reverse parking, and basic road positioning without the pressure of busy traffic. Sale's well-maintained residential grid makes this early-stage practice both safe and effective.

Confidence on the A56 Washway Road

Once your fundamentals are solid, DriveSQ will progressively introduce you to Washway Road (A56) — the busiest and most test-critical road in Sale. This dual-use A-road through Sale town carries both regular traffic and the Metrolink tram line, demanding sharp hazard awareness. We'll work through speed limit transitions, junction priority, and the discipline of sharing road space with trams — all skills the Stretford DTC examiner will assess directly.

Sale Water Park & Northenden Road

The Sale Water Park area provides an excellent mixed environment for intermediate learners. Northenden Road and the approach roads to the park combine 30 mph zones, roundabouts, and Give Way junctions in a compact area — perfect for targeted manoeuvre practice. Sale Water Park itself is a popular landmark for pickup and lesson starts for learners in the southern M33 postcode.

Trafford College & Sale Grammar School Zones

Sale is home to both Sale Grammar School and proximity to Trafford College. School zones introduce specific hazards: pedestrians stepping out between parked cars, crossing patrols, and 20 mph school-time limits. Our lessons specifically cover school-zone hazard anticipation — the kind of instinctive observation that prevents faults on test day and saves lives after you pass.

Pass Plus & Motorway Lessons — M60 Access

Once you pass your test, DriveSQ offers Pass Plus courses for Sale drivers that include motorway driving on the M60, accessible from Junctions 7 and 8 near Sale. Motorway driving is not part of the learner test, so newly qualified drivers often feel underprepared. Our Pass Plus course removes that gap entirely.
