A local guide for the Trafford & Stockport College Group learners

This group spans two boroughs, which makes it unusual. Trafford College has campuses in Altrincham and Stretford. Stockport College and Cheadle College (Cheadle Road, Cheadle Hulme, SK8 5HA) are in Stockport borough, and the group also includes Marple Sixth Form College. We teach all around those places, from Altrincham, Sale and Urmston to Stockport and Cheadle.

Two DVSA test centres in the Manchester group sit in these boroughs: Sale in Trafford and Bredbury in the Stockport area. Sale had a 51.4% pass rate in 2025/26 and Bredbury 56.0%, the highest of all eight Manchester centres. Pass rates reflect who takes tests there, not route difficulty, but if you live between the two, your instructor can talk you through which suits your routes and the earliest available dates.
