Test Centre & Routes

Rochdale Driving Test Centre & Test Routes

Everything you need to know about the Maclure Road test centre and the roads your examiner will take you on.

Rochdale Driving Test Centre — Maclure Road

The Rochdale Driving Test Centre is located at Maclure Road, Rochdale, OL11 1DN. It is the DVSA practical test centre serving learners across OL11, OL12, and OL16. All DriveSQ instructors are fully familiar with the test centre layout — the waiting area, the car park used for bay parking exercises, and the routes that leave from it.

We always conduct at least one pre-test lesson from the Maclure Road area so you know exactly what to expect on test day. There is parking nearby for accompanying adults, and the test centre is accessible by bus along Yorkshire Street.

Yorkshire Street (A58) — Bus Lanes & Pedestrian Crossings

Yorkshire Street is one of Rochdale's principal commercial roads and a staple of most test routes. As a learner, you need to be confident with:

  • Bus lanes — know when they are in operation and which vehicles may use them
  • Multiple pedestrian crossings, including near Rochdale Exchange shopping centre
  • Heavy footfall, delivery vehicles pulling in and out, and busy junctions like the Town Hall junction on The Esplanade
  • Observation at junctions feeding onto the Rochdale Ring Road

Queensway (A6045) — A-Road Confidence

The A6045 Queensway features a national speed limit section — an excellent preparation for A-road and dual carriageway driving. Many learners are surprised by the 50mph sections after the built-up approaches. We practise Queensway regularly so speed transitions feel natural, not alarming, on test day. This section of road is also excellent preparation for the M62 motorway lessons available through our Pass Plus course.

Rochdale Ring Road — Multi-Lane Junction Awareness

The Rochdale ring road requires strong lane discipline and early position decisions. The Kingsway roundabout and connecting junctions demand clear mirror-signal-manoeuvre routines and confidence in multi-lane environments. We build up ring road practice progressively, starting with quieter times of day before advancing to peak-hour runs.

Local Area Knowledge

Learning to Drive Across Rochdale

We use the best roads in the borough — from quiet residential beginners' routes to challenging A-roads.

Spotland — Manoeuvres & Observation

Spotland's residential streets are ideal for learning manoeuvres in a low-pressure environment. We use these roads for parallel parking, bay parking, and turn-in-the-road exercises before progressing to busier routes. The residential junctions here are excellent for building your give-way and observation habits.

Bamford — Quiet Roads for Beginners

Bamford offers wider, quieter roads perfect for complete beginners who need to find their feet before tackling the ring road or Yorkshire Street. Many DriveSQ Rochdale learners begin their first few lessons in the Bamford area, building speed control and steering confidence at a gentle pace before progressing.

Whitworth Road (A671) — Rural Road Experience

The A671 Whitworth Road heads south from Rochdale toward Whitworth and the Rossendale Valley, offering excellent rural road experience — bends, crests, varying speed limits, and overtaking judgement. We use this route to prepare learners for the independent driving section of the test and to build confidence beyond town centre driving.

Town Centre — Pedestrians & Priorities

Rochdale Town Hall sits at the heart of The Esplanade, a busy junction where pedestrian crossings, bus stops, and multi-directional traffic converge. Driving confidently past the Grade I listed building and through the Rochdale Exchange shopping centre area is essential for your test — and for everyday driving in the town.

School Zones — 20mph Awareness

Rochdale Sixth Form College on College Road and the Matthew Moss High School area both feature active 20mph zones. DriveSQ ensures every learner is aware of school zones, the times they apply, and how to spot the signage — crucial for avoiding minor faults on test day and for responsible driving after you pass.

M62 Motorway — Pass Plus Available

Junction 18 and Junction 20 of the M62 are within easy reach of Rochdale, making us ideally placed for Pass Plus motorway lessons after you pass your test. Motorway driving is not part of the practical test itself, but Pass Plus motorway lessons dramatically improve your safety and confidence on the UK's busiest motorway.

About DriveSQ in Rochdale

The Right Driving School for Rochdale

Rochdale is one of Greater Manchester's most characterful towns — the birthplace of the Co-operative movement, home to the stunning Grade I listed Rochdale Town Hall, and a place where community and local pride still run deep. It is also a town with a genuinely varied road network that demands a properly skilled driver: multi-lane ring road junctions, a busy commercial high street, a tram crossing, a level crossing, national speed limit A-roads, quiet residential manoeuvres streets, and rural roads that feel like the countryside within minutes of the town centre. DriveSQ's instructors love teaching in Rochdale precisely because the variety makes learners into genuinely complete drivers.

Why Rochdale's Roads Make Great Drivers

Learning to drive in Rochdale exposes you to almost every type of road condition you will encounter as a qualified driver. Yorkshire Street teaches you to manage bus lanes and busy pedestrian environments. Queensway (A6045) builds your confidence at A-road speeds. The Rochdale Ring Road and Kingsway roundabout develop multi-lane lane discipline. Whitworth Road (A671) takes you onto rural bends and crests. Spotland gives you a calm space to nail your manoeuvres. Bamford provides the quiet straight roads every beginner needs. And the M62 — just minutes from Rochdale at junction 18 or 20 — is available through our Pass Plus course.

We do not follow a rigid syllabus divorced from local reality. Instead, we teach each skill on the road where it will be examined — so when your examiner turns onto Yorkshire Street from Maclure Road, you have already driven it dozens of times and every bus stop, crossing, and junction is familiar.

Rochdale Sixth Form College & School Zones

The 20mph zones around Rochdale Sixth Form College on College Road and the Matthew Moss High School area are an important part of Rochdale's road landscape. Many learners assume 20mph zones only apply at certain hours, or miss the flashing speed signs outside schools. We cover school zone awareness explicitly — it's a common source of minor faults in the Rochdale area that a locally-knowledgeable instructor can help you avoid.

Two local landmarks frequently appear on Rochdale test routes: Rochdale Infirmary on Whitehall Street (busy hospital drop-off zone, ambulance bay awareness, restricted stopping) and the Crown Oil Arena, home of Rochdale AFC, which generates significant match-day traffic around Willbutts Lane and Sandy Lane. Middleton Junction & Manchester Road (A664)

Rochdale Driving Lessons — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Rochdale driving test centre?

Rochdale Driving Test Centre is located at Maclure Road, Rochdale, OL11 1DN. It is the DVSA practical test centre for learners in the OL11, OL12, and OL16 postcode areas. DriveSQ instructors know Maclure Road well — including the on-site bay parking area — and always familiarise you with the location before your test day.

What roads are on the Rochdale driving test routes?

Rochdale test routes typically include Yorkshire Street (A58), Queensway (A6045), Manchester Road (A664), Drake Street, Milkstone Road, Whitworth Road (A671), Spotland Road, Bury Road (A58), and Edenfield Road. Key hazards include the Rochdale Ring Road multi-lane junctions, the Kingsway roundabout, bus lanes on Yorkshire Street, the Milnrow Road level crossing, and the Metrolink tram crossing near Drake Street. DriveSQ trains you on all of these routes specifically.

Do you teach on the Queensway A-road in Rochdale?

Yes. The A6045 Queensway has a national speed limit section — effectively 50mph — which is an important part of Rochdale test preparation. Many learners are underprepared for the speed change between the built-up approach roads and the Queensway section itself. DriveSQ includes Queensway regularly in lessons so the speed transition feels natural. This also serves as excellent preparation for the Pass Plus motorway lessons on the nearby M62.

Do you offer automatic driving lessons in Rochdale?

Yes — DriveSQ offers both manual and automatic driving lessons in Rochdale at exactly the same price: £35 per hour. There is no automatic premium. If you are unsure whether to learn in a manual or automatic car, our instructors are happy to discuss your goals and help you decide. Automatic licences restrict you to automatic vehicles, so we help you weigh that up before committing.

Can I learn to drive in Spotland or Bamford?

Absolutely. Spotland residential streets are ideal for practising manoeuvres — parallel parking, bay parking, and turn-in-the-road — away from heavy traffic. Bamford offers wider, quieter roads that are perfect for complete beginners who need to establish speed control and steering before moving to busier roads. Most DriveSQ Rochdale learners begin in Bamford and progress through Spotland before tackling the ring road and Yorkshire Street.
