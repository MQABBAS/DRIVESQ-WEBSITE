Benchill Road

The residential spine of the area — a broad, straight road with side-road junctions at regular intervals. Ideal for practising emerging, giving way and reading parked-car hazards in a low-pressure environment.

Brownley Road

A main Wythenshawe through-road with bus stops, pedestrian crossings, parked delivery vehicles and school pedestrians at peak times. Working through Brownley Road builds awareness of multiple simultaneous hazards.

Portway

One of Wythenshawe's main distributor roads, linking Benchill to Wythenshawe town centre and Simonsway. Lane discipline and roundabout approach are both practised extensively on Portway before mock-test phase.

Simonsway

The main east-west artery through Wythenshawe, used on Altrincham test routes. Bus lanes, crossings, roundabouts and lane changes all appear within a short stretch — a road that concentrates every urban driving skill.

Altrincham Road

Carries Benchill traffic south toward Gatley and Altrincham. A faster road with 40 mph sections and a junction with Simonsway that requires confident lane selection under time pressure.

Peel Hall Road

Loops around the edge of Peel Hall park linking Benchill to Wythenshawe Road. School zones and park pedestrian crossings make it a useful road for practising observation at uncontrolled crossings.

🛣️
Wythenshawe Road

The A560 corridor running along the northern edge of Benchill — a fast multi-lane road connecting south Manchester to the M56. The junction where Wythenshawe Road meets Simonsway is one of the most complex in the area and appears regularly on test routes.

Complex junction

⚠️ Local Hazard: Brownley Road Pedestrian Crossings at School Times

Brownley Road has multiple informal crossing points near schools, as well as zebra crossings, and during morning and afternoon school-run times the pavement-to-road transition is rapid and unpredictable. Children often move between parked cars without checking traffic. Examiners note that many candidates drive Brownley Road with their eyes fixed too far ahead — we specifically train close-range scanning on this road so pedestrian movements register early, not late.
