What the University of Salford says about getting around

  • The University of Salford is across three campuses: Peel Park (University of Salford, 43 Crescent, Salford M5 4WT), Frederick Road (Frederick Road Campus, Broad Street, Salford M6 6PU) and MediaCity (B4, Orange Tower, Salford Quays M50 2HE).
  • Salford Crescent station is between the Peel Park and Frederick Road campuses. The University warns not to mistake Salford Central for Salford Crescent, which leaves a 20-minute walk.
  • Buses 8, 35, 36, 37, 38, 50, 67, 100, V1 and V2 stop at The Crescent outside the Peel Park entrance. Students and staff can travel free on the 50 bus from Monday to Friday by showing their University ID.
  • University of Salford students can get a 50% discount on adult season tickets through Northern's higher education scheme.
  • For bikes, the University lists over 400 secure shelter spaces and a 300-space Cycle Hub at MediaCity run by Transport for Greater Manchester. Lime e-scooters run under a scheme licensed by Salford City Council.
  • On parking, the University says you must hold a valid parking session, a pay and display ticket or a Blue Badge when you park on its campuses.

A local guide for the University of Salford learners

Salford's three campuses are close enough to link but different enough to plan around. Peel Park (43 Crescent, M5 4WT) and Frederick Road (Broad Street, M6 6PU) sit either side of Salford Crescent station, while MediaCity (Orange Tower, Salford Quays, M50 2HE) is further away. For lessons, that means three natural pick-up zones: around the Crescent, around Frederick Road and Broad Street, and around the Quays. Tell us which one you live nearest to and we will aim for an instructor already working that patch.

Because the 50 bus is free for students Monday to Friday and several routes stop at The Crescent, many Salford students manage without a car during term. A licence is most useful in the gaps: late finishes, placements away from the bus corridors, trips home and jobs after graduation. If you do bring a car, note the University's rule that you must hold a valid parking session, a pay and display ticket or a Blue Badge when parking on campus.

Salford has no centre of its own in the DVSA Manchester group, so your instructor will advise which test centre suits your route.

Questions students ask

Which buses stop at the University of Salford's Peel Park campus?

The University lists buses 8, 35, 36, 37, 38, 50, 67, 100, V1 and V2 as stopping at The Crescent, outside the Peel Park entrance. For Frederick Road, any of those except the 67 and 100 take you closer.

Is the 50 bus free for Salford students?

The University says students and staff can travel free on the 50 bus from Monday to Friday by showing their University ID.

What is the difference between Salford Crescent and Salford Central?

Salford Crescent station is between the Peel Park and Frederick Road campuses. The University warns that mistaking Salford Central for Salford Crescent leaves a 20-minute walk.

How much are lessons?

You pay from £35 an hour whichever gearbox you choose, and a new learner's first 10 hours cost £330 when paid in advance.

What do I get with my booking?

Your booking includes free access to the Student Portal, where you can work through 700+ theory questions, 14 mock tests and hazard perception clips.
