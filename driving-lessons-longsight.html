Longsight’s Most Misunderstood Junction — Stockport Road A6 / Plymouth Grove

The staggered crossroads where Plymouth Grove meets Stockport Road A6 is the most demanding junction in Longsight for learner drivers — and no other local driving school gives it the attention it deserves.

The junction features offset lanes that make it easy to misjudge your position relative to opposing traffic. Critically, the right-turn filter phase from Plymouth Grove onto Stockport Road is very short, and the filter arrow is positioned at an angle that many learners fail to notice in time. The result: learners either stall mid-manoeuvre while waiting for a phase that has already ended, or pull out during the wrong signal phase entirely — a serious fault on test.

DriveSQ covers this junction in dedicated practice runs before your mock test, including the specific timing of the filter arrow and the correct road positioning when the lanes narrow on approach from Plymouth Grove. No competitor covers this in their standard syllabus.

Examiner Routes You’ll Drive

Plymouth Grove examiners use three principal route structures. Your lessons are planned around all three so nothing on test day is unfamiliar.

Route A

Plymouth Grove → Dickenson Road Loop

Leaves the test centre via Plymouth Grove north, turns into Dickenson Road’s residential network, navigates through Rusholme and returns via Wilmslow Road. Tests residential observation, signal junctions and independent driving on busier arterials.

Route B

Stockport Road South → Levenshulme Return

Heads south on Stockport Road A6 into Levenshulme, includes the Slade Lane junction sequence, continues to Burnage and returns via the residential network. Tests bus lane discipline, arterial speed management and multi-exit junction decisions.

Route C

Anson Road → Fallowfield Residential

Takes Anson Road west, introduces cycle lane interaction, progresses through Fallowfield into Withington’s residential grid. Tests lateral awareness alongside cyclists, Give Way junctions and steady residential positioning.
