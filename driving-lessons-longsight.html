⚠️ Longsight Hazard Briefing: Stockport Road A6 / Plymouth Grove Junction

The staggered crossroads where Stockport Road A6 meets Plymouth Grove is one of the most technically demanding junctions on any Longsight test route — and one that no competitor currently briefs learners on specifically.

The right-turn filter phase from Plymouth Grove onto Stockport Road is unusually short and the green filter arrow is easy to miss if you are watching traffic rather than signals. Learners frequently stall during the wrong phase or pull out on a standard green when the filter has not yet activated — causing immediate test failure. DriveSQ instructors walk through the signal sequence before your first approach, so you know exactly when to commit and when to hold.
