Failsworth Roads We Train On

Oldham Road A62 — Dual Carriageway Through Failsworth

Oldham Road A62 is the main road connecting Manchester city centre to Oldham and passes directly through Failsworth between the Newton Heath boundary at Culcheth Lane and the Hollinwood junction at Ashton Road West. The dual-carriageway section through Failsworth has a 40 mph limit and heavy through traffic. Lessons here focus on the signal-controlled junction at Brierley Avenue where right-turners must hold in a box and wait for the filter arrow, the bus stops near the Failsworth Shopping Centre that cause sudden braking in the nearside lane, and the transition from dual carriageway to single carriageway at the northern end near Hollinwood. The pedestrian crossing near Failsworth Pole is also a test-route feature that catches pupils who miss the repeater signals on the far side.

Ashton Road West & Hollinwood Metrolink Crossing

Ashton Road West branches east from Oldham Road A62 at the Hollinwood junction and runs through Hollinwood industrial estate towards Oldham Way. The Oldham Metrolink line crosses Ashton Road West at the Hollinwood tram stop — a signal-controlled crossing with a white proceed light and audible warning system. Pupils learn to approach at a controlled speed, observe the full signal sequence, check both tracks visually before crossing, and resume normal speed promptly after clearing the rails. The junction of Ashton Road West with Roman Road provides additional signal practice, and the roundabout at the Hollinwood / Broadway intersection is used for multi-lane roundabout approach work ahead of Oldham DVSA test preparation.

Marlborough Street & Failsworth Residential — Manoeuvre Zone

The residential streets between Oldham Road and the Medlock Valley Way provide DriveSQ's Failsworth manoeuvre training area. Marlborough Street and the parallel streets of Birch Avenue and Elm Street are used for reverse parallel parking beside on-street parked vehicles typical of those examiners use on the Openshaw and Oldham circuits. Bay parking is practised in the Failsworth Shopping Centre car park on Oldham Road, where the bays are standard width and the approach lines are clean and well-marked. Three-point turns are completed on Frederick Street and Kenyon Lane off Manchester New Road — roads wide enough for a confident turn but narrow enough to require accurate reversing back to the kerb.

Openshaw & Oldham DVSA Mock Test Routes from Failsworth

Failsworth sits between two DVSA test centres: Openshaw on Ashton Old Road M11 (approximately 10 minutes south) and Oldham on Featherstall Road North OL9 (approximately 15 minutes north). The Openshaw southbound circuit from Failsworth covers Manchester New Road to Ashton Old Road, through Newton Heath residential streets, and back via Oldham Road A62. The Oldham northbound circuit extends along Oldham Road A62 through Hollinwood to Chadderton Way and the Broadway junction loop before returning via Foxdenton Lane. We offer full mock test sessions to both centres so pupils can choose which test centre to book and prepare for that specific circuit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which DVSA test centre serves Failsworth?
Failsworth is served by both Openshaw DVSA on Ashton Old Road M11 (approximately 10 minutes south) and Oldham DVSA on Featherstall Road North OL9 (approximately 15 minutes north). We prepare pupils for either centre's specific circuits.
Is Oldham Road A62 on the driving test route?
Yes, Oldham Road A62 features on both Oldham and Openshaw test circuits through Failsworth. The junction at Brierley Avenue, bus stops near the Shopping Centre and the Hollinwood transition from dual to single carriageway all appear on test routes.
How many automatic lessons will I need in Failsworth?
Most Failsworth pupils take 25–40 hours in an automatic car to reach test standard. The A62 dual carriageway sections, the Hollinwood Metrolink crossing and multi-lane roundabouts are covered progressively across the course.
Do you cover Hollinwood and Newton Heath from Failsworth?
Yes, Hollinwood, Newton Heath and Moston are all within our Failsworth coverage. These areas sit on the primary test circuits from both Oldham and Openshaw DVSA centres and are covered in every lesson series.
Are there Metrolink crossings near Failsworth?
Yes, the Oldham Metrolink line passes through Hollinwood near the A62 / Ashton Road West junction. The Hollinwood tram stop crossing features on Oldham DVSA test circuits and is given dedicated session time in our Failsworth lessons.
Can I book a trial automatic lesson in Failsworth?
Yes, a single 1-hour lesson costs £33. Many Failsworth pupils start with a trial lesson before moving to a 10-hour block (£330) or 20-hour intensive (£660).
