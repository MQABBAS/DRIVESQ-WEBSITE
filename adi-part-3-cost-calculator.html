The official DVSA fees

StageFee
ADI Part 1 (theory)£81
ADI Part 2 (driving ability)£111
Trainee driving instructor licence£140
ADI Part 3 (instructional ability)£111
First ADI badge£300

You pay the full fee again every time you retake a test. Fees can change, so check GOV.UK. Renewing your badge later costs extra, and so do other things this calculator does not cover.

What a Part 3 retake really costs

A retake is more than the £111 fee. You may need more training or practice lessons, and if you are earning from teaching, any time you cannot teach costs you too. That is why the cheapest plan is to be ready before you book. See the score calculator and the retake rules.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to become an ADI?

The DVSA fees alone are £743 if you pass every test at the first attempt and take a trainee licence: £81 for Part 1, £111 for Part 2, £140 for the trainee licence, £111 for Part 3 and £300 for the first badge. Training, books, car and insurance come on top.

How much is the ADI Part 3 test?

£111 (checked on GOV.UK on 25 November 2026). You pay it again for every attempt.

Is the trainee licence compulsory?

No, but you need one to give paid lessons before you qualify. It costs £140 and lasts 6 months.
