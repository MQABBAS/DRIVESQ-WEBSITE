Local Road Knowledge

Roads We Cover in Levenshulme

All lessons use real local roads, building from quiet residential streets to the A6 corridor and then to Openshaw DVSA test circuits.

Main Arterial Road

Stockport Road A6 — Bus Stops, Multiple Signals & Crowcroft Park Junction

Stockport Road A6 runs north to south through the heart of Levenshulme and is one of the most used roads on the Openshaw DVSA test circuit. The road carries frequent Stagecoach and First buses on the Stockport–city centre route, creating regular bus stop events where learners must check for passengers stepping out and maintain a safe passing distance from stationary buses. The section between Slade Lane and Broom Lane has four sets of traffic signals within 400 metres — a demanding sequence of signal junctions that requires consistent approach speed management and correct lane positioning at each. The Crowcroft Park junction at Stockport Road and Cromwell Grove is a priority junction with a narrow mouth on Cromwell Grove, frequently used by examiners as a turn point on independent driving circuits. We practise this junction in both directions until the approach angle and exit positioning feel natural.

Residential Network

Albert Road & Matthews Lane — Narrow Terraces, Parked Cars & Give-Way Junctions

Albert Road is a typical Levenshulme terraced residential road — one lane wide with parking on both sides that periodically narrows the through gap to a single-car width. Driving this road correctly requires reading ahead for oncoming vehicles, identifying suitable passing places and negotiating the informal give-way system that applies when two cars meet on a narrow section. This real-world skill transfers directly to the test. Matthews Lane in Burnage provides a similar environment but with slightly fewer parked vehicles, making it ideal for initial lessons before progressing to the more congested Albert Road section. Both roads have multiple give-way junctions that require proper observation before emerging — a skill that examiners specifically watch on the test. We use these roads from early lessons to build precise positioning habits that persist through to test day.

Manoeuvres

Slade Lane & Burnage Lane — Bay Parking, Parallel Parking & Three-Point Turns

The car park beside Levenshulme Baths on Cromwell Grove is used for bay parking practice. The bays are well-marked and the site is quiet on weekday mornings — ideal for learning the reference-point method without the pressure of live traffic. Parallel parking is introduced on Slade Lane where parked cars create consistent two-car-length gaps near the junction with Errwood Road. These gaps are the correct size to practise the manoeuvre at a realistic level of difficulty. Three-point turns are practised on Burnage Lane and Kingsway — Burnage Lane is wide enough to complete the turn without difficulty but has the added realism of occasional passing cyclists and pedestrians requiring you to pause mid-turn. All manoeuvres in an automatic car benefit from the absence of clutch control during the rearward phase, making precise rear steering noticeably easier than in a manual.

Test Route Preparation

Openshaw DVSA — Mock Test via Stockport Road, Longsight and Gorton

Openshaw DVSA on Ashton Old Road M11 is the test centre for Levenshulme pupils. The examiner's circuits from this test centre include the Stockport Road A6 southbound from Ardwick toward Levenshulme, the turn at Crowcroft Park and the return via Longsight. On the Gorton circuit, candidates head east along Hyde Road A57 and through the residential streets of Gorton before returning via Matthews Lane or Slade Lane. We run full mock tests replicating both circuits before the test date. The mock tests are timed at 38–40 minutes and conclude with a full debrief identifying any errors. Most Levenshulme pupils complete the Stockport Road circuit twice and the Gorton circuit once before their real test, arriving at Openshaw DVSA having driven every possible test road in the correct format.
