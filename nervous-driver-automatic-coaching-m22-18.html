Why are automatic lessons better for nervous drivers?

Removing clutch and gearbox management frees mental capacity for road awareness, which significantly reduces the anxiety caused by information overload. Most nervous learners in South Manchester find automatic lessons noticeably calmer — there's less to go wrong, and the car feels more forgiving in stop-start traffic.

How does DriveSQ support nervous drivers in M22?

DriveSQ instructors in M22 are experienced with anxiety and patience is core to how every lesson is structured. What if I've had a bad experience with driving before?

DriveSQ instructors in South Manchester start each learner from where they are now, not from any expectation.
