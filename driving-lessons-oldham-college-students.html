A local guide for Oldham College learners

Oldham College and University Campus Oldham have grown fast. The college says around £47 million has been invested in upgrading or replacing facilities since 2012, including a £1.95m Brickwork Centre completed in 2025, and in 2026 University Campus Oldham is being enhanced with a £1.2m new University Building. If you are on a higher education course there, you may be travelling in from towns across the north of Greater Manchester.
