Pass rates for 17 to 25 year olds in Manchester

Updated 15 November 2026 · Source: DVSA test-centre statistics, April 2025 to March 2026 (published June 2026)

If you are young and learning to drive in Manchester, the DVSA figures are encouraging. In 2025/26, 17-year-olds passed 64.3% of the time across the eight Manchester centres: the best pass rate of any age from 17 to 25 and 15.5 points above the all-ages rate of 48.8%. By 24 and 25 it had fallen to about 48 to 49%.

What the numbers show

  • 8,088 tests in the eight centres were taken by 17-year-olds. That's 8.7% of all tests there.
  • Every Manchester centre had a higher pass rate for 17-year-olds than for all ages, ranging from 57.9% to 70.2%.
  • The pass rate falls as age rises, from 64.3% at 17 to 55.6% at 18, and about 48.4% by 24.
Why might 17-year-olds pass more often? The figures don't say. Possible factors include younger learners booking lessons more regularly and having more time to practise, but treat those as ideas, not findings. Age is not destiny: what matters is how many hours of good-quality practice you have had.

If you are 17 or about to be

  • You can start lessons at 17 (your provisional licence can be applied for from 15 years 9 months, and you can drive a car on the road from your 17th birthday).
  • Plan your theory test around your practical. A theory pass lasts two years, so book it when you can take your practical within that time.
  • Book regular lessons. Consistent weekly lessons usually beat occasional long gaps.
  • Ask about a mock test before your real test.

Questions people ask

What is the driving test pass rate for 17-year-olds in Manchester?

64.3% across the eight Manchester centres in 2025/26, based on 8,088 tests (DVSA data).

Do young people pass their driving test more often?

In the Manchester data, yes: pass rates were highest at 17 (64.3%) and lower for learners aged 24 and 25 (about 48 to 49%). The figures don't explain why.

When can I start learning to drive?

You can apply for a provisional driving licence from 15 years and 9 months, and drive a car on the road with an instructor from your 17th birthday (or from 16 if you get the enhanced rate of the mobility component of Personal Independence Payment).

Which Manchester test centre is best for young drivers?

On 2025/26 figures, 17-year-olds passed most often at Bredbury (70.2%). That reflects what happened there, not route difficulty.

DVSA advises that small numbers should be treated with caution. We have calculated the Greater Manchester totals, the "manual" figures (all tests minus automatic tests) and the comparisons ourselves, and they are not DVSA headline figures.
