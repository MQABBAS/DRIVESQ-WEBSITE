Key Tips — Bay Parking

use reference points on your car — the kerb reflection in your mirror is your guide
take it in three moves: pull forward, reverse in, adjust — don't rush
check both mirrors AND blind spots before each reversing movement
a bay that's slightly off-centre is fine — being over the line is a fault
