Local Road Knowledge and Driving Education

Building local road knowledge is one of the most underestimated aspects of driver training. Knowing which junctions require extra observation, where speed limits change, and where vulnerable road users are most likely to appear turns a competent learner into a genuinely safe driver for life. DriveSQ lessons are planned around the actual roads you will drive every day after passing.

Refresher Driving Lessons — Essential Knowledge

Returning to driving after a break requires rebuilding confidence, not relearning — a structured refresher programme gets you back to test-standard driving in the minimum number of hours. The following evidence-based tips represent the most common areas where learner drivers in Greater Manchester gain or lose marks during training and testing.

  • Refresher lessons on a motorway are recommended for anyone who has not driven on a motorway for more than two years — high-speed motorway driving requires specific confidence that cannot be rebuilt on urban roads alone.
  • Your insurance premium may reduce significantly after completing a recognised refresher programme — some insurers offer up to 15% reduction for documentary evidence of refresher training.
  • After refresher lessons, progressive solo practice on familiar routes before attempting challenging roads or long journeys is the recommended reintegration sequence.
  • Most returning drivers need 2-6 refresher hours to rebuild confidence after a break of 1-5 years — those returning after 10+ years typically need 8-15 hours of structured relearning.
  • Start your refresher in a familiar area — using roads you remember from your earlier driving removes the navigation challenge and lets you focus purely on building confidence in car control.

Highway Code update 2022: The Highway Code was substantially updated in January 2022 with a new hierarchy of road users placing pedestrians at the top and motorists at the bottom. Key changes include: pedestrians have priority at junctions when drivers are turning; cyclists may use the centre of a lane; and 'Dutch reach' door-opening technique is now recommended. All DriveSQ learners are taught the updated 2022 rules.
