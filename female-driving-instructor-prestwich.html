Roads You'll Learn on in Prestwich

Bury New Road (A56)

The A56 Bury New Road is the spine of Prestwich, running from Manchester city centre north through Prestwich village and on to Whitefield and Bury. The Metrolink Bury line runs parallel through much of this corridor, with level crossings at Heaton Park and Prestwich stations. Your instructor will cover both crossings in detail — the correct approach, where to stop if the signals activate mid-crossing, and how to position so your vehicle doesn't block the tram track. These are specific to this road and not covered by generic instruction.

Scholes Lane

Scholes Lane runs west from Prestwich village across to Kersal and Salford, passing through a predominantly residential area with good practice for priority situations at narrow junctions. The approach to the Ostrich Inn roundabout at the western end — a compact multi-exit roundabout where approaching vehicles from the left have priority — is a specific junction your instructor will cover, as it regularly features in independent driving tasks for Bury DVSA routes.

Priority junctionsCompact roundaboutResidential roads

Fairfax Road & Prestwich Village

Fairfax Road is one of the most useful residential streets in Prestwich for practising bay parking and parallel parking — it has a consistently available car park at Prestwich Arena, which your instructor can use for extended parking practice without time pressure. The surrounding streets around the Longfield Centre provide a realistic simulation of test manoeuvre conditions.

Bay parking practiceManoeuvre conditionsResidential

Kingswood Road & Heaton Park Approach

Kingswood Road leads to the Heaton Park gates and is a 30 mph road that becomes far busier than its speed limit suggests during school pickup and drop-off times. The junction with Bury New Road here is one of the most common independent driving features for Prestwich learners — emerging onto a fast A-road with limited sightlines to the right requires a specific approach that your instructor will practise until it becomes habitual.

Your Test Centre & Local Knowledge

Prestwich learners typically test at Bury DVSA on Rochdale Old Road, BL9 — approximately 15 minutes from Prestwich village. The test route will include sections of Bury New Road, the A56 bypass and residential routes through Whitefield. Your instructor prepares you specifically for the Bury DVSA route structure.
