⚠️ Whitefield-specific hazard: Bury New Road dual-carriageway merge

Where Bury New Road becomes dual carriageway north of Besses o' th' Barn, the entry from the side roads requires a confident merge decision at 40mph approach speed. Learners who hesitate here often lose position and provoke following-traffic pressure. DriveSQ instructors identify this merge point early and build student confidence through deliberate approach-and-merge repetitions.
