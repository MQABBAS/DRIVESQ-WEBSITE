Bolton Test Centre

Mastering the Bolton Driving Test Routes

Bolton DTC · Folds Road · Bolton · BL1 2RZ — A compact urban centre on a one-way system. We prepare you for every metre of every DVSA route.

⚠️ One-Way System Awareness Is Essential at Bolton DTC: The test centre sits on Folds Road, embedded within Bolton's compact urban one-way network. Learners will face one-way streets, shared pedestrian areas near the Octagon Theatre, and tram-line crossings on Trinity Street on virtually every test route. We make sure you're completely at home here before test day.

🚋 Trinity Street — Town Centre One-Way & Tram Lines

Trinity Street is part of Bolton town centre's one-way system and runs alongside tram-line infrastructure. Learners must observe pedestrians crossing tram tracks, allow for shared surfaces near Victoria Square, and maintain correct lane discipline on a road that changes character quickly. Examiners pay close attention to observation and hazard anticipation in this area.

📷 Chorley New Road (A673) — Speed Camera Transitions

The A673 is one of Bolton's longest straight arterial roads and features heavily in test routes. Speed limit transitions from 30mph in town to 40mph approaching Lostock — with fixed cameras between them — are a frequent source of minor faults for learners who either fail to speed up or overshoot the limit. DriveSQ instructors practise every camera location until it's second nature.

🚌 Deane Road (A676) — Dual Carriageway & Bus Lanes

Deane Road is a wide dual carriageway with active bus lanes running through residential and retail areas. Test routes use this road to assess dual carriageway lane discipline, bus-lane awareness, and safe merging. We practise the full length of Deane Road so you know exactly where bus lanes begin and end.

⚡ Manchester Road (A666) — Mixed Speed Zones

Manchester Road is Bolton's historic southern arterial route, featuring fixed speed cameras and multiple mixed speed-limit zones. The transition between 30mph and 40mph sections catches learners who haven't been coached specifically on this road. DriveSQ covers every speed change and camera position.

🛣️ Ring Road A58 — Dual Carriageway to Motorway Network

The A58 ring road provides the dual carriageway training ground linking Bolton town centre to the M61 motorway network. Examiners use this section to assess higher-speed driving, safe following distance, and lane positioning. We build dual carriageway confidence early in your training.

Know the Roads

Bolton Road Hazards We Prepare You For

Local hazards that catch unprepared learners — and why DriveSQ coaches every one of them specifically.

🚋 Tram Lines & Shared Surfaces — Trinity Street / Victoria Square

Bolton's Metrolink tram infrastructure means learners must judge safe crossing angles over tram rails, watch for pedestrians in shared-surface zones near the Octagon Theatre and Victoria Square, and understand that trams have absolute priority. Misjudging wheel angle over wet tram lines is a common fault. We practise this until it's automatic.

📷 Speed Camera Zones — Chorley New Road & Manchester Road

Both the A673 and A666 have fixed speed cameras at 30/40mph boundary points. A very common test fault is either continuing at 30mph into a 40mph zone (unnecessary slowness) or not noticing the camera before a limit transition. DriveSQ instructors walk you through every camera location on lesson routes so there are zero surprises on test day.

🏟️ Matchday Traffic — Burnden Road & University of Bolton Stadium

Bolton Wanderers FC — holders of four FA Cups and one of England's most historic clubs — play at the University of Bolton Stadium. On matchdays, Burnden Road and surrounding streets see intense pedestrian and vehicle congestion. Lessons timed near kick-off or full-time provide excellent real-world hazard training; examiners do schedule tests on matchdays and expect full awareness.

🛒 Delivery Lorries — Bolton Market Hall on Market Street

Bolton Market Hall is one of the finest Victorian indoor markets in the North of England, drawing heavy delivery lorry traffic along Market Street. Early-morning and mid-morning lessons frequently encounter lorries partially blocking lanes, reversing into loading bays, and generating blind spots. We use these conditions as deliberate hazard-perception training.

🔄 Middlebrook Retail Park Roundabout — Horwich

The large roundabout serving Middlebrook Retail Park near Horwich is a busy HGV and shopper interchange. Large vehicles making wide turns, difficult right exits, and heavy lane-change decision points make this one of the most challenging roundabouts in the Bolton area. We prepare learners from BL5 and BL6 specifically for this junction before their test.

Winter Hill — The Bolton Landmark

Winter Hill's iconic TV transmitter tower is visible from dozens of lesson routes across Bolton. It's the local landmark learners use to orient themselves during lessons in BL1 and BL6. Country lanes in the Rivington area beneath Winter Hill — including Lever Park — are used for rural driving, steep-gradient practice, and learning to meet oncoming traffic on narrow roads.

The Octagon Theatre & Victoria Square

Bolton's Octagon Theatre sits at the heart of a pedestrian-priority area around Victoria Square with shared surfaces, informal crossings, and event-related foot traffic. This zone demands heightened observation and reduced speed — examiners look for appropriate caution and complete mirror checks in pedestrian-priority areas.

Bolton Wanderers — 4x FA Cup Winners

BWFC, winners of the FA Cup four times, attract significant matchday traffic to the University of Bolton Stadium area. Burnden Road and surrounding residential streets fill with supporters on home fixtures. Lever Park & Rivington Country Lanes

Rivington Pike and the Lever Park estate offer some of Greater Manchester's finest rural driving — steep gradients, narrow country lanes, farm entrances, and passing places. We use these roads for learners who need to build confidence beyond town-centre driving before their test. The climb up toward Rivington Pike is excellent preparation for hill-start mastery.

Bolton Market Hall — Victorian Heritage

Bolton Market Hall, built in 1855, is a Grade II listed Victorian market generating busy pedestrian and delivery traffic on Market Street. Test routes through the town centre pass this area, requiring careful observation for shoppers stepping out between parked vehicles and lorries unloading on narrow sections.

Bolton Driving Test Centre is at Folds Road, Bolton, BL1 2RZ. What speed limits should I know for Chorley New Road? +

Chorley New Road (A673) runs from Bolton town centre toward Lostock and features multiple fixed speed cameras. The limit transitions from 30mph in the town-centre section to 40mph approaching Lostock. Failing to increase speed after the 40mph signs — or not noticing the transition — is a common minor fault at Bolton test centre. DriveSQ instructors specifically prepare you for these exact transition points and camera locations so you're never caught out.
