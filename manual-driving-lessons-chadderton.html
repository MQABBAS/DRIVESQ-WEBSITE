The Roads We Cover in OL9

Four contrasting Chadderton environments build every skill the Oldham DVSA examiner will test.

Middleton Road A669 — Core Arterial Training

The A669 Middleton Road runs through the heart of Chadderton connecting Oldham to Middleton and carrying a constant flow of mixed traffic. Between the Chadderton town-centre signal junction at King Street and the Boardman Lane crossroads the road passes through a 30mph residential zone, widens to accommodate a shared bus lane near the Tesco junction, and presents a series of side-road give-way junctions that demand consistent mirror discipline. The right-turn into Foxdenton Lane at the northern end of the commercial strip is a key Oldham DVSA test junction — a dedicated right-turn lane with a filter arrow that releases on a short independent phase. Learners practise the full MSPSL routine on approach, positioning into the filter lane well before the junction, then executing the turn smoothly without cutting the corner into the opposing bus lane on Foxdenton Lane.

Broadway Roundabout — Four-Arm Junction Complexity

Where Broadway A663, Middleton Road A669, and Foxdenton Lane converge sits the Broadway roundabout — four arms, inconsistent traffic volumes, and a bus lane on the Broadway western approach that ends 30 metres before the give-way line. Learners approaching from Middleton Road must select the correct lane for their destination before reaching the give-way because the lanes are established by road markings alone, with no overhead gantries or signs. From the Foxdenton Lane arm the roundabout has a tighter approach curve that requires a slower entry speed than the other arms. We practise all four entry points and all exit combinations, including the commonly mistaken straight-ahead exit from the Broadway arm which requires lane discipline to avoid crossing into the right lane on exit. Examiners from Oldham DVSA regularly route test candidates through this roundabout in both directions.

Foxdenton Lane — Semi-Rural Speed and Observation

Foxdenton Lane runs north-east from the Broadway roundabout through the Foxdenton Hall estate and into open countryside before meeting the Higginshaw Lane junction at the Royton border. The speed limit transitions from 30mph through the residential section near Foxdenton Park to 40mph at the countryside boundary, with the change marked by a limit sign on a slight left bend that drivers can miss if they are not scanning signage. We practise reading the transition accurately, settling to the new limit, and maintaining it across a series of small crests where the road dips toward a stream crossing before rising again toward Higginshaw. The narrow lane section near Foxdenton Hall itself — single-file between stone walls — introduces learners to priority negotiation on unmarked rural roads, a skill tested occasionally on Oldham examiner routes when they venture this far from the town centre.

Chadderton Park Estate — Beginner Foundation Streets

The residential grid of streets between Turf Lane and Burnley Lane on the eastern edge of Chadderton provides the calm, flat, well-surfaced roads that first-lesson beginners need. Belfield Road and Hollinwood Avenue are straight and wide with low traffic — ideal for establishing clutch control, gear selection, and mirror discipline without any junction pressure. The gentle S-bend near Chadderton Park itself introduces low-speed steering without sharp turns, building confidence before more complex manoeuvres are attempted. We use the Chadderton Park car park off Turf Lane for bay parking and three-point turn practice before progressing to the on-street bays near the shops on Middleton Road. This structured progression from park to estate to arterial road means pupils develop real skills in logical order rather than being overwhelmed by complexity before they are ready.

Common Questions

Which DVSA test centre do Chadderton learners use?

Chadderton pupils sit their practical test at Oldham DVSA Test Centre on Medlock Road OL1, approximately 2 miles east via Middleton Road and the A671. Our instructors practise the full route structure so test day feels familiar.

What are the most challenging junctions in Chadderton for learners?

The Broadway roundabout at the junction of Middleton Road, Broadway, and Foxdenton Lane is the most demanding — four arms with uneven traffic volumes, a short exit to the M60 slip road, and a bus lane on Broadway before the roundabout entry. The Oldham Road A62 right-turn filter at Chadderton Way junction also features regularly.

How many hours do Chadderton learners typically need?

Most OL9 learners require 35–45 hours combining professional lessons with private practice on the quieter Chadderton Park estate roads. Our structured approach means every hour builds directly on the last.

Do you teach on the M60 approach roads near Chadderton?

Yes. The Broadway A663 feeds directly to the M60 junction 20 slip road. We include approach work and lane discipline on Broadway as part of standard lessons once pupils are comfortable at 40mph, so higher-speed merging feels natural rather than frightening.

Is Chadderton a good place to learn to drive?

Chadderton offers an excellent mix for learners — quiet estate streets for beginners, the A669 arterial for mid-stage speed work, the Broadway roundabout for junction complexity, and M60 approach roads for high-speed preparation. Everything needed to pass the Oldham DVSA test is within a 3-mile radius of the town centre.
