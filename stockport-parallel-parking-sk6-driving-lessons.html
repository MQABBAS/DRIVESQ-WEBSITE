Key Tips — Parallel Parking

pull up alongside the car you're parking behind — your door mirror level with theirs
reverse slowly until your front clears their rear bumper, then turn
aim to finish within 50cm of the kerb and within 2 car lengths of the reference car
if you end up at a bad angle, pull forward and re-approach — it's not a fault to correct
