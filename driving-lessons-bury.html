About the Test Centre

Bury Driving Test Centre is located on Angouleme Way, Bury, BL9 0EH — a road named after Bury's twin town of Angoulême in France, reflecting Bury's European civic connections. The centre sits adjacent to The Rock Shopping Centre, making the immediate exit complex, with vehicle and pedestrian traffic from one of Bury's busiest retail destinations right on the doorstep.

From the test centre, examiners commonly route learners towards Manchester Road (A56), Bolton Road (A58), and through the town centre network including Peel Way and Princess Street before returning via Knowsley Street. DriveSQ instructors rehearse every likely route with you before test day.
