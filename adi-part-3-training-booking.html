Questions people ask

Is the ADI Part 3 training booking form free?

Yes. Sending the form does not cost anything and no payment is taken. We use it to message you about training.

Who sees the details I send?

The form goes to a private DriveSQ system that only DriveSQ can read, and we also receive an email copy. See our privacy policy.

What if I do not know my test date or centre?

Leave them blank. Every question after the first is optional except your name, mobile number and the contact tick.

Can I just message you instead?

Yes. Message DriveSQ on WhatsApp on 07352 932003 and say where you are with your Part 3.

Do I have to have a trainee licence?

No. You can ask about training at any stage. If you want to teach paying pupils before you qualify you will need a trainee licence. See our trainee licence guide.
