How DriveSQ keeps driving lesson quality high

Updated 16 November 2026

Learners can't watch what happens in every car, so we do three things. We set clear standards, we remind every instructor of them every week, and we give learners their own way to track progress and tell us how it's going. Here is what that looks like in practice.

What we ask of every instructor, every lesson

  • Check the provisional licence is valid and matches before the first drive.
  • Check eyesight before the first lesson: reading a number plate from 20 metres, with glasses or lenses if needed.
  • No hand-held phone while supervising a learner (Highway Code rule 149).
  • L plates front and back, dual controls checked and seat belts on (rule 99).
  • The instructor's badge (or trainee licence) on display in the car.
  • Update the portal after each lesson, with status, a lesson log and progress.

A different focus every week

Each Monday our instructors get a short reminder with one topic to concentrate on. The topics rotate through:

First lessons

Start somewhere quiet, explain the controls clearly and agree a plan.

Planning a quality lesson

Read the last lesson's notes, agree one or two goals with the learner and finish with them reflecting on their own performance.

Coaching, not just instructing

Ask before you tell, match the learner's learning style and give specific, timely feedback.

Risk management

Agree how responsibility for risk is shared, give directions clearly and in good time, and debrief any intervention.

Test preparation

Use mock tests on local routes, practise show me / tell me questions and use the theory mock tests in the Student Portal.

Weather and visibility

Check lights, demisting and tyres, teach longer stopping distances on wet roads and reschedule if conditions aren't safe.

Rest and fatigue

Don't teach tired. Build in breaks on long teaching days.

Professional standards

Be on time, keep messages professional and take payments only as agreed.

Lesson records

Log every lesson so learners can see their progress, with the topics covered and what to work on next.

Reviews

Ask every learner for an honest Google review at the end of a lesson, never offering discounts or free lessons for one.

What learners get

  • The free Student Portal with 700+ theory practice questions, 14 mock tests, hazard perception training and a progress dashboard. See the DriveSQ Student Portal.
  • Lesson records so you can see what was covered and what to work on next.
  • Mock tests on local routes before you book or confirm your real test.
  • A clear way to tell us if something isn't right. Message us on WhatsApp.
An honest note. These are the standards we ask for and remind instructors about every week. We can't be in every car, so if a lesson isn't up to scratch, tell us and we'll look into it.

Questions people ask

How does DriveSQ make sure driving lessons are good quality?

We set clear standards for every lesson, remind our instructors of them every week with a rotating focus (such as test preparation, risk management and coaching), keep lesson records in a portal, and give learners a way to tell us how it's going.

What checks do DriveSQ instructors do before a lesson?

We ask instructors to check the learner's provisional licence and eyesight, have L plates and dual controls in order, display their badge, avoid hand-held phones and update the portal after the lesson.

Do DriveSQ learners get mock tests?

Instructors are asked to use mock tests on local routes before a test is booked or confirmed, and learners have 14 theory mock tests in the free Student Portal.

What if I'm not happy with a lesson?

Message us on WhatsApp on 07352 932003 and we'll look into it. We want to hear it straight away.
