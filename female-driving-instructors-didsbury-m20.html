Female learners and the driving test around Didsbury

The DVSA publishes pass rates by test centre and by gender. Didsbury learners commonly take their test at one of these Manchester-group centres: West Didsbury, Sale. Figures are for April 2025 to March 2026.

At West Didsbury, the DVSA recorded 8,292 tests by women, with a pass rate of 44.7%, and 9,514 tests by men, with a pass rate of 50.3%. That is a gap of 5.6 percentage points in men's favour.
