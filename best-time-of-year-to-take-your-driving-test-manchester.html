Best time of year to take your driving test in Manchester

Updated 15 November 2026 · Source: DVSA test-centre statistics, April 2025 to March 2026 (published June 2026)

Does it matter when you take your driving test? In Manchester, barely. Across the eight test centres in 2025/26, the monthly pass rate stayed between 47.4% (November) and 50.2% (July). That's a spread of just 2.9 percentage points, so booking the earliest date you are ready is better than waiting for a "lucky" month.

What the numbers show

  • Highest pass rate: July 2025 (50.2%). Lowest: November 2025 (47.4%).
  • Busiest month: March 2026 (8,673 tests). Quietest: August 2025 (6,821).
  • The gap between best and worst month is small, so don't wait months for a better slot.
What these figures can't tell you. They don't show waiting times, weather on the day, or how busy particular routes are. In winter you may face darkness, rain or low sun, so practise in those conditions before testing. A difference of a couple of points is small.

How to pick your test date

  • Book when your instructor says you are consistently at test standard, usually after one or two mock tests.
  • Look for cancellations. Check the DVSA booking service regularly for earlier dates, and at more than one centre.
  • Allow for the weather. If you test in winter, practise in the dark and in wet weather beforehand.
  • Don't book just because a date is available. Retakes cost time and money, so be ready first.

Questions people ask

What is the best month to take a driving test in Manchester?

On 2025/26 figures, July had the highest pass rate (50.2%) and November the lowest (47.4%), but the gap was only 2.9 points, so don't hold back a test you are ready for.

Is it harder to pass a driving test in winter?

Manchester pass rates in the winter months (December to February) were between 48.4% and 49.1%, close to summer. Practise in the dark, rain and low sun so you are prepared.

How many driving tests are taken in Manchester each month?

Between 6,821 and 8,673 tests a month at the eight centres in 2025/26.
