Bury Roads We Train On

Manchester Road A56 — Bury's Main Artery

Manchester Road A56 is the primary route between Bury town centre and the M60/M66 junction at Whitefield. Lessons cover the dual-carriageway section from the Elton reservoir bridge to the Ainsworth Road junction where 50 mph gives way abruptly to 30 mph at the edge of the residential area. Traffic signals at Tesco Bury and the pedestrian crossing near the Metrolink Bury interchange on Manchester Road require careful observation — the interchange exit brings large numbers of pedestrians onto the road at Bury station. Pupils also learn to manage the right-turn into Baron Street from Manchester Road, a junction with limited sight lines due to the banking on the eastern side.

Walmersley Road — Uphill Junctions & Gradient Work

Walmersley Road climbs steeply north from Bury town centre towards Walmersley village and the B6214 Ramsbottom Road junction. In an automatic car the hill start anxiety that manual learners experience is eliminated, but the road still demands precise speed management — approaching the Walmersley Church T-junction at the top of the steeper section requires controlled braking on a downhill camber. The bus stops near Hollins Vale are tight pull-ins that block the lane when buses dwell and require a good following distance. We also use the Walmersley Golf Club entrance road and Lowe Mill Lane for three-point turn practice away from the main gradient.

Kay Gardens Roundabout & Bury Town Centre Gyratory

Kay Gardens roundabout sits at the heart of Bury town centre where Manchester Road, Rochdale Road, The Rock shopping centre approach and Silver Street all converge. The roundabout has three marked lanes on Manchester Road approach and two on Rochdale Road, and the exit onto The Rock car park approach requires an early lane choice. The Bury town centre gyratory around The Rock also features heavily in lessons — one-way sections along Market Street and Union Square require pupils to read lane arrows correctly under time pressure. Bay parking is practised in the Millgate shopping centre surface car park where both parallel and angled bay configurations are available.

Bury DVSA Mock Test — Bolton Road Circuits

Bury DVSA test centre sits on Bolton Road BL9, south of the town centre. The test circuits leave Bolton Road and head north towards the Kay Gardens roundabout, loop through Walmersley Road and the residential streets off Tottington Road, then return via Manchester Road southbound before crossing the Metrolink line on the Bolton Road approach. An alternative route extends east through Heap Bridge and Ainsworth, using the rural B-roads before returning through the Elton area. We run both circuits as fully timed mock tests so pupils experience the length, pacing and independent driving sections that the actual examiner will use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the DVSA test centre nearest to Bury?
The nearest DVSA test centre for Bury pupils is on Bolton Road BL9, south of Bury town centre. Test routes cover Walmersley Road, Manchester Road A56 and the Kay Gardens roundabout area.
Is Kay Gardens roundabout on the Bury driving test?
Yes, Kay Gardens roundabout features regularly on Bury DVSA test circuits. It has multiple entry and exit lanes and requires careful approach positioning and early lane selection.
How many automatic lessons will I need in Bury?
Most Bury pupils take 25–40 hours to pass in an automatic car. Hill starts on Walmersley Road and multi-lane roundabouts in the town centre are covered progressively throughout the course.
Do you cover Whitefield and Radcliffe from Bury?
Yes, we cover Whitefield, Radcliffe, Ramsbottom, Tottington and Elton from our Bury base. Lessons from these areas extend along the Manchester Road A56 corridor towards the Bolton Road DVSA circuit.
Are there hill starts on the Bury driving test?
Yes, Walmersley Road and several routes off Manchester Road involve noticeable gradients. The automatic gearbox removes the risk of rolling back on inclines, which is one of the main reasons many Bury pupils choose an automatic car.
Can I book a single automatic lesson in Bury first?
Yes, a single 1-hour lesson is £33 with no commitment to buy a block. Many Bury pupils start with a trial lesson to assess their starting point before moving to a 10-hour block (£330) or 20-hour intensive (£660).
