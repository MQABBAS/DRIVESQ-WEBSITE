What the University of Manchester says about getting around

  • The University's halls of residence are spread across three residential campuses: City, Fallowfield and Victoria Park.
  • Fallowfield is around 2.5km south of the main campus. Victoria Park sits between the City and Fallowfield campuses, about a 15-minute walk from the main campus, and the city centre is about 10 minutes away by bus.
  • Next to Victoria Park is Rusholme, where a section of Wilmslow Road is known as the Curry Mile.
  • The University lists a Fallowfield Campus Redevelopment: plans approved by Manchester City Council in January 2024, with first-phase works running until 2027.

A local guide for the University of Manchester learners

Most first-years live in one of three places, and each suits a different lesson routine. City-campus halls such as Whitworth Park, Weston Hall and Denmark Road put you on or near Oxford Road, so we usually collect you from the halls door or the campus and head south through Rusholme and Withington. From Fallowfield, about 2.5km south of the main campus, you learn on the same suburban roads you are later likely to be tested near. Victoria Park sits in between, about a 15-minute walk from campus, so it works well if you want a pick-up point that is easy to stop at.

Meal times matter if you live in a catered hall. The University lists Ashburne Hall, Dalton-Ellis Hall, Hulme Hall and St Anselm Hall as catered. In Fallowfield, breakfast runs from 7.30am to 9.30am and dinner from 5.15pm to 7.15pm on weekdays, so a late-afternoon or after-dinner lesson avoids clashing with the dining room. Fallowfield is also the site of the University's campus redevelopment (first-phase works until 2027), so ask your instructor to meet you on a quiet street that is easy to stop on.

Check the University's current parking rules before bringing a car. We cover where you live, not campus permits.

Questions students ask

Which University of Manchester halls are catered?

The University lists Ashburne Hall, Dalton-Ellis Hall, Hulme Hall and St Anselm Hall as its catered halls. The other halls are self-catered.

How far is Fallowfield from the main campus?

The University describes the Fallowfield campus as around 2.5km south of the main campus. Victoria Park is between the two, about a 15-minute walk from the main campus.

Where do you pick up from if I live in Fallowfield or Victoria Park?

From your halls, your house or the nearest safe stopping place. Give us your full postcode on the form and we match you with an instructor close by.

How much are lessons?

Manual and automatic both start at £35 an hour. As a student, you can book a 10-hour block for £320, paid up front.

What do I get with my booking?
