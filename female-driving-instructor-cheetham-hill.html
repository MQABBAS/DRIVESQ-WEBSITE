Learning to Drive in Cheetham Hill — A Vibrant, Diverse Area

Cheetham Hill M8 sits just north of Manchester city centre, bordered by the A665 Cheetham Hill Road running north from the inner ring road and the A56 Bury New Road heading through Prestwich and beyond. The area has one of Manchester's most diverse communities — a long-established Jewish neighbourhood in the south around the Cheetham Hill Road shops, a very large Somali community centred around Cheetham Hill Road itself, and significant South Asian families in the residential streets east of Queens Road. For many women in Cheetham Hill, having a female driving instructor is not just a preference — it's a meaningful choice, and DriveSQ fully accommodates it.

Teaching on Cheetham Hill Road progressively

Cheetham Hill Road (A665) is the area's main artery and is unavoidable for learners here. It features bus lanes, a busy pedestrianised shopping section, multiple controlled crossings and frequent lane changes — all of which feature in local test routes. Our female instructors always begin lessons in the calmer residential streets east of Cheetham Hill Road — Moston Lane, Tetlow Lane and the Queens Road grid — before progressively introducing the A665 as confidence builds. By test day, Cheetham Hill Road feels completely manageable.

Bury New Road — A-road confidence for Cheetham Hill learners

Bury New Road (A56) is another key road for Cheetham Hill M8 drivers — a busy northbound A-road with a tram line running alongside through Prestwich. Understanding the relationship between your car and the Metrolink tram on sections of Bury New Road is a specific Cheetham Hill challenge. Our instructors prepare you carefully for this, covering the tramway crossing at the Queens Road junction and the Bury New Road / Heywood Street approach that frequently comes up in test routes from this area.
