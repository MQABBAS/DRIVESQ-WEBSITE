Manchester driving test pass rates by test centre

Updated 15 November 2026 · Source: DVSA test-centre statistics, April 2025 to March 2026 (published June 2026)

Across the eight Greater Manchester test centres, learners took 92,762 practical car tests in 2025/26 and 48.8% passed. That is 1.3 points below the Great Britain figure of 50.0%. The best-performing centre was Bredbury at 56.0% and the lowest was Atherton at 44.3%, a gap of 11.6 points.

What the numbers show

  • 3 of the 8 centres were above the national rate: Bredbury, Bolton, Sale.
  • Biggest improvement: Rochdale rose 7.1 points on 2024/25, followed by Bury (+6.0). Biggest fall: Bolton, down 4.9 points.
  • Busiest centre: West Didsbury (17,806 tests). Quietest: Bury (7,024).
  • Men and women: women passed less often than men at 7 of the 8 centres. The widest gap was at Cheetham Hill (men 48.7%, women 40.5%). At Bredbury the two were within 0.2 points of each other.
Read these figures with care. A centre's pass rate is not a score for how hard its routes are. It reflects who takes tests there, how many are retakes, how many lessons people have had and many other things the figures cannot show. Year-to-year moves at a single centre can be small-number noise.

Which test centre should I book?

  • You can usually choose. You can book at any DVSA centre, not just the nearest. Check the earliest dates at the centres within reach, because waiting times can differ.
  • Practise the area you will be tested in. Ask your instructor to take you on the roads around your chosen centre in the weeks before the test. Familiarity with the local junctions and speed limits matters more than the centre's average.
  • Book when you are ready, not when you are anxious to book. A mock test with your instructor shows whether you are consistently at test standard.

Questions people ask

Which Manchester driving test centre has the highest pass rate?

In 2025/26 the highest pass rate among the Manchester centres in the DVSA tables was Bredbury at 56.0%, against 50.0% nationally. The lowest was Atherton at 44.3%.

Is it easier to pass at a test centre with a higher pass rate?

Not necessarily. Pass rates show what happened to the people who took tests there. They don't measure route difficulty, and they are affected by who books there and how prepared they are.

How many driving test centres are there in Manchester?

DVSA's 2025/26 tables list eight centres in the Manchester group: Atherton, Bolton, Bredbury, Bury, Cheetham Hill, Rochdale, Sale and West Didsbury.

How many people pass their driving test first time in Manchester?

Across the eight centres, first-attempt pass rates ran from 44.1% to 57.9% in 2025/26. The Great Britain first-attempt rate was 50.5%.
