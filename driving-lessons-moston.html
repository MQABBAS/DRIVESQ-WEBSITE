Moston Lane / Kenyon Lane — The Junction No Competitor Mentions

The staggered T-junction where Kenyon Lane meets Moston Lane is one of the most misread junctions on Manchester’s test network — and it regularly catches learners off guard. When emerging from Kenyon Lane, the road bends sharply to meet Moston Lane at an oblique angle. The give-way line is set back further than most learners expect, which means you must look right over a longer distance than the angle of the junction suggests.

Compounding this: parked vans on Moston Lane — common throughout the day — reduce the visibility of approaching vehicles from the right. Learners who do not account for the extra sweep distance may pull out before they have a clear view of oncoming traffic.

What DriveSQ does: Every Moston learner practises this junction specifically. We approach it from Kenyon Lane multiple times during the lesson programme, teaching you to identify the set-back give-way line, perform a full right-look sweep, and assess gaps around parked vehicles before emerging. This junction appears on Manchester test routes, and our instructors have mapped exactly how examiners evaluate it.

Key Roads and Junctions Around Moston

Rochdale Road A664 is the main arterial spine connecting Moston to Manchester city centre and north toward Rochdale. Learners must manage multi-lane approaches, traffic signal timing, and frequent bus movements throughout the day. The road shifts between 30mph and 40mph sections, demanding constant speed awareness rather than a set-and-forget approach.

Moston Lane runs east to west through the heart of the district. Its junction with Kenyon Lane is the most technically demanding junction in the area — a staggered T-junction where the road bends, the give-way line sits further back than expected, and parked vans regularly obscure the right-look sightline. Every DriveSQ Moston lesson plan includes specific work at this junction.
