Northenden Local Area Guide

Lessons around Northenden use real local roads including Hollyhedge Road, Boat Lane and Ford Lane, so by the time you're ready for your test you've already driven the streets you'll use every day after passing. Northenden's River Mersey crossing was once a ford so awkwardly aligned that travellers had to wade roughly 500 feet sideways along the riverbed — it was replaced in 1901 by the iron Simon's Bridge, funded by engineer and local benefactor Henry Simon.

We also plan around school-run traffic near Button Lane Primary School and St Wilfrid's RC Primary School, using quieter spots like Simon's Bridge over the River Mersey for early manoeuvre practice before stepping up to busier sections of Hollyhedge Road.

Test centre: most learners around Northenden test at West Didsbury Driving Test Centre, Unit 11, Christie Park, West Didsbury, M21 7QY (some candidates also use Sale Driving Test Centre); mock tests are planned around the routes examiners actually use from there.
