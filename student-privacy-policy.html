Privacy Policy

DriveSQ Student App
Last updated: 4 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DriveSQ ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the DriveSQ Student App (available on Google Play and at drivesq.co.uk).

1. Who We Are

DriveSQ is a DVSA-approved driving school based in Greater Manchester, UK. Contact us at: info@drivesq.co.uk

2. Information We Collect

When you use the DriveSQ Student App, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (provided when you register or enquire)
  • Lesson booking information (dates, times, lesson type)
  • Progress and test readiness information (as recorded by your instructor)
  • Payment information (processed securely — we do not store card details)
  • Device information (operating system, app version) for technical support
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used) to improve the app

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Manage your lesson bookings and scheduling
  • Track your learning progress and provide feedback
  • Send you reminders and notifications about upcoming lessons
  • Process payments for lessons and packages
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve the app and our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: to provide the driving lesson services you have booked
  • Legitimate interests: to improve our services and communicate relevant updates
  • Consent: for marketing communications (you can opt out at any time)
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws and regulations

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Your assigned DriveSQ instructor — to coordinate your lessons
  • Payment processors — to securely process lesson payments
  • Cloud service providers (e.g. Supabase) — for secure data storage
  • Legal authorities — if required by law

All third parties are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your data within 90 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@drivesq.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The DriveSQ Student App may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on encrypted servers.

10. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for users aged 17 and over (the minimum age to apply for a UK provisional driving licence). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 17 without parental consent.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact:

DriveSQ
Greater Manchester, UK
Email: info@drivesq.co.uk
Website: drivesq.co.uk

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Everything You Need to Pass — Manchester Driving Guide

DriveSQ students in Manchester (M1) don't just get lessons — they get a complete learning ecosystem built around passing first time at Manchester City Driving Test Centre.

Driving in Manchester — Local Knowledge

Manchester city centre has one of the UK's most complex one-way systems, with bus-only lanes and tram crossings requiring specific knowledge. DriveSQ instructors in Manchester cover Oxford Road and Deansgate in every lesson plan, ensuring students develop genuine local road confidence before test day. Your instructor's knowledge of M1 roads, pedestrian patterns, and local junction behaviour gives you a measurable advantage over learners who have simply followed a generic syllabus. The Trafford Centre, opened in 1998, is the UK's second largest indoor shopping centre with over 35 million annual visitors. Its road network — Trafford Way and the A56 — is one of Greater Manchester's busiest non-motorway driving environments.

The DriveSQ Student App — Your 24/7 Learning Companion

Every DriveSQ student in Manchester gets full access to the DriveSQ student app from the moment they book — at no extra cost. The app works on any device (phone, tablet, laptop) with no download required. It connects directly to your instructor's dashboard, so your progress updates in real time after every lesson. Here are the features our M1 students use most:

Hazard Perception Tips

The DriveSQ student app includes a dedicated hazard perception coaching section with tips drawn from real DVSA test data. Learn exactly when to click, how to identify developing hazards versus static ones, and how to avoid the most common scoring mistakes. Hazard perception is one of the most underestimated parts of the theory test — the app gives it the attention it deserves.

Daily Streak Tracker

The DriveSQ app rewards consistent study with a daily login streak counter. Every day you engage with the app — whether completing a theory mock, reviewing topics, or checking your progress — extends your streak. Research shows spaced repetition and daily engagement significantly improves theory test scores.

Progress Tracker

Your DriveSQ student dashboard tracks every lesson you complete, showing total hours done, topics covered, and an automatically calculated test readiness score. Your instructor updates your progress after each lesson — you see exactly what you have mastered and what still needs work, so every lesson has a clear purpose.

Theory Test Guide & Mock Tests

Contraflow bus lanes operate in the opposite direction to normal traffic. Red lines on the road edge and blue circular signs indicate mandatory bus-only lanes. DriveSQ students learn to identify these in urban Manchester environments during lessons. The DriveSQ student app includes a complete theory mock test engine with DVSA-style questions, instant scoring, and detailed answer explanations. Students can run unlimited mock tests before their theory test date — the app tracks scores over time so improvement is clearly visible.

The theory guide section covers all 14 subject areas assessed by the DVSA: alertness, attitude, safety and your vehicle, safety margins, hazard awareness, vulnerable road users, other types of vehicle, vehicle handling, motorway rules, rules of the road, road and traffic signs, documents, accidents, vehicle loading, and environmental responsibility.

Practical Driving Test Guide

A candidate can request their test in Welsh at any DVSA test centre in Wales. In England, tests are conducted in English only, though written guidance is available in multiple languages. DriveSQ serves learners from diverse backgrounds across Greater Manchester. DriveSQ's practical guide is built into the student app and covers every element of the Manchester City test routes. From the moment you sit in the car with the examiner to the independent driving section and final manoeuvre — the guide prepares you for exactly what to expect, eliminating surprises on test day.

The guide includes step-by-step instructions for all three possible manoeuvres (reverse bay parking, parallel parking, pulling up on the right), the Show Me Tell Me vehicle safety questions, emergency stop procedure, and independent driving using both sat-nav and road signs — so whichever combination your examiner chooses, you are ready.
