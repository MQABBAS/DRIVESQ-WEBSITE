Common Questions

Honest Answers

We'd rather tell you the truth than tell you what sounds good. WhatsApp us if anything's not covered here.

Are female instructors always available immediately? +

Honestly — usually, but not always. Availability depends on your area and current bookings. In most parts of Greater Manchester we can confirm a female ADI quickly. If there's a short wait, we'll tell you straight away and offer you the choice of waiting or starting with a highly-rated male ADI. We never pressure you either way.

Is there an extra charge for a female instructor? +

No. Female instructors cost exactly the same as male instructors. It's £35/hr, £15 for a first lesson, £299 for 10 lessons — regardless of who teaches you. No surcharge, ever.

Can I switch instructors if I'm unhappy? +

Yes, absolutely. If at any point you'd like to change your instructor — for any reason or no reason at all — just let us know. No questions asked, no awkward conversations with your previous instructor. We'll sort it quietly and quickly.

Are female instructors as qualified as male instructors? +

Yes. The DVSA ADI qualification is identical regardless of gender. Every DriveSQ instructor — male or female — holds a full green ADI badge issued by the DVSA. The teaching standards, test knowledge and professional conduct are the same across the board.

What if there's no female instructor available in my area? +

We'll be upfront about it. Your options are: wait a short period for a female ADI to become available in your area, travel slightly further to meet an available female ADI, or begin lessons with a highly-rated male ADI and switch if a female ADI becomes available locally. We'll present the options and let you decide — no pressure, no upselling.

Do you offer female instructors for nervous drivers? +

Yes — and it's one of the most common reasons people request a female ADI. Driving anxiety is real and very common. Choosing the instructor dynamic that feels calmest for you is a practical decision, not a vanity one. All DriveSQ instructors are trained to support nervous learners, and many learners find that starting with someone they feel immediately comfortable with makes a measurable difference to how quickly their confidence builds.

Can parents request a female instructor for their child? +

Absolutely. Many parents get in touch on behalf of their son or daughter with a preference for a female ADI. Just mention it when you contact us and we'll confirm availability in your area.
