Why choose a female instructor

Learning to Drive in Longsight — Comfort Drives Confidence

Longsight is one of Manchester's most culturally rich communities — a tight-knit M13 neighbourhood centred on Stockport Road, with a large South Asian, Somali and East African population. Many learners here prefer a female instructor for personal, religious or cultural reasons, and at DriveSQ those preferences are honoured without question or comment.

Religious and cultural comfort

For many Longsight learners — especially those from Muslim, South Asian or East African backgrounds — sharing an enclosed space with a male stranger for extended periods can feel uncomfortable regardless of his professionalism. Our female instructors understand this completely. Hijab, niqab and all religious dress are welcomed without remark, and lessons are adapted to your cultural comfort without any need to explain yourself.

Safety and confidence in the car

Driving lessons require physical closeness and repeated correction — a female instructor removes the discomfort that some learners feel in that dynamic with a male instructor. Many of our Longsight students book their first lesson only once a female instructor becomes available, and the results show: learners who feel safe and comfortable in the car progress faster and pass sooner.

Open communication from the first lesson

Telling an instructor you're nervous, that you don't understand a manoeuvre, or that you need something explained differently is much easier when you feel comfortable. Our female instructors in Longsight are specifically praised in reviews for their patience on Stockport Road — a stretch of road that genuinely challenges new drivers — and their willingness to adapt the lesson structure to the learner's pace.

Theory test preparation

Apps & Tools to Pass Faster

Your female instructor covers practical skills — these apps will help you pass the theory test alongside your lessons.

DVSA Theory Test Kit (Official)

The only officially licensed theory test app from the DVSA. Contains all 900+ questions in the live question bank — the same questions you face on test day. Also includes hazard perception clips filmed by the DVSA itself. Free on iOS and Android. If you only install one app, make it this one.

Theory Test Pro

Theory Test Pro organises revision by topic — road signs, road markings, vulnerable road users, motorway rules and more. Its spaced-repetition system focuses your revision time on questions you keep getting wrong, rather than recycling questions you already know. Available as browser and mobile apps; pairs well with the official DVSA app.

Hazard Perception Practice Apps

Driving Test Success and the DVSA's own hazard perception clips help you practise spotting developing hazards in video — the most commonly failed section of the theory test for M13-area learners. The test uses 14 clips with 15 scored hazards; aim to score consistently above 44 out of 75 before booking your test date.

The Highway Code (GOV.UK)

The free, always-current Highway Code on GOV.UK is the legal foundation behind every theory question. Reading it section by section — especially road markings, priority rules, pedestrian crossings and national speed limits — gives your answers the context that question apps alone cannot. Your instructor will highlight the chapters most often tested at Whalley Range.

Mock Test Tracking with DriveSQ

Every DriveSQ instructor logs your progress and tracks mock test scores lesson by lesson. Before your practical test, you'll complete a full 40-minute mock test with written fault feedback. Learners on 10-hour and 20-hour packages receive at least one structured mock, and those on 40-hour complete courses receive two full mocks as standard.
