A local guide for Manchester Met learners

Manchester Met has a wider spread of accommodation than most universities, with 19 halls across its own blocks and partner providers. Ten are run by the university itself, including the five Birley halls (Dale, Dunham, Naylor, Vine and Warde), Archway, Briarfield, Cavendish, Needham Court and Oxford Court. The other nine are partner halls run by Unite Students, IQ Students, Vita Students and Mezzino. If you are in one of the 12-person Birley houses (Dale, Naylor or Warde), agree one clear pick-up point with your instructor so you are not hunting for the right door.

Distance is where a licence earns its keep. Most halls list journeys of under 15 minutes to the Business School and Brooks Building, but Brook Hall, Chandos House, Ropemaker Court and Wilmslow Park each list at least one journey of 20 minutes or more. Students in those halls have the most to gain from learning early, because a longer commute is the journey a car shortens.
