Ardwick’s Unique Hazard: Ardwick Green Roundabout (Hyde Road A57)

The Ardwick Green / Hyde Road A57 junction is a busy multi-arm roundabout where the exits are unevenly spaced and signage is partially obscured by mature trees lining the green. This combination catches out learners who rely on overhead signs rather than road markings to select the correct exit lane.

The road markings themselves fade quickly — heavy goods vehicles heading for the M60 grind through the painted arrows on a daily basis, leaving many approach lanes ambiguous weeks after resurfacing. A learner committing to the wrong exit lane here will receive a serious or dangerous fault on test day.

The A57 also carries a consistently high volume of articulated lorries, creating an additional pressure on the approach: learners must hold their chosen lane confidently rather than drifting in response to large vehicles alongside them.

How we teach it: Our instructors approach Ardwick Green from all four entry roads across multiple lessons, building a mental map of each approach before road markings are visible. We teach lane commitment early — before the markings fade — so you always know exactly which lane to choose and why.
