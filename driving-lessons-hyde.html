A fast dual-carriageway approach road with extended national speed limit sections beyond the Hyde boundary. Learners practise lane discipline, overtaking position, and approach-road speed management on this key examiner route.

Corporation Street — School Zones

Corporation Street passes school frontages with time-restricted parking, zig-zag markings, and pedestrian crossings that operate during busy drop-off and pick-up periods. An essential road for developing school-zone awareness.
