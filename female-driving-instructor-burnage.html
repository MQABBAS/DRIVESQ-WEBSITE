Local Road Knowledge

Roads We Teach in Burnage M19

Every lesson is rooted in Burnage roads — not generic content. You'll know every junction, crossing and speed change before your test day.

Mauldeth Road – Speed Limits, School Zone and Junction Discipline

Mauldeth Road is Burnage's primary east-west route and carries heavy local traffic between Withington, Levenshulme and Heaton Chapel. The speed limit is 30 mph throughout, but the road's width and relatively straight alignment tempt drivers to creep above this — something examiners are alert to near the junctions with Burnage Lane and Fog Lane. Mauldeth Road passes Burnage High School for Boys, where morning and afternoon traffic creates significant pedestrian hazard: students cross informally between the gates and adjacent streets, requiring early observation and smooth speed reduction well in advance of the formal crossing. The road terminates at a signal-controlled crossroads with Didsbury Road B5093, which features a right-turn filter used regularly on test routes from Wythenshawe DVSA. We practise both the signal sequence and the position needed to complete the filter safely.

Kingsway A34 – Dual Carriageway Confidence and Safe Emerging

The Kingsway A34 runs north-south along the western edge of Burnage and forms one of south Manchester's busiest dual carriageways. For learners, the key skills here are matching speed before joining from Parrs Wood Road or Mauldeth Road, maintaining appropriate following distance at 40 mph, and selecting the correct lane when approaching the major junctions at Fog Lane and Barlow Moor Road. The Kingsway slip road emerging from Burnage onto the northbound carriageway has limited sightlines to the right — a real-world challenge that goes well beyond what quiet residential streets can teach. We include the Kingsway section in lessons once a learner has solid control at 30 mph, building up progressively so higher speeds feel controlled rather than stressful.

Burnage Lane – Priority Junctions and Residential Observation

Burnage Lane runs the full length of the area from Mauldeth Road south to Fog Lane, threading through a residential grid of 1930s terraces with vehicles parked on both sides. Sightlines at the side junctions — particularly the junction with Stovell Avenue and the crossing at Green End Road — are restricted by parked cars, requiring slow approach speeds and a two-stage look before committing. Priority on several sections of Burnage Lane is informal rather than signed, meaning the learner must read road width and give way instinctively. We also use the quieter parts of Burnage Lane for parallel parking exercises — the road's consistent parking demand makes finding a realistic space straightforward, and the repetition builds the accuracy needed for the test version of the manoeuvre.

Fog Lane and Didsbury Road – Crossroads and Independent Navigation

Fog Lane connects Burnage to Didsbury and Withington and forms part of the independent driving section in many DVSA test routes from this area. The road is 30 mph with a zebra crossing near the Burnage / Didsbury boundary that requires timely response rather than emergency braking — a distinction examiners note. The junction at Fog Lane and Burnage Lane is a four-arm crossroads without signal control: the learner must read road markings, assess traffic from all approaches, and move decisively when safe. Didsbury Road B5093 at the east end of Fog Lane broadens to a two-lane approach at the signal junction and requires correct lane selection for turning movements. This entire corridor is frequently used in the independent driving section, so we cover it in detail during the mock test preparation stages of your lessons.

Test Centre Info

Test Centres for Burnage Learners

Burnage learners most commonly test at Wythenshawe DVSA (Simonsway M22) or Stretford DVSA (Lacy Street M32), both 15–20 minutes from Burnage. Test routes from both centres include the Kingsway A34, Mauldeth Road and Fog Lane sections that form the backbone of your lessons.

Your pre-test lesson leaves from your Burnage address, covers the most common route variants, and ends at the test centre — included within your lesson time, not an extra charge.

On your practical test

  • Eyesight check at the test centre before departing
  • Show-me / tell-me vehicle safety questions
  • Around 40 minutes of driving including independent section
  • Independent driving — typically 20 minutes, sat-nav or road signs
  • One or two manoeuvres chosen by the examiner
