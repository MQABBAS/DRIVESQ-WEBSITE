Learning to Drive in Great Moor

Great Moor is a settled SK2 suburb between Offerton to the north and Hazel Grove to the south, with Stepping Hill Hospital just to the east and the A6 Buxton Road running through its western edge. For learner drivers, this creates an excellent progression: wide, quiet streets for early lessons, then a natural build-up through Dialstone Lane to the A6 and eventually the Torkington roundabout at the Hazel Grove boundary — a roundabout that appears on multiple Stockport test routes.

Dialstone Lane — the Great Moor connector

Dialstone Lane links Great Moor to Offerton and carries a mix of residential and through traffic. It's a reliable road for practising junction approaches, signalling discipline and positioning — neither so busy that it overwhelms beginners nor so quiet that it fails to teach anything. We use it throughout the lesson journey from early intermediate onwards.

A6 Buxton Road — building speed confidence

The A6 Buxton Road runs along the western side of Great Moor and continues south toward Hazel Grove and Disley. It's a national-speed-limit road in places and carries significant through traffic. Joining it confidently, maintaining appropriate speed and exiting safely are all covered as soon as a learner is ready — typically from lesson six or seven onwards.

Torkington roundabout — a landmark challenge

The Torkington roundabout at the southern tip of Great Moor where the A6 meets the A523 is one of the larger roundabouts on the Stockport test network. Multiple entry points, variable speed approaches and the need to read other drivers' intentions all come together here. We dedicate lesson time to approaching it from every direction so there are no surprises on the day.

Quiet residential streets for first lessons

The streets around Great Moor Street, Norbury Street and the cul-de-sacs off Dialstone Lane provide a genuinely calm first-lesson environment. Bay reversals, turn-in-the-road and the basics of clutch and brake control all happen here before any main road is attempted.
