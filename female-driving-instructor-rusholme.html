Theory test preparation

Apps & Tools to Pass Faster

Your female instructor covers practical skills — these apps help you nail the theory test alongside your lessons.

DVSA Theory Test Kit (Official)

The only officially licensed theory test app from the DVSA. Contains all 900+ questions from the live question bank, plus hazard perception clips filmed by the DVSA team. Free on iOS and Android. This is the only app we recommend if you only install one — the questions are identical to your actual test.

Theory Test Pro

A popular supplement to the official app, Theory Test Pro organises questions by topic (road signs, rules of the road, vulnerable road users, etc.) and tracks your weak areas. The spaced-repetition feature means you spend more time on the questions you keep getting wrong. Available as a browser app and mobile app.

DriveSQ WhatsApp Booking

Message us on WhatsApp at 07352 932003 to book lessons, reschedule, or ask your instructor a question between sessions. Most learners find it the fastest way to stay in contact. Send your postcode, preferred transmission and available times — we typically reply within the hour.

Hazard Perception Practice Apps

The hazard perception test catches many learners who focus too heavily on the multiple-choice section. Apps like Driving Test Success and the DVSA Hazard Perception app let you practise the 14-clip format (one developing hazard per clip, except one clip with two). Aim to score above 44/75 consistently before your test date.

The Highway Code (GOV.UK)

The free, always-updated version of the Highway Code is available on GOV.UK and as a PDF. Many learners overlook it in favour of question apps, but reading the actual rules — particularly the sections on signals, road markings, priority and motorway driving — gives your theory answers context that pure question practice doesn't. Your instructor will point out which sections are most heavily tested.

Mock Test Tracking with DriveSQ

Every DriveSQ instructor tracks your lesson progress and mock test scores in a simple shared format — no app required. Before your practical test, you'll do at least one full 40-minute mock test with written feedback on faults. Many learners in our 10-hour and 20-hour packages have access to two mock tests built into the package price.
