⚠️ Local Hazard: Carrington Road Speed Limit Transitions

Carrington Road B5160 transitions between 30 mph, 40 mph, and national speed limit zones within a relatively short stretch. The signs are sometimes obscured by seasonal vegetation, and the road environment doesn't always signal the change intuitively. Many learners either creep below the limit on open sections or fail to reduce speed quickly enough coming back into the 30 zone. We dedicate specific lesson time to reading advance warning signs and matching speed early — before the limit sign, not after it.
