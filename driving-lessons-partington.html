⚠️ Partington-specific hazard: Moss Lane rural give-way blind bends

Moss Lane across Carrington Moss has several blind bends where the road narrows to a single track — give-way situations at these points require reading of approaching vehicle headlights before the vehicle is visible. DriveSQ instructors introduce Moss Lane carefully, with a specific briefing on anticipating hidden traffic at bends on rural roads.
