📖 Portal Guide

Add lessons by clicking any day slot on the calendar — choose a saved student or enter details manually. Drag and drop lessons to reschedule them instantly.
  • Add: Tap a date slot → fill in student, time, type → Save
  • Edit/Reschedule: Tap a lesson → edit date, time or type → Save
  • Confirm: You and your student both confirm attendance in-app
  • Delete: Open the lesson → tap the delete icon

The Students tab shows all your saved students. Tap any student card to open their full profile, view progress, topics, messages and theory results.
  • View lesson count, hours and progress status at a glance
  • Share the student portal link via WhatsApp from the App Login tab
  • Archive students who have passed or paused — keeps their history intact
  • Use the Theory tab to see mock test scores and practical hours

In-app messages go directly to your student's portal and trigger a push notification. WhatsApp links open a pre-filled message in WhatsApp — you still tap Send.
  • Chat tab inside a student's card → sends in-app message
  • WhatsApp button → opens WA with a pre-filled greeting
  • After rescheduling a lesson, you'll get a quick WA link to notify the student

The stats at the top of your dashboard show your weekly hours, total students, lessons this week and earnings. Update each student's practical hours and test readiness in their Progress tab.
  • Set hours done and target → student sees their progress bar in the app
  • Set test readiness (Not Ready / Nearly There / Ready) — visible to student
  • Rate driving topics (0–3) in the Topics tab — syncs to student app

Direct students to drivesq.co.uk/student — they log in with their phone number. No download required; it works on any device and can be installed as an app.
  • Use the "Send Portal Link via WA" button (App Login tab) to share the link
  • Students can view lessons, confirm bookings, study theory (700 questions + mocks)
  • Their mock test results and practical hours appear in your dashboard's Theory tab
