Apps & Tools to Pass Faster

Your female instructor handles the practical road skills — these apps run alongside your lessons and help you clear the theory test.

DVSA Theory Test Kit (Official)

The DVSA's own licensed app — the only one that contains the full, current question bank used in your actual theory test. Includes all 900+ multiple-choice questions and the hazard perception clips filmed by DVSA. Free on iOS and Android. Many Fallowfield students use it on their phones during commutes — even short daily sessions add up quickly.

Theory Test Pro

Theory Test Pro adds topic-based organisation and spaced repetition to your revision — it tracks which questions you keep getting wrong and surfaces them more frequently. Useful for learners who find the DVSA app too unstructured. Available on mobile and browser, so it works alongside the official app rather than replacing it.

Hazard Perception Practice Apps

Hazard perception is the part of the theory test that catches the most learners who don't specifically practise it. The DVSA app and Driving Test Success both include practice clips in the correct 14-clip format. You're scored on how quickly you spot the developing hazard — aim to score above 44/75 consistently before your test date.

The Highway Code (GOV.UK)

Reading the Highway Code on GOV.UK gives the theory questions context that question apps cannot. The sections on pedestrian crossings, lane discipline and national speed limits are heavily tested and are easiest to understand when you read the actual rules rather than just practise the answers. Free, always current, available online and as a PDF download.
