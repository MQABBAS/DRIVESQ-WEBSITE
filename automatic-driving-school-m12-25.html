Why choose an automatic driving school in M12?

Automatic cars remove clutch and gearbox management, letting you focus entirely on road awareness, observation, and decision-making. Many learners in South Manchester find they progress faster in automatics — and once you've passed, you can upgrade to manual anytime with a relatively short course.

How much do automatic driving lessons cost in M12?

DriveSQ automatic lessons in M12 start from £33/hr. Block bookings of 5 or 10 hours are available at a reduced rate. The automatic licence is slightly less versatile than manual (you can't drive manual cars), but lessons are often shorter because learners progress faster.

Can I take my driving test in an automatic car?

Yes. You take the practical test in the same type of car you've been learning in. If you pass in an automatic, your licence is restricted to automatics. If you later want to drive manual, you take an additional practical test in a manual car.

How quickly can I learn automatic in South Manchester?

The DVSA average is 45 hours before a first test attempt. Many automatic learners in South Manchester reach test standard faster because there's less to master. DriveSQ structures lessons for efficient progress — clear targets each drive, mock tests throughout, and honest feedback on readiness.
