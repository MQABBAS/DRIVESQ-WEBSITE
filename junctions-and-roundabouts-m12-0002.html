FAQs — Junctions & Roundabouts in M12

What are the rules for emerging at junctions?

At a junction, apply MSPSL: check mirrors, signal if turning, take the correct position (left for left turns, right of your lane for right turns), reduce speed appropriately, then look carefully before emerging. At Give Way junctions, wait until there's a safe gap. At Stop junctions, stop completely regardless of visibility.

How do I know who has priority at a roundabout?

At roundabouts, traffic already on the roundabout has priority. You must Give Way to the right. Enter only when there is a safe gap. At multi-lane roundabouts, choose your lane on approach based on your exit: left or straight ahead uses the left lane; exits to the right use the right lane.

What are the most common junction faults on the test?

The most common junction faults are: emerging without adequate observation (too fast or without looking), incorrect approach position (too far right when turning left), inappropriate speed on approach (too fast or too slow), and not giving way to pedestrians already crossing when turning.
