Instructor Package Terms

Terms and conditions for DriveSQ weekly packages: what you pay, how many students we guarantee, and what happens if we fall short.

Version 1 · applies from the first full Monday after your package starts

These terms apply to every DriveSQ instructor on a Silver, Gold or Platinum package (“you”). “DriveSQ”, “we” and “us” means DriveSQ Driving School. They sit alongside your franchise agreement. If the two ever disagree, your franchise agreement applies.

The fee and number of students for your package are as agreed with you in writing or by message. If yours differ from the table, your agreed figures apply, and the same rules below are used with those figures.

We may change the number of guaranteed students or the fee for a package by giving you at least 4 weeks' written notice. Changes never apply to a week that has already started.

2. How a week works

  • A week runs Monday to Sunday.
  • We start measuring on the first full Monday after your package begins. Any part-week before that is not measured.
  • Each student counts once, in the week we first refer them to you.

3. What counts as a delivered student

A student counts towards the guarantee if either of these is true:

  1. they book a first lesson with you, or
  2. we referred them to you and you did not contact them within 24 hours, or did not follow up after they replied.

This second rule means a lead you ignored is still counted as delivered, so you cannot claim a discount for a student you did not follow up.

A student does not count if they were not referred by DriveSQ, if we cancelled them before they could be contacted, or if they duplicate a student already counted.

4. If we do not deliver enough students

If we deliver fewer students than the guarantee in a week, your fee for that week is reduced. Your fee never falls below £40 for the week.

These amounts are rounded to the nearest £5 to keep them simple. The discount is applied to that week's fee, or credited against your next fee. It is never paid as cash.

5. When no discount applies

  • Weeks when you told us you were unavailable, on holiday, or could not take new students.
  • Students you declined, or did not respond to, as set out in section 3.
  • Weeks when your account was suspended or you were in breach of your franchise agreement.

We decide each week's discount by checking our records against these terms. If you think a week has been counted wrongly, tell us and we will review it with you.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Contact every student we refer to you promptly and professionally, within 24 hours.
  • Keep your availability and student statuses up to date in the Instructor Portal.
  • Pay your weekly fee on time. A discount reduces the fee, it does not delay it.

7. Reviews and ending a package

  • We review every package each month. If a package keeps falling short or you keep missing leads, we may change the number of students, the fee, or end the package.
  • You can leave a package at any time by giving 4 weeks' notice. After that you can move to the Normal package or leave DriveSQ, as agreed.

8. General

  • We may update these terms by telling you in writing. Updated terms apply from the next full week after we tell you.
  • Nothing here makes you an employee of DriveSQ. You remain a self-employed driving instructor responsible for your own tax, insurance and ADI registration.
