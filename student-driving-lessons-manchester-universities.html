Why learn to drive while you study

  • Your time is flexible. You can book lessons around your timetable, and tell us when you are free on the booking form.
  • It widens your options. A licence can open up placements, jobs with early or late shifts, and trips home.
  • Your data is on your side. See the DVSA figures for Manchester: 17 to 25 year olds' pass rates and pass rates at every test centre.

Questions students ask

Which Manchester universities and colleges do you cover?

We cover the University of Manchester, Manchester Metropolitan University, the University of Salford, the University of Greater Manchester (formerly the University of Bolton) and the colleges listed on this page, and we teach students from any other institution too. Enter your pick-up postcode on the form.

Do you offer a student discount?

We offer a new learner deal: your first 10 hours for £330, paid in advance, for manual or automatic lessons. Lessons are from £35 an hour for everyone.

Can you pick me up from halls or campus?

Yes. Tell us your pick-up postcode and whether you want to be collected from your halls, home or campus.

Is the Student Portal free?

Yes. It is free for every DriveSQ learner and includes 700+ theory questions, 14 mock tests and hazard perception training.
