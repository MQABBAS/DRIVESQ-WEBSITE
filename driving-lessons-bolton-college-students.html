A local guide for Bolton College learners

Bolton College's main site is the Deane Road Campus (BL3 5BG), and it also runs the Deane & Derby Community Learning Centre. The University of Greater Manchester is on the same road (BL3 5AB), so Deane Road is the heart of Bolton's student area. Reception is open Monday to Thursday from 8.15am to 7.30pm and on Friday until 4pm, which helps if you are fitting a visit around work.

Bolton has its own DVSA test centre in the Manchester group. In 2025/26 it had a 51.8% pass rate, above the Great Britain figure of 50.0%, although it fell 4.9 points on the year before. As with every centre, that reflects who takes tests there, not how hard the routes are. Your instructor can plan lessons on Bolton roads so the junctions and speed limits feel familiar by test day.
