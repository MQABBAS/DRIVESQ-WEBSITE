⚠️ Eccles-specific hazard: The Church Street gyratory exit onto Liverpool Road A57

The Eccles town centre one-way system runs clockwise around Church Street and Regent Street. The critical danger point is the exit from Regent Street onto Liverpool Road (A57): the exit lane is unmarked at the point of entry from the gyratory, meaning learners must anticipate and commit to the correct lane position well before the exit sign comes into view. Arriving in the wrong lane at the point the sign finally appears leaves no room to correct without a serious lane discipline fault — and multiple test failures in Eccles are attributed to exactly this moment. DriveSQ instructors walk every Eccles student through this sequence specifically — both directions — before test day. No competitor page covers the Eccles one-way exit hazard.
