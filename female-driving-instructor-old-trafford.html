Local Road Knowledge

Roads We Teach in Old Trafford M16

Lessons are anchored in actual Old Trafford roads — not generic Manchester content. Every junction we teach appears on or near Stretford DVSA test routes.

Talbot Road – Stadium Area, Bus Lanes and Event-Day Awareness

Talbot Road runs past Old Trafford stadium and the Emirates Old Trafford cricket ground and is one of the most recognisable roads in M16. Outside match days it carries steady local traffic at 30 mph with bus stops, pedestrian crossings and junctions to the residential grid on either side. Learners practise reading bus stop hazards — a bus indicating means checking for passengers who may step into the road before the doors have closed — and positioning correctly at the signal-controlled crossing near the stadium access road. The road also contains a section with a raised cycle lane marking that new learners sometimes mistake for the driving lane; understanding road layout from markings rather than assumption is a skill developed here that applies across many test routes. The junction with Seymour Grove at the east end of Talbot Road is a busy multi-arm junction covered in detail because it appears frequently on Stretford DVSA independent driving sections.

Stretford Road – Tram Crossing, Priority and Urban Complexity

Stretford Road carries the Metrolink Eccles line as it crosses from Old Trafford toward Trafford Bar and the city centre. The tram-crossing give-way procedure on Stretford Road is practised as a distinct drill: observe the signal sequence, stop before the give-way line when amber light shows, wait for green, check left and right for trams running late, then proceed. This two-stage check is not the same as a traffic-light junction and many learners conflate them — an error that can cause a serious fault. Beyond the tram crossing the road has a one-way section near the junction with Ayres Road that requires early lane selection from the correct approach — something easily missed without knowledge of the local layout. We cover this section in both directions so learners can approach from Trafford Bar or from the M16 residential grid without hesitation.

Chester Road A56 – Metrolink, Bus Gate and Lane Discipline

Chester Road A56 is the primary north-south corridor through Old Trafford, carrying Metrolink trams on the Altrincham line, a designated bus lane during peak hours, and multiple lanes of private traffic. The bus lane operates 7–10am and 4–7pm on weekdays — learners must know both the times and the physical boundary of the lane to avoid driving in it during restricted hours. The tram stops at Trafford Bar and Old Trafford stations interrupt the traffic flow at irregular intervals, and the crossing sequences differ slightly from standard traffic signals. The junction with Seymour Grove / Stretford Road is the busiest point on Chester Road in the M16 area and is used on Stretford DVSA test routes — we cover all approach angles, including the right-turn from Chester Road into Seymour Grove, which requires patience and correct position in the turning area while oncoming traffic clears.

Seymour Grove and Edge Lane – Residential Grid and Manoeuvres

Seymour Grove runs west from Chester Road through the residential centre of Old Trafford and connects to Edge Lane toward Chorlton. The road is 30 mph with compact residential parking on both sides — parallel parking here requires accurate judgement of gap size and a willingness to take multiple moves to achieve the correct position rather than abandoning a gap that is workable. Edge Lane provides quieter conditions for three-point turns and bay parking exercises using the Stretford Mall car park approach near the M32 boundary. We also use the grid of residential streets including Great Stone Road and Kings Road for independent driving exercises where learners navigate from a start point to a destination using sat-nav only — good preparation for the independent driving section of the Stretford DVSA test.

Test Centre Info

Stretford DVSA Test Centre

Old Trafford learners sit their practical test at Stretford DVSA Test Centre, Lacy Street M32 9BJ — approximately 10 minutes from Old Trafford via Chester Road. Test routes from Stretford include Chester Road, Seymour Grove and Talbot Road — all covered in depth during your lessons.

Your pre-test lesson departs from your Old Trafford address and ends at Lacy Street within your lesson hour.

On your practical test

  • Eyesight check at the test centre
  • Show-me / tell-me vehicle safety questions
  • Around 40 minutes of real-road driving
  • 20-minute independent section — sat-nav or road signs
  • One or two manoeuvres chosen by the examiner
