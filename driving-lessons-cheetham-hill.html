8 Key Roads We Cover in Cheetham Hill

Every lesson is built around roads you will actually drive every day — and the exact stretches that appear on DVSA examiner routes.

Arterial A665

Cheetham Hill Road A665

The backbone of M8 driving. Delivery vehicles block bus stops and double yellows through the shopping zone throughout the day, forcing merges into live traffic. Lane narrows from three to two at Bury Old Road — lane choice must be committed 150 m before the filter.

Filter Junction

Bury Old Road

The 3-to-2 lane convergence where Cheetham Hill Road meets Bury Old Road is a critical decision point. A protected right-turn filter runs on a separate phase — you must read lane markings well in advance and hold your position through the narrowing.

Residential

Crumpsall Lane

Residential driving through established M8 streets with school zones near Crumpsall Nursery and St John’s CE Primary. Speed limit changes between 30 mph sections require constant sign awareness.

Estate Roads

Elizabeth Street

Estate driving environment with tight residential geometry, frequent parked vehicles on both sides, and priority decisions at unmarked junctions. Excellent for building low-speed observation and spatial awareness.

Bus Lanes

Queens Road

A key route with dedicated bus lanes and frequent bus stops pulling in and out of traffic. Learners practise identifying bus lane operating hours, giving way to buses signalling, and maintaining progress in the remaining lanes.

Narrow Road

Delauneys Road

Consistently parked vehicles reduce the effective road width to single-file in sections. Learners develop passing place judgement, appropriate use of priority, and hazard spotting at the residential pace that examiners specifically assess.

Motorway Prep

M60 J19 Approach

The Queens Road and A576 approach to M60 Junction 19 is used for Pass Plus motorway preparation. Practise lane joining, 70 mph cruise, mirror routines and exit planning from a Cheetham Hill starting point.

Arterial A664

Arterial approach from north Manchester into the city. Multi-lane sections, pelican crossings, and bus corridor management. Used on Route C examiner tests — understanding signal phasing here is a significant test-day advantage.

Pinch-Point Alert

The Hazard No Other School Tells You About

Cheetham Hill Road A665 — The Shopping Zone Pinch

Throughout the trading day, delivery vehicles park across bus stop zones and on double yellow lines along the Cheetham Hill shopping stretch of the A665. This forces learners to merge into live moving traffic at speed — a high-pressure manoeuvre that requires early mirror checks, a clear signal, and smooth acceleration to match the flow.

Immediately north, the road narrows from three lanes to two at the Bury Old Road junction. The lane-choice decision must be made 150 metres before the filter signal — far earlier than most learners expect. A wrong lane choice here creates a last-second weave that generates an immediate serious fault on test.

DriveSQ specifically trains this pinch-point. We approach it repeatedly in lessons until the read-ahead habit is automatic — something no competitor school has documented or prepared learners for at this location.
