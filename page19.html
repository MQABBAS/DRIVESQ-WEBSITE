Reddish Local Area Guide

Lessons around Reddish use real local roads including Crowther Street, Houldsworth Street and Broadstone Road, so by the time you're ready for your test you've already driven the streets you'll use every day after passing. Reddish Vale Country Park is home to Houldsworth Mill, which when it opened in 1865 was the largest cotton-spinning mill in the world, employing over 2,500 workers across 110,000 spindles.

We also plan around school-run traffic near Abingdon Primary School and Vale View Primary School, using quieter spots like Houldsworth Square for early manoeuvre practice before stepping up to busier sections of Crowther Street.

Test centre: most learners around Reddish test at Bredbury Driving Test Centre, Lingard Lane, Bredbury, Stockport SK6 2QT; mock tests are planned around the routes examiners actually use from there.
