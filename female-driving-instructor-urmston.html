Roads You'll Learn on in Urmston

Flixton Road

Flixton Road is the main east–west route through Urmston and Flixton, connecting the town centre to Carrington Lane and the A57. The road changes speed limit twice — 30 mph through the built-up section, dropping to 40 mph past the allotment area — and learners need to recognise these transitions without being prompted. The Flixton Road / Moorfield Road junction is a staggered crossroads that frequently features in test routes from Stretford.

Urmston Lane

Urmston Lane runs north from the town centre toward the River Irwell and the Trafford boundary. The road narrows as it approaches Higher Road, with parking on both sides reducing the usable carriageway to one car's width in places. Learners practise forward planning — identifying gaps ahead and slowing before they're forced to stop — rather than reacting at the last moment. The junction with Stretford Road at the northern end requires careful observation of fast-moving traffic.

Crofts Bank Road

Crofts Bank Road connects Urmston to Davyhulme, passing Urmston Grammar School where term-time hazards mirror conditions on test routes — parents stopping suddenly, children crossing mid-block and cyclists pulling out from school gates. The road also offers good practice for the controlled approach to a mini roundabout: the junction with Roseneath Road requires a proper signal-mirror-manoeuvre sequence that examiners mark carefully.

Davyhulme Road East

Davyhulme Road East runs along the southern edge of the M60 motorway embankment toward Trafford General Hospital. The right turn into the hospital access road — across a fast-flowing 40 mph road — is a useful advanced junction exercise. The road connects naturally to the A57 Chapel Road, which your instructor will include once your road positioning and speed are consistently correct.

Your Test Centre: Stretford DVSA

Urmston learners sit their practical test at Stretford DVSA on Lacy Street, Stretford M32 — approximately 8–10 minutes from Urmston town centre. Test routes regularly include Lostock Road, Seymour Grove, Edge Lane and stretches of the A56 Chester Road. Your instructor will cover every road on the typical route list before your test date.
